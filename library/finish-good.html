4.1 Transfer of Finished Goods from Blast Freezer to Cold Room.

1.Purpose

To establish a standardized process for the inspection, documentation, and storage of items transferred from the blast freezer to the cold room, ensuring compliance with defined quality, temperature, and labeling requirements, and ensuring that all ERP entries are accurate, complete, and verified to support traceability, data integrity, and regulatory compliance.

2.Scope

This procedure applies to all products transferred from the blast freezer to the cold room, including semi-processed goods, and finished products. It covers all activities related to inspection, storage, and ERP data entry, including verification process. This procedure is applicable to all relevant departments and personnel involved in handling, storage, and data management, including production, finished goods, inventory, and administrative functions.

3.Responsibility

Role

Responsibility

Finished Goods (FG) Unloading Supervisor

Responsible for conducting inspections of items transferred from the blast freezer, recording findings, and ensuring accurate and timely confirm entry item details into the ERP system.

Production Unloading Supervisor

Responsible for ensuring proper handling and timely transfer of items from the blast freezer to the cold room and ensuring accurate and timely entry of item details into the ERP system.

Cold Room Supervisor

Responsible for receiving items, verifying product category, and ensuring correct storage in designated areas in accordance with storage requirements.

Quality Assurance (QA) Supervisor

Responsible for verifying inspection results and ensuring compliance with quality, temperature, hygiene, and food safety standards during inspection and storage

FG Administrator

Responsible for reviewing and rechecking all ERP entries for accuracy and completeness.

FG Manager

Responsible for overseeing implementation of this procedure and ensuring overall compliance.

Managers (Relevant Departments)

Responsible for ensuring that verification processes are followed and for approving any corrected or updated ERP entries.

ERP Administrator

Responsible for system-level oversight, maintaining ERP functionality, and supporting error correction when required

6.Procedure

4.1 Receiving and Inspection of Items

4.1.1 Receive all items transferred from the blast freezer by the FG Unloading Supervisor.

4.1.2 Inspect each item for product condition, temperature compliance, labeling accuracy, weight, and secondary packaging integrity.

4.1.3 Immediately inform the Production Unloading Supervisor and return items for clarification and reprocessing if any non-conformity is identified (e.g., incorrect item code, damaged packaging, underweight bags, or other discrepancies).

4.1.4 Record item details (item name, item code, batch number, quantity, and relevant information) in the manual log and ERP system if items meet all requirements.

4.2 Storage of Items in Cold Room

4.2.1 Transfer accepted items to the designated cold room without delay.

4.2.2 Store portion items in Cool Room 01 and systematically store H/C and W/C items in Cool Room 02&03 according to defined storage criteria (e.g., weight/category).

4.2.3 Store all items in accordance with Stock Movement Guideline and company storage standards, ensuring proper placement, labeling, and temperature control.

4.3 ERP Data Entry

4.3.1 Ensure that all relevant item details are accurately entered into the ERP system without delay by the FG Unloading Supervisor.

4.3.2 Include item name, item code, batch number, quantity, and transfer details in the data entry.

4.4 Data Verification and Record Maintenance

4.4.1 Recheck all ERP entries by comparing them with manual inventory records and supporting documents.

4.4.2 Verify item name, quantity, and batch numbers to ensure consistency between manual records and the ERP system.

4.4.3 Maintain daily records of items transferred from Production to the FG cold room by the FG Admin Assistant.

4.4.4 Ensure proper filing and retention of daily production manual sheets and related documents by the FG Admin Assistant.

7.Safety & Compliance Requirements

  • Personal Protective Equipment (PPE): All personnel must wear the required PPE when handling items, including gloves, safety shoes, cool room jackets or thermal wear, aprons, and safety helmets as applicable.
  • Hygiene and Food Safety: Follow company hygiene, GMP, and food safety guidelines at all times during handling, inspection, storage, and transfer of items.
  • Cold Room Handling: Ensure doors are properly managed to prevent temperature fluctuations; verify that cold-room temperature is within required limits before storing items. Avoid over-stacking and keep aisles clear to prevent accidents.
  • Manual Handling: Use proper lifting and handling techniques to prevent injuries such as strains, slips, or frostbite when handling frozen or heavy items.
  • ERP and Data Compliance: Access to the ERP system is restricted to authorized personnel. Maintain data security, confidentiality, and compliance with IT and audit requirements.
  • Ergonomics: Ensure proper workstation setup and ergonomics to prevent strain during prolonged data entry or review activities.

8.Non-Conformance Handling

    • Identification and Recording: All non-conforming items—including damaged products, mislabelled items, temperature deviations, storage in incorrect categories, or documentation discrepancies—must be immediately recorded in the Non-Conformance Form.
    • Isolation: Non-conforming items must be isolated in the designated “Hold” area until reviewed by the QA Department.
    • Notification: QA, FG Manager, or relevant supervisory personnel must be notified immediately of all non-conformances.
    • Corrective Actions: Follow instructions from QA or management to correct the issue, which may include:
  • Moving items to the correct storage location
  • Correcting ERP entries and maintaining supporting records
  • Implementing process improvements, staff training, or system checks to prevent recurrence
  • Escalating major discrepancies using the company’s NCR procedure
    • Documentation: All corrective actions, data entry corrections, and related activities must be fully documented to maintain traceability and support audit compliance

4.2 Stored Items obtained from the blast freezer are stored in the cool room according to their batch-related item numbers

1.Purpose

To ensure that all finished goods are systematically sorted, organized, and issued according to defined Batch numbers and product types, enabling accurate identification, traceability, and inventory control, while ensuring efficient stock handling and adherence to FIFO principles based on Sales Admin requirements.

2.Scope

This procedure applies to all finished goods received, sorted, stored, categorizing, labeling, identification and issued within the cool room,items based on batch numbers and product types. This procedure is applicable to all personnel involved in handling, storage, recording requirements.

3.Responsibility

Role

Responsibility

FG Staff

Responsible for accurately sorting and arranging finished goods according to Batch numbers and product types

FG Unloading Supervisor

Responsible for organizing and arranging finished goods, ensuring correct identification and separation of items, including old and new batch numbers.

Cold Room Supervisor

Responsible for sorting and storage activities, ensuring proper organization, and issuing items as per approved requests while following Stock Movement Guidelines.

FG Supervisors

Responsible for ensuring overall compliance with this procedure during sorting, storage activities.

FG Manager

Responsible for monitoring implementation of this procedure, verifying compliance, and addressing operational gaps, discrepancies, or issues.

4.Definitions

Term

Definitions

Batch Number

A specific code assigned to a product for identification and classification

Goods Type

The classification of items based on characteristics such as finished goods, Semi Processed Goods

Sorting

The process of organizing items according to predefined Batch numbers and goods types

Old Batch Number

The batch number assigned to items produced or received earlier, used to identify older stock for proper rotation

New Batch Number

The batch number assigned to recently produced or received items, used to distinguish newer stock from existing inventory

FIFO (First-In First-Out)

A stock rotation method where the oldest items in inventory are issued first to ensure proper stock rotation and minimize product aging

5.Required Records & References

  • Batch Lists
  • Goods Receiving Notes / Transfer Documents
  • Cool room Layout Charts
  • ERP System Records
  • Special Approved Item Request Forms
  • Stock Movement Guidelines and Relevant SOPs

6.Procedure

4.2.1 Receiving and Initial Handling

4.2.1.1 Collect required pallets from designated storage areas prior to handling finished goods.

4.2.1.2 Transfer items daily from the blast freezer to the cold room, ensuring proper handling and clear identification.

4.2.1.3 Place and stack all items securely on pallets in a stable and organized manner to prevent damage and ensure safe handling.

4.2.2 Sorting and Organization

4.2.2.1 Sort all received items according to batch numbers and product types.

4.2.2.2 Clearly identify and arrange items according to the annexe FGD04/GUD/24/2026 Stock Movement Guideline to distinguish between old and new batch numbers.

4.2.2.3 Ensure proper labeling and placement to support easy identification, traceability, and retrieval.

4.2.3 Storage

4.2.3.1 Store items in designated locations according to batch and cool room layout chart.

4.2.3.2 Always issue items following FIFO principles, ensuring that older batch numbers are issued before newer ones. Annexe FGD04/GUD/24/2026 Stock Movement Guideline

7.Safety & Compliance Requirements

  • Personal Protective Equipment (PPE): All personnel must wear appropriate PPE, including gloves, Cool room Jacket Mask, safety footwear, Hair Cap when handling items.
  • Material Handling: Follow proper lifting techniques and safe material-handling practices to prevent injuries and product damage during sorting, storage, and issuing activities. annexe FGD04/GUD/30/2026 Safety Guideline
  • Workplace Safety: Ensure all pathways and storage areas are clean, organized, and free from obstructions to prevent slips, trips, and accidents. annexe FGD04/GUD/30/2026 Safety Guideline
  • Product Protection: Handle all items carefully to avoid damage and maintain product quality during movement and storage.
  • Hygiene and GMP Compliance: Adhere to company hygiene standards, Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP), and Cool room safety procedures at all times. annexe FGD04/GUD/30/2026 Safety guideline
  • Labeling and Traceability Compliance: Ensure all items are properly labeled and documented in accordance with company Inventory Batch Report and traceability requirements.
  • Regulatory and Audit Compliance: Comply with all company policies, internal audit requirements, and applicable operational standards.

8.Non-Conformance Handling

  • Identification and Reporting: Any non-conformance, including incorrectly sorted items, misplacement, incorrect categorization, mislabeling, mixed items, or deviations from FIFO and incorrect issuance, must be identified and reported immediately to the Supervisor & QA and Production Department through Finish Good Manager.
  • Correction and Reorganization: Non-conforming items shall be promptly re-sorted, re-categorized, or relocated to the correct storage location. Any corrections made must be properly recorded.
  • Recording: All non-conformances shall be documented in the relevant Non Conformance Form, including details of the issue and corrective actions taken.
  • Investigation and Corrective Action: The root cause of the non-conformance shall be investigated, and corrective actions such as staff retraining, process improvements, or procedure updates shall be implemented to prevent recurrence according to the annexure QA11 Non Conformance Procedure.

4.3 Coordinating with the Sales Admin Department to ensure the timely release of finished products.

1.Purpose

To ensure the accurate, timely, and controlled processing of sales orders, enabling efficient issuance of finished goods and proper coordination for the handling and verification of issued items, in accordance with Sales Admin requirements and company procedures.

2.Scope

This procedure applies to all personnel involved in receiving, verifying, and processing sales orders for the issuance of finished goods, as well as the handling and processing of issued items. It includes FG Department staff responsible for issuing goods, Sales Administration personnel responsible for submitting and authorizing requests, and all relevant staff involved in documentation, verification, and stock adjustments related to order processing and returns.

3.Responsibility

Role

Responsibility

Sales Administration Department

Responsible for submitting accurate, complete, and authorized requests for finished goods release, and for notifying and providing supporting documentation for any issued items and Responsible for coordinating and approving the order receiving process.

Finished Goods Administration & Loading Officers

Responsible for receiving and verifying orders, accurately issuing finished goods as per approved requests, and maintaining accurate stock records for issued goods.

FG Cold Room Supervisor

Responsible for verifying orders, arranging, and releasing finished goods in accordance with approved instructions

FG Managers / All Supervisors

Responsible for monitoring compliance with this procedure, ensuring proper implementation, and resolving any discrepancies or operational issues related to order processing

4.Definitions

Term

Definitions

Finished Goods (FG)

Products that have completed all stages of production and are ready for storage, dispatch, or distribution

Sales Order

A formal, authorized document or system-generated request issued by the Sales Administration Department for the release of finished goods.

Sales Administration Officer

Personnel responsible for preparing, coordinating, and approving sales-related documentation, including sales orders for finished goods issuance.

Issuing (of Goods)

The process of releasing finished goods from the warehouse or storage area in accordance with an approved sales order

Authorization

The formal approval of a sales order or request by designated personnel, confirming that the request is valid and permitted for processing

FIFO (First-In First-Out)

A stock rotation method where the oldest available stock is issued first to ensure proper inventory management and minimize product aging

5.Required Records & References

  • System-Generated Orders (Authorized)
  • Goods Issue Notes
  • FG Stock Records
  • ERP System Entries
  • Dispatch Records

6.Procedure

4.3.1 Order Receipt and Authorization

4.3.1.1 Receive sales orders from the Sales Administration Department for the release of finished goods.

4.3.1.2 Submit all requests to the FG Department Head (HOD) for review and approval.

4.3.1.3 Accept orders for finished goods only up to 8:00 PM, except on Sundays and Tuesdays, where accept orders only up to 7:00 PM.

4.3.1.4 Process orders received after the specified cut-off times only with the approval of the FG Department Head.

4.3.2 Order Verification and Stock Availability

4.3.2.1 Verify available stock in the cool rooms prior to processing orders by the FG Admin Assistant.

• If items are available → proceed with issuing.
• If items are not available → inform the Sales Administration Department immediately.

4.3.2.2 Communicate available stock details to the Sales Administration Department where required by the FG Admin Assistant.

4.3.3 Order Approval and Changes

4.3.3.1 Approve all orders, including additional or urgent requests, by the FG Department Head before processing.

4.3.3.2 Reject orders and inform the Sales Department if the FG Department Head does not approve the request.

4.3.3.3 Communicate any urgent changes or additional requests through the Sales Department by phone call & message and follow the same approval process.

4.3.4 Issuing of Finished Goods

4.3.4.1 Process approved orders by issuing finished goods from the FG Department.

4.3.4.2 Issue items accurately in accordance with the approved order and FIFO principles.

4.3.4.3 Record all issued items in the Loading Sheet and ERP system.

4.3.5 Dispatch and Confirmation

4.3.5.1 Load finished goods into the designated freezer vehicle in a safe and organized manner.

4.3.5.2 Notify the Sales Department upon completion of loading.

7.Safety & Compliance Requirements

  • Safe Handling: All personnel must follow proper manual handling, lifting, and loading procedures when issuing finished goods to prevent injuries and product damage.
  • Product Protection: Finished goods must be handled carefully during picking, staging, and loading to maintain product quality and prevent damage.
  • Storage Area Control: Cleanliness, orderliness, and safe working conditions must be maintained in FG storage and loading areas at all times.
  • Compliance Requirements: All issuance activities must comply with company policies, quality standards, stock control procedures, and internal audit requirements.

8.Non-Conformance Handling

  • Any discrepancy, including incorrect items, incorrect quantities, missing documents, mismatched stock details, or unauthorized requests, must be reported immediately to the FG Supervisor and FG Manager.
  • All non-conformances are documented in the designated Non Conformance Form.
  • The issue reviewed and investigated to identify the root cause according to the non conformance procedure.
  • Where necessary, communication with the Sales Administration Department and relevant internal personnel are carried out to resolve the discrepancy and update records accordingly.

4.4 The Finished Goods Department accepts orders sent by the Sales Department.

1.Purpose

To ensure that all orders received from the Sales Department are reviewed, accepted, and processed in a timely manner, and that the Finished Goods Department prepares the required resources and workforce in advance to support smooth, efficient, and on-time dispatch operations in accordance with customer delivery requirements.

2.Scope

This SOP applies to all sales orders received and processed by the Finished Goods Department for dispatch to representatives and distributor customers. It covers order review, acceptance, dispatch planning, manpower preparation, and coordination of goods for delivery. This procedure is applicable to all relevant FG personnel involved in dispatch operations, including FG Admin Officer, FG Supervisors, labor staff, drivers, loading officers, loading supervisors, cold room supervisors, and security officers.

3.Responsibility

Role

Responsibility

Sales Department

Responsible for providing complete, accurate, and timely order details for dispatch planning

FG Manager

Responsible for ensuring timely order acceptance, overseeing dispatch and manpower planning, and resolving escalation issues.

Loading Officer

Responsible for preparing the loading schedule and coordinating manpower allocation based on dispatch requirements

Loading Supervisor

Responsible for reviewing, confirming, and monitoring the loading schedule, and assessing manpower requirements based on order volume.

Cold Room Supervisor

Responsible for supporting manpower assessment and coordinating operational readiness of goods for dispatch

HR Department

Responsible for arranging additional manpower when required to support dispatch operations

4.Definitions

Term

Definitions

Sales Order (SO)

A formal request issued by the Sales Department specifying the item, quantity, delivery date, and customer details required for dispatch.

Acceptance of Order

Confirmation by the Finished Goods (FG) Department that an order can be fulfilled based on stock availability, operational readiness, and delivery requirements.

Lead Time

The period between receipt of an order and its planned fulfillment or dispatch.

Workforce Preparation

The process of allocating and organizing the necessary personnel to handle, move, and load goods for dispatch.

Dispatch Workforce

Personnel involved in preparing, handling, moving, and loading finished goods for delivery

Manpower Requirement Assessment

The evaluation of the number of personnel needed based on order volume, item type, delivery schedule, and operational workload.

5.Required Records & References

  • Sales Order Sheet (Manual or ERP-Generated)
  • Stock Availability Report
  • Loading Schedule / Daily Loading Schedule
  • Duty Roster
  • Workforce Attendance Records
  • Sales-to-FG Communication SOP

6.Procedure

4.4.1 Receipt and Review of Sales Orders

4.4.1.1 Receive Sales Orders by the Finished Goods Department from the Sales Department through the ERP system or approved communication Through Phone.

4.4.1.2 Ensure that each order contains complete and accurate details, including item description, quantity, delivery date, customer information, and any special dispatch requirements by the Sales Department Representative.

4.4.1.3 Review received Sales Orders for completeness, accuracy, and feasibility of fulfillment by the FG Department.

4.4.1.4 Check stock availability and operational capacity before accepting orders for dispatch processing.

4.4.1.5 Immediately communicate with the Sales Department for clarification or revision if any discrepancy, incomplete information, or stock limitation is identified.

4.4.1.6 Accept and schedule orders for dispatch if all requirements are met.

4.4.1.7 Refer any order requiring escalation or special approval to the FG Manager for decision and further action.

4.4.2 Dispatch Planning and Loading Schedule Preparation

4.4.2.1 Prepare the loading schedule based on approved Sales Orders, delivery timelines, item volume, and operational priorities by the Loading Officer after order acceptance. Annexe FGD04/FMT/10/2026 Loading Schedule Format

4.4.2.2 Include required dispatch time, order sequence, expected loading time, and order quantities in the loading schedule.

4.4.2.3 Review and confirm the loading schedule to ensure practicality, completeness, and alignment with operational capacity by the Loading Supervisor.

4.4.2.4 Communicate any required changes to the loading schedule to Loading Supervisor without delay.

4.4.3 Manpower Requirement Assessment

4.4.3.1 Assess manpower requirements based on order volume, type of goods, loading complexity, and dispatch deadlines by the Loading Supervisor and Cold Room Supervisor.

4.4.3.2 Consider the number of laborers, drivers, loading officers, and other supporting personnel required for smooth dispatch operations in the manpower assessment.

4.4.3.3 Communicate insufficient manpower requirements to the Loading Officer and FG Manager for necessary action.

4.4.4 Workforce Preparation and Allocation

4.4.4.1 Coordinate allocation of available manpower in accordance with the confirmed loading schedule and operational requirements by the Loading Officer.

4.4.4.2 Clearly communicate duty assignments to relevant personnel before dispatch operations begin.

4.4.4.3 Inform through FG Manager to the HR Department to arrange additional manpower where required in line with workforce allocation.

4.4.4.4 Oversee adequacy and effectiveness of workforce planning to ensure dispatch readiness by the FG Manager.

4.4.5 Final Readiness for Dispatch Operations

4.4.5.1 Confirm that required workforce, loading schedule, and operational arrangements are in place prior to dispatch by Cool room & Loading supervisors.

4.4.5.2 Escalate any issues affecting dispatch readiness immediately to the FG Manager for resolution.

4.4.5.3 Proceed with dispatch operations in accordance with the approved schedule and relevant dispatch procedures once readiness is confirmed.

7.Safety & Compliance Requirements

  • Product Quality: Ensure that order fulfillment and dispatch operations do not compromise product quality. Only products that meet QA release standards selected for dispatch.
  • Stock Handling: Maintain proper stock rotation practices such as FIFO/FEFO when picking and allocating items for dispatch.
  • Personal Protective Equipment (PPE): All personnel involved in handling, moving, or loading goods must wear the required PPE, including gloves, safety boots, and cool room jackets.
  • Workforce Competency: Only trained staff operate the ERP system or handle specialized equipment related to dispatch.
  • Safe Loading Practices: Follow proper lifting, moving, and loading procedures to prevent injuries and product damage. Adhere to weight-lift limitations and use equipment safely.
  • Pre-Operation Briefing: Workers must be briefed on safety requirements, particularly before large, high-volume, or high-risk loading operations.

8.Non-Conformance Handling

  • Order-Related Issues:
    • If an order cannot be accepted or processed on time due to insufficient stock, incomplete or incorrect order details, pending QA release, overlapping dispatch schedules, or system discrepancies, the FG Department must:
      • Inform the Sales Department immediately and provide reasons for delay or rejection.
      • Request revised order details or a new dispatch timeline.
      • Escalate to the FG Manager if delays affect customer commitments.
  • Workforce-Related Issues:
    • If manpower preparation cannot be completed due to insufficient staff, absenteeism, untrained workers, equipment unavailability, sudden increase in order volume, or miscommunication of required goods, the FG Department must:
      • Inform the FG Manager and HR immediately.
      • Request additional manpower or reassign available staff to cover the gap.
      • Adjust the dispatch schedule if necessary to maintain operational safety and order fulfillment.
      • Record the issue in the Daily Loading Log. annexure FGD04/BK/29/2026
      • Conduct a quick safety briefing before resuming operations to prevent accidents or errors.

4.5 Preparing the order issued by the Sales Department to the Finished Goods Department

1.Purpose

To ensure that an accurate, complete, and timely loading schedule and item list are prepared for each sales order, in alignment with dispatch plans and customer delivery requirements, to support efficient picking, verification, and dispatch of finished goods from cold storage.

2.Scope

This SOP applies to the preparation of loading schedules and item lists for all dispatch operations involving finished goods stored in cold storage, including chilled & frozen items. It covers sales orders for representatives and distributor deliveries, and is applicable to all relevant personnel involved in planning, picking, and loading activities, including Loading Officers, Loading Supervisors, Loading Teams, Cold Room Supervisors, and Cold Room Laborers.

3.Responsibility

Role

Responsibility

Loading Supervisor

Sales Department

Responsible for providing confirmed order details and required delivery timelines

Sales Transport Coordinator

Responsible for confirming vehicle availability.

Cold room Supervisor

Responsible for verifying stock availability, coordinating with the picking team, and ensuring correct items are prepared for dispatch.

Cool Room Labourers

Responsible for picking and handling the required items from cold storage in accordance with the approved item list and instructions from the supervisor.

FG Admin Officer

Responsible for confirming system quantities, batch availability, and stock accuracy before loading preparation is finalized.

6.Procedure

4.5.1 Receipt and Review of Dispatch Requirements

4.5.1.1 Obtain the confirmed Sales Order list and dispatch requirements for the day from the FG Manager & Sales Department by the Loading Officer.

4.5.1.2 Review order details, delivery priorities, dispatch timelines, and customer requirements before preparing the loading schedule by the Loading Officer.

4.5.1.3 Confirm vehicle availability and loading time slots with the Sales Transport Coordinator prior to finalizing the loading plan.

4.5.2 Preparation of Loading Schedule

4.5.2.1 Prepare the Daily Loading Schedule based on confirmed sales orders, dispatch priorities, vehicle allocation, and available loading time slots by the Loading Officer.

4.5.2.2 Include order reference, distribution category, vehicle details, loading sequence, planned loading time, and dispatch priority in the loading schedule, as applicable.

4.5.2.3 Submit the prepared loading schedule to the Loading Supervisor for review and approval.

4.5.2.4 Communicate the approved loading schedule to relevant operational personnel involved in picking and loading activities.

4.5.3 Verification of Stock Availability

4.5.3.1 Verify the stock report, including item codes, quantities, and batch availability, against relevant Sales Orders by the Cool room Supervisor.

4.5.3.2 Confirm the physical availability of required items in cold storage by the Cold room Supervisor.

4.5.3.3 Report any discrepancy between system stock and physical stock immediately to the Loading & Cool room Supervisors for clarification before proceeding.

4.5.4 Preparation of Order Collection List

4.5.4.1 Prepare an Order Collection List for each specific order based on confirmed Sales Orders and stock verification.

4.5.4.2 Include required item name, item code, quantity, batch details where applicable, and identification details needed for picking in the Order Collection List.

4.5.4.3 Check the list for completeness and accuracy before issuing it to the Cold Storage Supervisor or designated picking personnel.

4.5.5 Coordination of Picking Preparation

4.5.5.1 Brief the Cool Room Labourer picking team on the approved Order Collection List and required dispatch priorities by the Cold Storage Supervisor.

4.5.5.2 Collect items from cold storage in accordance with the approved list and handling requirements by the picking team.

4.5.5.3 Organize items prepared for dispatch in a manner that supports efficient loading according to the approved schedule.

4.5.6 Final Review and Readiness for Loading

4.5.6.1 Verify that the loading schedule, stock confirmation, and Order Collection Lists are complete and aligned before loading begins by the Loading Supervisor.

4.5.6.2 Communicate any issue affecting loading readiness, including stock shortages, batch discrepancies, or scheduling conflicts, immediately to the Loading & Cool room Supervisors for corrective action.

4.5.6.3 Release prepared items and loading schedule for loading and dispatch operations in accordance with the 4.10 Finished Goods Loading Operations Procedure.

4.8 Finished Goods Weighing Process

1.Purpose

2.Scope

This procedure applies to all freezer trucks undergoing weighing and loading operations prior to final dispatch. It covers first weight (pre-loading), loading bay weighing activities, and final weight verification of finished goods packed in bags. The scope includes weighbridge operations (company or approved external weighbridge), FG dispatch activities, transport processes, and loading bay operations. It is applicable to all relevant personnel involved, including Security Officers, Loading Staff, and FG Supervisors.

Definitions

6.Procedure

4.8.1 First Weighing of Freezer Truck Prior to Loading

4.8.1.1 Present the freezer truck at the weighbridge in accordance with instructions given by the Loading Supervisor or other authorized responsible person by the driver.

4.8.1.2 Ensure that the weighbridge is clean, calibrated, in proper working condition, and displaying zero (“0”) prior to commencement of weighing by the Weighbridge Operator.

4.8.1.3 Verify that the freezer truck is empty, clean, and free from any loose, foreign, or unauthorized materials prior to recording the first weight by the Weighbridge Operator or designated responsible person.

4.8.1.4 Properly position and keep the freezer truck stationary on the weighbridge before capturing the weight.

4.8.1.5 Record relevant vehicle details, including truck number and driver name, in the applicable system or register by the Weighbridge Operator.

4.8.1.6 Capture and record the first pre-loading weight using the approved weighing system.

4.8.1.7 Generate and issue the first weighbridge slip upon completion of the weighing process by the Weighbridge Operator.

4.8.2 Weighing of Product at the Loading Bay During Loading Operations

4.8.2.1 Transfer the freezer truck to the designated loading bay under the supervision of the Loading Supervisor after completion of the first weighing.

4.8.2.2 Ensure that the loading bay scale is calibrated, functional, and suitable for the required weighing operation prior to commencement of loading by the Loading Supervisor or other authorized responsible person.

4.8.2.3 Weigh each bag individually using the loading bay scale during loading operations by Security Officers.

4.8.2.4 Accurately record the net weight of each bag in the manual loading weight sheet.

4.8.2.5 Deduct the standard bag tare weight to ensure accurate net weight calculation.

4.8.2.6 Verify all recorded bag weights for compliance with applicable product specifications and standards.

4.8.2.7 Enter all relevant loading details into the applicable system, including dealer or distributor name, truck number, item name, batch number, and bag-wise weights by the FG Officer.

4.8.2.8 Ensure proper handling of bags and maintain a clean and safe working area throughout the loading process by all personnel involved in loading operations.

4.8.3 Second Weighing of Freezer Truck After Loading

4.8.3.1 Direct the freezer truck to the weighbridge for final weighing upon completion of loading.

4.8.3.2 Ensure that the weighbridge is in proper working condition and displaying zero (“0”) prior to the second weighing by the Weighbridge Operator.

4.8.3.3 Properly align and keep the freezer truck stationary on the weighbridge before recording the final weight.

4.8.3.4 Capture and record the second post-loading weight using the approved weighbridge.

4.8.3.5 Generate and issue the final weighbridge slip upon completion of the second weighing by the Weighbridge Operator.

4.8.3.6 Compare the final weight against the GIN weight and the total bag weight recorded during loading.

4.8.3.7 Verify the difference between the final weighbridge weight and the recorded loading weight to ensure it remains within the allowable variance of ±5 kg.

7.Safety & Compliance Requirements

  • Ensure freezer trucks are clean, empty, and free from loose or unnecessary materials before weighing.
  • Confirm the scale displays zero (“0”) before positioning the vehicle on the weighbridge.
  • Ensure the freezer truck is properly aligned and stationary during weighing.
  • Allow only one vehicle on the weighbridge at any given time.
  • Ensure only authorized personnel operate the weighing system.
  • Verify that all weighing equipment (weighbridge and loading bay scales) is calibrated, functional, and within the valid calibration period.
  • Ensure loading bay scales meet the minimum allowable weight requirement of 0.1 kg.
  • Ensure all recorded weights comply with declared product specifications and approved standards.
  • Maintain clean and dry working areas in loading bays to prevent slips and ensure safe handling.
  • Use proper manual handling techniques when weighing and moving bags.
  • Ensure drivers follow all weighbridge instructions and safety instructions.

8.Non- Conformance Handling

  • If the scale does not display zero (“0”) or shows unstable/incorrect readings, stop the weighing process immediately and inform Maintenance.
  • If incorrect or incomplete data/records are identified, they must be corrected promptly and properly verified or countersigned before finalization.
  • If recorded weights fall outside specified limits or do not comply with product standards, immediately inform the FG Supervisor/FG Manager for further action.
  • If the weighbridge or loading bay scale is not operational, escalate to the FG Manager and follow approved alternative weighing procedures using a company-approved weighbridge, ensuring all relevant slips are attached to documentation.
  • If the final recorded weight differs by more than the allowable variance (±5 kg) from the GIN weight and bag weight, inform the FG HOD immediately.
  • In case of weight discrepancies, the freezer truck must be returned to the loading bay for inspection and corrective action.
  • Re-weighing should carried out after resolving the issue, and all actions must be properly documented.

6.Procedure

4.10.1.1 Verification of Dispatch Information

4.10.1.1.1 Obtain and verify all primary dispatch information, including Rep/Dealer name, vehicle number, driver details, and route by the Loading Officer.

4.10.1.1.2 Check all details against the approved Sales Order and dispatch documents for accuracy.

4.10.1.1.3 Allow only authorized vehicles and personnel to proceed for loading.

4.10.1.2 Vehicle Positioning and Preparation

4.10.1.2.1 Direct the vehicle to the designated loading bay.

4.10.1.2.2 Ensure that the vehicle, including freezer trucks, is in suitable condition for loading by the Loading Supervisor.

4.10.1.3 System Entry and Documentation Setup

4.10.1.3.1 Enter dispatch details into the ERP system, including item name, quantity, weight, and batch number by the Loading Officers.

4.10.1.3.2 Prepare and maintain the Manual Loading Sheet for independent recording of loading details by the Security Officers.

4.10.1.4 Weight Tag Verification

4.10.1.4.1 Check each bag to ensure it has a valid issued weight tag with complete and legible information.

4.10.1.4.2 Reject and correct any bag without proper identification before loading proceeds.

4.10.1.5 Loading of Finished Goods

4.10.1.5.1 Load finished goods in accordance with the verified dispatch plan.

4.10.1.5.2 Follow proper stacking guidelines:
• Place heavy items at the front.
• Place medium-weight items in the middle.
• Place portion items at the rear.

4.10.1.5.3 Place sensitive items on top to prevent damage.

4.10.1.5.4 Follow safe lifting and handling practices at all times during loading operations.

4.10.1.6 Real-Time Recording During Loading

4.10.1.6.1 Record all loading details simultaneously in both the ERP system and the Manual Loading Sheet.

4.10.1.6.2 Ensure accuracy for item names, quantities, weights, batch numbers, loading time, date, and bay number.

4.10.1.7 Weighing Process (if applicable)

4.10.1.7.1 Weigh the loaded vehicle and obtain the weighbridge slip (second weight).

4.10.1.7.2 Verify recorded weight to ensure alignment with loaded quantities.

4.10.1.8 Reconciliation of Records

4.10.1.8.1 Compare ERP records with the Manual Loading Sheet after completion of loading.

4.10.1.8.2 Ensure all details match exactly.

4.10.1.8.3 Resolve any discrepancies prior to dispatch.

4.10.1.9 Sealing and Securing the Vehicle

4.10.1.9.1 Properly close and lock vehicle doors after loading.

4.10.1.9.2 Apply seal tags to freezer trucks or other required vehicles.

4.10.1.9.3 Record seal tag details in the Seal Tag Record Book.

4.10.1.10 Final Verification and Dispatch Release

4.10.1.10.1 Conduct a final check on documentation, records, and physical loading condition.

4.10.1.10.2 Obtain necessary approvals from the FG Loading Supervisor.

4.10.1.10.3 Release the vehicle for dispatch only after completion of all verification and approval steps.

7.Safety & Compliance Requirements

  • All primary dispatch information must be verified against approved sales orders and transport documents before loading begins. Unauthorized vehicles or unverified personnel must not be permitted for loading.
  • Accurate and real-time recording must be maintained throughout the loading process. Loading Officers must enter all loading details into the ERP system, while Security Officers must independently record the same in the Manual Loading Sheet. All entries must be clear, complete, and legible.
  • Each bag must have a unique issued weight tag attached prior to loading. Tags must clearly display all required information and remain secure and readable throughout loading and dispatch. Bags without proper identification are not permitted.
  • Proper stacking and handling practices must be followed to prevent product damage and ensure safety. Heavy, medium, and portion items must stacked in designated positions unless otherwise specified. Sensitive items must be placed on top, and safe lifting techniques must be practiced at all times.
  • Reconciliation between manual records and ERP records must be completed after loading. All details, including item names, quantities, weights, and batch numbers, must match before dispatch approval.
  • Freezer trucks must be securely closed, locked, and sealed immediately after loading. Seal tags must applied and recorded in the Seal Tag Record Book. Only authorized personnel are permitted to handle sealing activities.
  • Clear communication must be maintained among all personnel involved to ensure safe, accurate, and compliant operations.

8.Non-Conformance Handling

  • If any mismatch, discrepancy, or missing information is identified at any stage, loading or dispatch operations must immediately halted.
  • All non-conformances must be promptly reported to the Finished Goods (FG) Loading Supervisor and relevant departments (e.g., Sales Administration, Security, or QAD) for investigation and clarification.
  • Discrepancies between ERP records and manual records must be investigated, corrected, and properly documented before proceeding.
  • Bags without issued weight tags or with incomplete or illegible information must rejected. Re-tagging and verification must be completed prior to loading.
  • Any improper stacking, handling issues, or packaging damage must be corrected immediately. Re-stacking or corrective actions must be completed before dispatch approval.
  • In case of quantity or reconciliation errors, the data must be recalculated, and the root cause identified and corrected before continuation.
  • If seal tags are missing, damaged, or incorrectly recorded, dispatch must held. New seal tags must be applied and records updated accordingly.
  • Loading or dispatch may resume only after all non-conformances are resolved, corrective actions are implemented, and approval is obtained from the FG Loading Supervisor.
  • Above non conformance should be documented in Daily FG Log

4.11 Sales and Shop Return Handling Procedure

1.Purpose

To ensure proper verification, authorization, weighing, documentation, identification, and controlled handling of returned finished goods, including their segregation, storage, and formal transfer to the Quality Assurance (QA) Department, in order to maintain inventory accuracy, traceability, and compliance with management requirements.

2.Scope

This procedure applies to all finished goods returned through sales operations and shop return items received, recorded, and handled by the Finished Goods (FG) Department.

3.Responsibility

Role

Responsibility

Loading Officer:

Responsible for receiving returned finished goods, verifying returned quantities against provided documents, assisting in unloading, ensuring proper identification of returned items, and supporting accurate recording during the return process.

Security Officer

Responsible for verifying vehicle entry and return authorization, recording return details in the manual return records, monitoring unloading activities, and ensuring only authorized returns are accepted into the Finished Goods (FG) area.

Finished Goods (FG) Supervisor

Responsible for supervising all return handling activities, verifying return quantities and documentation, ensuring proper segregation and temporary storage of returned items, resolving discrepancies, and authorizing further processing.

Sales Representative

Responsible for initiating return requests, providing accurate return details and supporting documents, coordinating returned goods from customers, and ensuring proper communication with the FG Department.

FG Admin (Return)

Responsible for recording return details in the ERP system, maintaining return records, preparing return documentation, updating inventory records, and coordinating the transfer of returned goods to the Quality Assurance Department.

Quality Assurance (QA) Department

Responsible for inspecting returned finished goods, evaluating product condition, determining product disposition (such as acceptance, rejection, rework, or disposal), and documenting inspection and decision records.

Managing Director (MD)

Responsible for reviewing and approving return-related decisions that exceed authorized limits, including high-value returns, disposals, or exceptional cases requiring management approval.

Definitions

Finished goods returned from sales representatives or market delivery routes.

Finished goods returned directly from retail shops or dealers

An official document attached to returned goods to ensure identification and traceability during the return process.

Shop Return Form

An authorized document used to support and record the return of goods from shops or dealers.

ERP Shop Return Bill:

A system-generated return document created in the ERP system to validate and record return transactions.

MD Approval

Written or authorized approval from the Managing Director required for returns exceeding the specified approval limit (e.g., 10 kg).

Quality Assurance (QA) Department:

The department responsible for inspecting returned goods and determining their final status.

6.Procedure

4.11.1 Handling and Processing of Returned Finished Goods

4.11.1.1 Return Authorization and Verification

4.11.1.1.1 Verify all returned finished goods to ensure proper return documentation, such as Return Note or Shop Return Form, is provided.

4.11.1.1.2 Confirm authorization details, including Managing Director (MD) approval for shop returns exceeding the specified limit (e.g., 10 kg).

4.11.1.1.3 Allow only approved return items to proceed to the receiving stage.

4.11.1.2 Receiving of Returned Goods

4.11.1.2.1 Direct return vehicles or items to the designated return receiving area.

4.11.1.2.2 Record return details in the manual return recording sheet by the Security Officer.

4.11.1.2.3 Verify returned quantities and item details against provided documents by the Loading Officer.

4.11.1.3 Weighing of Shop Returns (If Applicable)

4.11.1.3.1 Weigh shop return items using the approved weighing scale with required accuracy.

4.11.1.3.2 Verify recorded weight against the Shop Return Form and ERP Shop Return Bill.

4.11.1.3.3 Report any discrepancies immediately to the Finished Goods (FG) Supervisor.

4.11.1.4 ERP Recording of Returns

4.11.1.4.1 Enter all sales return details into the ERP system according to standard return processing procedures by the Loading Officer.

4.11.1.4.2 Record shop returns in the ERP system later by the FG Admin as per established procedures.

4.11.1.4.3 Ensure all ERP entries are accurate, complete, and properly maintained.

4.11.1.5 Identification and Segregation of Returns

4.11.1.5.1 Attach a Return Note to each returned batch or item to ensure proper identification and traceability.

4.11.1.5.2 Place returned goods in the designated return area.

4.11.1.5.3 Store sales returns and shop returns separately as required.

4.11.1.6 Storage of Returned Goods

4.11.1.6.1 Maintain returned goods under proper storage conditions.

4.11.1.6.2 Store temperature-sensitive items in designated refrigerated storage areas.

4.11.1.6.3 Ensure returned goods remain clearly labeled and accessible for inspection at all times.

4.11.1.7 Transfer of Returns to Quality Assurance (QA)

4.11.1.7.1 Verify that all returned goods are properly recorded and documented prior to transfer.

4.11.1.7.2 Prepare Return Transfer Notes where applicable.

4.11.1.7.3 Physically hand over returned goods to the QA Department.

4.11.1.7.4 Acknowledge receipt of returned goods by QA personnel.

4.11.1.8 Final Documentation and Record Maintenance

4.11.1.8.1 Complete and properly file all return records, including ERP entries and manual records.

4.11.1.8.2 Maintain traceability of returned goods through proper documentation.

4.11.1.8.3 Report any unresolved discrepancies to the FG Supervisor for further action.

7.Safety & Compliance Requirements

  • All sales and shop returns must be supported with authorized return documents and verified before acceptance into the Finished Goods (FG) Department.
  • All sales return details must be accurately entered into the ERP system by the Loading Officer, while the Security Officer mustmaintain independent manual records for verification. All entries must be clear, complete, and accurate.
  • Shop returns must be accompanied by an approved Shop Return Form and ERP Shop Return Bill. Shop return items must weighed using an approved weighing scale with the required accuracy.
  • Shop returns exceeding the authorized limit (e.g., 10 kg) must receive prior approval from the Managing Director (MD) before processing.
  • All returned goods must be clearly identified with return notes and placed in designated return storage areas. Sales returns and shop returns must stored separately as required.
  • Returned goods must be maintained under appropriate storage conditions, including temperature-controlled storage for temperature-sensitive products.
  • All returns must be properly recorded and documented before transfer to the Quality Assurance (QA) Department. Physical handover of returned goods must be acknowledged and recorded by authorized QA personnel.
  • ERP entries for shop returns must completed by the FG Admin according to established return processing procedures.

8.Non-Conformance Handling

  • Returns without proper documentation or required authorization mustnot be accepted into the return area.
  • Shop returns exceeding the authorized limit (e.g., 10 kg) without Managing Director (MD) approval must rejected until proper approval is obtained.
  • Any mismatch between physical returned goods and system or manual records must be reported immediately to the Finished Goods (FG) Supervisor and corrected before storage or further processing.
  • Incorrectly identified or labeled returns must be re-verified, corrected, and properly documented before placement in the designated return area.
  • Any discrepancies in weight, quantity, or documentation must be investigated and resolved before acceptance or transfer.
  • Improper storage conditions, including incorrect segregation or delayed ERP entry, must be corrected immediately and documented.
  • Returns that are not properly recorded or documented mustnot be transferred to the Quality Assurance (QA) Department.
  • If returned goods are rejected by QA due to documentation errors, storage issues, or quantity mismatches, corrective actions must be completed before resubmission.
  • All non-conformances must be resolved and approved by the FG Supervisor before proceeding to the next stage.
