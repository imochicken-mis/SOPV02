8.1. Live Birds Receiving

1. Purpose

To ensure safe, humane, and efficient receiving of live birds into the processing plant while maintaining animal welfare standards, biosecurity, traceability, and operational efficiency.

2. Scope

This procedure applies to all incoming live bird transport vehicles, drivers, receiving team and supervisors.

All processes from arrival to bird unloading and crate sanitation.

3. Responsibility

Role

Responsibility

Live Birds Receiving Supervisor

Oversees all operations and compliance.

Oversee live birds receiving workers.

Security Officer

Manages gate logging of vehicle entry & check the seal, weight & issue the way bridge report.

Truck Driver & Catching Crew

Truck driver present the documents.

Catching crew support to unloading.

Receiving Team

Perform unloading, crate handling, and sanitation.

Maintenance Technician

Ensures proper functioning of fans, trolly and etc.

Quality Assurance (QA)

Verify records and compliance.

4. Definitions

Term

Meaning

Transport Mortality

Birds found dead during transportation.

Welfare Check

Assessment of bird condition (injuries, heat stress, bruises).

Truck Sanitation

Cleaning and disinfecting trucks.

5. Required Records & References

  1. PRD08/FMT/001/2026 Daily bird catching report
  2. PRD08/FMT/002/2026 Weight bridge report
  3. PRD08/FMT/003/2026 Transport mortality report

6. Procedure

Steps

Description

Vehicle Arrival at Security Gate
  1. Verify truck arrival time.
  2. Record vehicle number, driver name, farm name, number of crates, number of empty crates, seal no. and disable bird count.
  3. Check seal tags.
  4. Direct truck to the live bird receiving bay.
Documentation Verification
  1. Receiving Supervisor must verify: Bird Count
  2. If documents are incomplete → hold vehicle and inform live sales department.
Positioning the Vehicle
  1. Guide truck to unloading platform.
  2. Ensure brakes engaged and chocks placed.
  3. Break the seal.
Welfare Check Prior to Unloading
  1. Check birds for: heat stress, injuries
  2. Birds loaded into crates based on their average weight

Refer annexure PDR08/PDM/002/2026.

  1. Record transport mortality count.
Bird Unloading
  1. Unload crates carefully to prevent stress and injury.
  2. Handle birds gently; no throwing or dragging.
  3. Follow correct posture and lifting techniques.
  4. Maintain continuous flow.
  5. Stack up to a maximum height of crates

Refer annexure PDR08/PDM/003/2026.

  1. Maintain gap between every two rows of crates

Refer annexure PDR08/PDM/004/2026.

  1. Spray only clean, potable water.
Welfare and Health Checks
  1. QA supervisor must check:
    • Bird alertness and behaviour
    • Heat stress
    • Bruising or visible injuries
    • Broken wings or legs
    • Overcrowding
    • Signs of disease
Immediate Actions for Welfare Issues Found
  1. Provide ventilation and spray water.

Increase unloading speed if birds show heat stress

Refer annexure PDR08/PDM/005/2026.

Truck Sanitation and Approval
  1. Direct vehicle to sanitation area.
  2. Wash and disinfect exterior and interior.
  3. Check presence of fecal material.
  4. Ensure vehicle is inacceptable sanitary condition.
  5. Inspect crate condition.

7. Safety & Compliance Requirements

  1. Follow all biosecurity protocols (PPE, use footbaths, hand washing).
  2. Entry only authorizes persons.
  3. Avoid rough bird handling.
  4. Keep receiving area clean and non-slippery to avoid worker injury.
  5. Maintain physical distancing between crates and maintain proper ventilation.

Refer annexures PDR08/PDM/004/2026.

  1. Comply with food safety regulatory standards (FSSC 2200, SLS, ISO 9001, ISO 14001).

8. Non-Conformance Handling

Examples of Non-Conformance-:

  1. Excess transport mortality
  2. Unclean truck or crates
  3. Mishandling birds
  4. Incorrect way bridge record and incorrect bird count

Corrective Actions-:

  1. Hold and investigate before unloading
  2. Notify QA supervisor and re-clean truck or crates
  3. Retrain staff
  4. Conduct investigation

8.2. Live Birds Handling and Slaughtering

1. Purpose

To ensure humane, safe, efficient, and hygienic handling and slaughtering of live birds in compliance with animal welfare, food safety regulations, and facility standards.

1. Scope

This SOP applies to all operations from receiving live birds at the hanging station to transfer evisceration.

2. Responsibility

Role

Responsibility

Live bird Handling & Slaughtering Supervisor

Ensures welfare compliance, team supervision and monitors bird welfare, health, and stress conditions.

Trained workers

Perform hanging, slaughtering, removing fin feathers.

Maintenance Technician

Ensures proper functioning of stunning, scalding, de-feathering machinery, cleaning equipment and etc.

Quality Assurance (QA)

Verifies records, hygiene, and OPRPs.

3. Definitions

Term

Meaning

Welfare Check

Assessment of stress, injuries, and overall condition.

Anti-mortem inspection

The examination of live bird external characteristics.

Stunning

Process of making a bird unconscious before slaughter.

Bleeding

Allowing blood to drain after the cut.

Scalding

Process of immersing a bird in hot water after slaughter to loosen feathers and make defeathering easier during processing.

De-feathering

Mechanical removal of feathers.

Feet Removal

Process of cutting of a bird’s feet at the hock joint after slaughter during processing.

1. Procedure

Steps

Description

Welfare Assessment of Birds
  1. Before handling:
    • Anti mortem inspection

Refer annexure PDR08/PDM/007/2026

    • Avoid rough movement
    • Ensure proper ventilation and spray water to reduce bird stress

Refer annexure PDR08/PDM/004/2026

    • Maintain quiet environment, avoid loud noises
Pre-Hanging Holding Conditions
  1. Segregate sick or injured birds.
  2. If abnormal welfare issues appear →inform QA supervisor immediately.
Preparing for Hanging
  1. Ensure shackles are clean.
  2. Adjust line speed according to manpower and welfare requirements

Refer annexure PDR08/PDM/008/2026.

  1. Hang birds one-by-one.
Hanging Process
  1. Hold birds by both legs to prevent wing flapping.
  2. Hang on to shackle gently, avoiding injury.
  3. Ensure breast faces forward, wings remain free.
Welfare Monitoring
  1. QA supervisor must check:
    • Wing flapping levels
    • Any mishandling by workers
  2. Correct immediately if handling is rough or unsafe.
Stunner Pre-Operational Check
  1. Confirm stunning parameters

Refer annexure PDR08/PDM/009/2026.

  1. Ensure stunner is functioning correctly.
Stunning Birds
  1. Ensure birds’ heads fully contact with the Water.
  2. Observe signs of effective stunning:
    • No rhythmic breathing
    • No blinking
    • Head drooping
    • No vocalization
  3. Do not proceed to slaughter without an effective stunning.
Slaughtering-Cutting Technique
  1. Use sharp, sanitized knives.
  2. Ensure the trachea, esophagus, and major blood vessels are severed without cutting spinal cord.
  3. Ensure cut is smooth, quick, and deep enough.
  4. Workers must be trained and halal certified.
Slaughtering-Hygiene Control
  1. Sanitize knives regularly.
  2. Maintain personal hygiene and avoid cross-contamination.
Bleeding-Time
  1. Allow birds to bleed fully before entering scalding

Refer annexure PDR08/PDM/010/2026.

  1. Maintain correct line speed to ensure time is achieved

Refer annexure PDR08/PDM/008/2026.

Monitoring
  1. Check absence of wing flapping.
  2. Ensure no bird is alive when entering scalding.
  3. Report and record any signs of incomplete bleeding.
Head Removing
  1. Remove heads mechanically.
  2. Verify that birds are fully dead before removal.
  3. Ensure clean equipment to avoid contamination.
Scalding Setup
  1. Set water temperature

Refer annexure PDR08/PDM/011/2026.

  1. Maintain water circulation and cleanliness.
  2. Monitor breast appearance for pink coloration or overcooking.
Scalding Process
  1. Ensure carcass is fully submerged.
  2. Maintain exposure time.
  3. Monitor temperature continuously.
  4. Record scalding temperature

Refer annexure PDR08/PDM/012/2026.

De-Feathering
Machine Operation
  1. Check fingers for cleanliness.
  2. Adjust rubber fingers to avoid bruising or tearing skin.
  3. Allow carcasses to pass through automatically.
  4. Check the feather removal quality on carcass.
  5. If any remaining feathers are present, remove them manually.
Hygiene
  1. Timely removal of feather, offal and blood build-up

Refer annexure PDR08/PDM/013/2026.

Feet Removal
  1. Remove feet using mechanical cutter.
  2. Ensure removal is clean and does not contaminate carcass.
Post-Removal Handling
  1. Transfer feet, offal, feathers to offal room.
  2. Dispose of condemned feet as per waste management SOP.

2. Safety & Compliance Requirements

  1. Workers must follow PPE: aprons, gloves, masks, hairnets, safety gloves.
  2. Maintain sanitation: use foot bath, wash hands, tools, and surfaces frequently.
  3. Follow HACCP, FSSC 22000, SLS, ISO 9001, ISO 14001.
  4. Avoid movement from slaughtering to EV and packing section.
  5. Use only EV entrance to enter live birds handling and slaughtering section.
  6. Entry only authorizes persons.
  7. Maintain correct line speed to avoid injuries.
  8. Knives must be cleaned.
  9. Stunning voltage must comply with regulatory limits

Refer annexure PDR08/PDM/009/2026.

  1. Maintain scalding temperatures within documented ranges

Refer annexure PDR08/PDM/011/2026.

3. Non-Conformance Handling

Non-Compliance:

  1. Birds entering scalding while conscious
  2. Rough handling or welfare violations
  3. Incorrect bleeding time
  4. Equipment malfunction (stunner, scalding tank, de-feathering)
  5. Contamination (feces, feathers, grease)

Correction:

  1. Stop line severe quality issue detected
  2. Retrain staff where required
  3. Check bleeding time
  4. Submit job card for equipment malfunction immediately to maintenance department.
  5. Notify QA supervisor

8.3. Evisceration

1. Purpose

To ensure hygienic, safe, and efficient removal of internal organs and processing of liver, heart, gizzard and proventricular from carcasses while preventing contamination, ensuring product quality, and meeting regulatory food safety requirements.

2.Scope

This SOP applies to: Ensure the proper and hygienic removal of internal organs (viscera) from chicken carcasses, followed by inspection of external and internal defects.

  1. Responsibility

Role

Responsibility

Evisceration Supervisor

Overall control of process.

Oversee EV workers.

Evisceration workers

Perform cutting, venting, extraction.

Gizzard, Liver & Proventricular Processing workers

Clean, trim, cut.

QA Supervisor

Conduct post-mortem inspection, maintain hygiene.

Maintenance Team

Ensure the proper functioning and maintenance, calibration of machines, tools & equipment.

Cleaning Team or evisceration workers

Clean equipment and surrounding areas.

  1. Definitions

Term

Meaning

Evisceration

Removal of internal organs (viscera) from the carcass.

Vent

The cloaca area where initial skin cut and vent cutting are performed.

PMI (Post-Mortem Inspection)

Inspection of carcasses external and internal characteristics.

  1. PRD08/FMT/008/2026 Post-mortem inspection check list

  1. Procedure

Steps

Description

Post-Mortem Inspection (PMI)

  1. Provide good lighting and raised platform

Refer annexure PDR08/PDM/015/2026

  1. Examines external inspection criteria and Internal inspection criteria.

Refer annexure PDR08/PDM/016/2026

Actions After Inspection

  1. Healthy for consumption: proceed to evisceration.
  2. Totally condemned: Remove carcass from line and record.

Hanging Carcasses

  1. After de-feathering, hang the carcasses securely on the evisceration shackles.
  2. Ensure legs are fully secured to avoid falling.
  3. Ensure proper alignment of carcasses for safe evisceration (Position the breast side of the carcass facing the person who hanging it).

Pre-check

  1. Check for contamination (fecal, feathers, bruises)
  2. Remove carcasses with defects or abnormalities.

Skin Cut Near the Neck Area

  1. Operator uses a sharp knife to make a small incision at the base of the neck.
  2. Gently pull back the loose skin.

Contamination Prevention

  1. Avoid cutting into the underlying breast muscles.
  2. Replace blunt knives immediately when required.

Using Vent gun

  1. Position vent gun properly around vent.
  2. Activate machine to cut a circular opening.
  3. Maintain machine cleanliness.

Inspection

1. If vent gun cut is incomplete, then send to manual cutting.

Manual vent cutting

  1. Operator must follow proper cutting to complete or correct vertical cut on the vent area using a sharp knife.

Evisceration

  1. Insert the EV tool into the body cavity.
  2. Extract all internal organs in a single clean motion.
  3. Remove liver, heart, gizzard, intestine, proventricular and trachea in order.

Preventing Contamination

  1. Ensure that the intestines are not damaged to prevent fecal contamination.
  2. Remove any visible fecal contamination immediately.
  3. Use water sprayer to wash carcass.
  4. Segregate contaminated carcasses for reprocessing.

Viscera Handling

  1. Avoid contact with floor or dirty surfaces.
  2. Maintain line speed to avoid accumulation.

Gizzard Opening

  1. Remove excess fat using the fat removing machine
  2. Cut the gizzard manually into two halves.
  3. Remove feed particles by scraping.
  4. Use the gizzard peeler machine to remove the yellow inner membrane.
  5. Remove any abnormal gizzard.

Gizzard Washing

  1. Wash thoroughly with clean water.
  2. Ensure no residual feed or lining remains.
  3. Transfer gizzard to the water bath.

Refer annexure PDR08/PDM/017/2026

Gizzard Inspection

  1. Reject the damage, contaminated, or abnormal gizzard.

Proventricular opening

  1. Cut the proventricular into two halves.
  2. Remove feed particles inside the proventricular.

Proventricular Washing

  1. Wash with clean water and salt.

Refer annexure PDR08/PDM/018/2026.

  1. Ensure no residual feed.
  2. Transfer proventricular to the water bath

Refer annexures PDR08/PDM/019/2026.

Inspection

  1. Check for damage, contamination, or abnormalities.
  2. Record any rejections.

Liver Separation

  1. Detach liver from gall bladder carefully.
  2. Avoid rupturing gall bladder (bile contamination hazard).

Liver Washing & Final Check

  1. Wash liver in potable water.
  2. Check for colour uniformity and cleanliness.
  3. Reject abnormal liver.
  4. Transfer liver to the water bath

Refer annexure PDR08/PDM/020/2026.

  1. Safety & Compliance Requirements
  1. Workers must follow PPE: aprons, gloves, masks, hairnets.
  2. Maintain sanitation: use foot bath, wash hands, tools, and surfaces frequently.
  3. Follow HACCP, FSSC 22000, SLS, ISO 9001, ISO 14001.
  4. Avoid movement from EV to slaughtering and packing section.
  5. Use only EV entrance
  6. Keep knives sharp, clean regularly and remove blunted knives and Report.
  7. Entry only authorizes persons.
  8. Maintain proper temperatures (gizzard, liver, proventricular).

Refer annexure PDR08/PDM/017/2026

Refer annexure PDR08/PDM/018/2026

Refer annexure PDR08/PDM/019/2026

  1. Non-Conformance Handling

Non-Compliance:

  1. Carcasses bypassing PMI
  2. Fecal contamination during vent cut
  3. Gallbladder rupture during evisceration, unclean vent cut, incomplete removal of viscera and damaged hearts/gizzards/livers
  4. Equipment malfunction

Correction:

  1. Notify QA supervisor
  2. Trim contaminated area or rewash
  3. Retrain worker
  4. Submit job card for equipment malfunction immediately to maintenance department

8.4. Packing

1. Purpose

To ensure poultry carcasses are properly washed, chilled, drained, processed, rework and portion products packed under hygienic and controlled conditions to maintain product quality and food safety.

2. Scope

This SOP applies to all production staff and workers involved in,

screw chiller operation, drip line handling, portioning, grading, weighing, product packing, reworking, labelling and transfer products to blast freezer.

Role

Responsibility

Packing Supervisors

Oversee packing workers.

Overall control of packing process.

Ensure all packing materials meet approved standards.

Verify product core temperature after chilling.

Conduct hygiene, microbiological limits, labelling, chlorine level and weight checks.

Maintenance Technician

Ensure proper functioning of screw chiller, drip drum, grading machine, scales, printing machine, sealing machines, cutter machine and other equipment as per standards.

Conduct pre-operation check of machineries.

Packing workers

Conduct portioning, packing, sealing, and labelling.

Follow hygiene and safety requirements.

Meaning

A rotating chilled-water system used to reduce carcass temperature.

Drip Time

Time required to allow excess water to drain after chilling.
