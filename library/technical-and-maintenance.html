  1. Purpose

To establish a standardized procedure for the safe operation, inspection, preventive maintenance, breakdown maintenance, and servicing of production machinery and equipment used in the poultry processing plant.

  1. Scope

This SOP applies to all production Purpose -related machinery and equipment.

  1. Responsibility

Role

Responsibility

Maintenance Manager

  • Plan, organize, and supervise all maintenance activities to ensure maximum equipment reliability and uptime.
  • Monitor and improve equipment performance, minimizing breakdowns and downtime.
  • Ensure all machinery, utilities, and facilities are maintained according to safety and operational standards.

Maintenance Executive

  • Prepare and implement preventive maintenance schedules for all machines and equipment.
  • Ensure maintenance activities are carried out as per the planned schedule.
  • Maintain proper records of all preventive maintenance work.

Production technicians

  • Carry out maintenance activities

20.5 Wastewater plant maintenance

  1. Purpose

The propose of the wastewater treatment system is to treat and manage wastewater generated from production and utility operations to meet required environmental and regulatory standards before discharge or reuse.

  1. Scope

This procedure applies to all wastewater generated from production, cleaning, and utility operations withing the facility.

  1. Responsibility

Role

Responsibility

Maintenance manager

  • Plan and oversee maintenance of all wastewater treatment systems and equipment.
  • Develop and implement preventive maintenance schedules.
  • Coordinate with production and quality teams to handle wastewater issues.

Maintenance Executive

  • Execute daily operation and maintenance of wastewater treatment system.
  • Attend and resolve breakdowns of pumps and treatment units.

Technicians

  • Carry out preventive and corrective maintenance task as assigned.
  • Attend breakdown and support quick fault recertification.
  • Perform routine inspections and report issues immediately.
  • Use tools and equipment properly and safely.
  1. Definitions

Term

Meaning

Waste water

Water contaminated from production, Cleaning or utility operations.

Effluent

Treated wastewater discharge from the plant

Influent

Untreated wastewater entering the treatment plant.

Sludge

Solid waste generated during treatment.

Equalization tank

Equalization is the process of collecting and homogenizing wastewater to balance variations in flow and pollutant concentration, ensuring a consistent and controlled feed to subsequent treatment processes.

Aeration blowers

Aeration blowers are mechanical devices used to supply air or oxygen into the aeration tank to maintain adequate dissolved oxygen levels for effective biological treatment of wastewater.

The propose of welding activities is join, repair and fabricate metal components to ensure the safe, reliable and continuous operation of plant equipment and structures

This procedure applies to all welding activities carried out within the maintenance and technical department for repair, fabrication, and modification of plant equipment and structures.

  • Carry out welding, cutting, and fabrication work as assigned.

20.7 Aluminium & Fabrication

  1. Propose

This SOP will provide clear guidelines for all activities related to the aluminium section.

  1. Scope

This SOP will cover all processes within the aluminium works.

  1. Responsibility

Role

Responsibility

Maintenance Manager

  • Oversee overall operations of the Aluminum Section
  • Approve the SOP and ensure its effective implementation
  • Coordinate with maintenance and quality teams

Maintenance Executive

  • Ensure daily operations are carried out according to the SOP
  • Supervise operators and allocate tasks efficiently
  • Monitor production quality
  • Report any deviations, breakdowns, or safety issues
  • Maintain production and process records

Aluminium Technicians

  • Perform aluminum cutting, drilling, bending, and fabrication activities according to approved drawings and specifications
  • Operate fabrication machines (cutting machines, drilling machines, bending tools, etc.) safely and efficiently
  • Read and interpret technical drawings, measurements, and job orders accurately
  • Ensure proper material selection and usage to minimize wastage
  • Follow all safety procedures, including proper use of PPE.
  • Maintain cleanliness and proper housekeeping of the work area
  • Support preventive maintenance activities in coordination with the maintenance team
  • Record daily work activities and report progress as required

Aluminium Helpers

  • Assist the Aluminum Fabrication Technician in all fabrication activities (cutting, drilling, bending, and assembly)
  • Support material handling, including loading, unloading, and transferring aluminum sections safely
  • Prepare materials and tools required for fabrication work as instructed
  • Hold and position materials during cutting, drilling, or fitting operations
  • Clean and deburr fabricated parts as required
  • Assist in installation and on-site fitting work when necessary
  • Maintain cleanliness of the work area and ensure proper housekeeping at all times
  • Handle tools and equipment carefully and return them to designated locations after use
  • Follow all safety instructions and use required PPE
  • Support waste segregation and proper disposal of scrap materials

  • Perform cutting using appropriate machines/tools
  • Ensure edges are straight and within specified tolerance

Drilling and shaping

  • Drill holes and perform shaping as per design requirements
  • Maintain correct dimensions and alignment
  • Remove burrs and sharp edges after processing

Assembly and fitting

  • Assemble parts according to drawings and instructions
  • Use proper fasteners, joints, or welding methods if required
  • Check alignment and fit-up before final fixing

Finishing work

  • Clean fabricated items and remove dust, oil, or sharp edges
  • Apply protective covering if required
  • Prepare items for storage or dispatch

  • Decide rework, repair, or scrap
  • Apply corrective action before restart
