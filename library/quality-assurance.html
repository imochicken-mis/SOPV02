SOP-QA-10.01: Implementation Food Safety Management System

01.Purpose

The purpose of this procedure is to establish, implement, maintain, and continually improve the Food Safety Management System (FSMS) to ensure the safe production of raw frozen chicken products.

The FSMS integrates:

• Prerequisite Programs (PRPs)
• Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP)
• Operational PRPs (OPRPs)
• HACCP Principles

02. Scope

This procedure applies to all activities related to the processing, storage, and distribution of raw frozen chicken products and is applicable to all employees, contractors, visitors, and management involved in these operations.

03. Responsibility

6.3 Live Bird Receiving

Ensure that only healthy live birds are accepted for processing and to prevent the entry of diseased or contaminated birds into the production process.

Inspected all incoming live birds by the QA supervisor at the receiving area.

Inspection conducted in accordance with Annex 23 - ICMS-WI-PD-01

Results of the inspection documented in Annex 24 - QAD_010_CEL_2026_004

Any birds found dead, diseased, or unfit for processing shall be segregated and reported immediately to the and QA Manager.

Acceptance or rejection of the batch determined based on Annex 11 - Procedure for NC products

Document the incident in the Non-Conformance Form. Annex 26 - NCF Form

6.4 Holding

Maintained live birds under appropriate animal welfare conditions prior to slaughter, minimizing stress, injury, and mortality accordance with Work Instruction Annex 23 - ICMS-WI-PD-01

Birds showing severe injury, illness, or distress immediately segregated and reported to the QA Manager.

6.5 Anti Mortem Inspection

Approve only healthy and fit birds are for slaughter, preventing the entry of diseased or contaminated birds into the processing line.

Conducted Antemortem inspection in the live bird holding area prior to slaughter.

Carried out the inspection by a trained supervisor under the supervision of a qualified veterinary doctor.

Inspection performed in accordance with Annex 25 - Ante-Mortem and Post-Mortem Rejection Criteria.

6.6 Hanging

Handled live birds minimizing stress, injury, and contamination prior to stunning and slaughter.

Handled gently to avoid stress or injury

Both legs must be securely placed in shackles

Injured or weak birds shall be identified and segregated

6.7 Stunning

Ensure effectively stunned prior to slaughter, minimizing pain and stress while ensuring proper bleeding and compliance with animal welfare and food safety requirements.

Verify the effectiveness of stunning through observation of bird condition immediately after stunning by QA Supervisor. Annex 27 - Monitor effectiveness of stunning and compliance Procedure

Record all monitoring results in Annex 28 - QAD010/CEL/2026/022 - Stunning Monitoring Checklist

6.8 Slaughtering & Bleeding

Slaughtering and bleeding check accordance with:
Annex 29 - Work Instruction ICMS-WI-PD-02

Annex 30 - Work Instruction ICMS-WI-PD-03

6.10 Post Mortem Inspection

Ensure that only healthy, safe, and high-quality carcasses enter further processing, by detecting and removing diseased, contaminated, or defective birds after slaughter.

QA Supervisor verify all rejected carcasses are properly handled and recorded.

Inspection performed in accordance with Annex 25 - Ante-Mortem and Post-Mortem Rejection Criteria.

6.11 Evisceration

Ensure proper removal of internal organs while maintaining food safety, hygiene, and product quality, preventing contamination of carcasses with fecal matter or other hazards.

Inspection performed in accordance with Annex 31 – Work Instruction ICMS-WI-PD-04

Results of the inspection documented in Annex 24 - QAD_010_CEL_2026_004

Equipment & Line Setup

  • Check Proper vent cut (no tearing of intestines)
  • Ensure No sharp or damaged parts in EV tool
  • Ensure Line speed within validated limits
  • Check Backup tools available and clean
  • Check intestinal rupture and fecal contamination

Fecal Contamination Control

  • Check zero tolerance for visible fecal matter on carcass
  • Check vent area, and abdominal cavity cleanliness
  • Immediate removal of contaminated carcasses
  • Reprocessing only with approved trimming and washing

Intestinal Integrity

  • Check for Intestines removed intact
  • Ensure no ruptured gall bladder (bile contamination)
  • Check ruptured crop or gizzard
  • If bile contamination is seen, Immediate trimming

Cross-Contamination Prevention

  • Check whether separation of edible offal and inedible waste
  • Check workers handling offal and not touching carcasses with same gloves

Liver processing

  • Monitor for Uniform color (reddish-brown) and free from green bile stains, Abscesses, Hemorrhage, Parasites, Gall bladder removed intact. Reject liver if: bile-stained, ruptured, diseased.
  • Verify liver chilling temperature.

Gizzard Processing

  • Monitoring for - No residual feed or membrane, Smooth, clean muscle surface
  • Verify gizzard chilling temperature.
  • Inspection performed in accordance with Annex 32 – Work Instruction ICMS-WI-PD-05

Proventriculus Processing

  • Monitoring for No lesions, discoloration and no off-odor

6.12 Washing & Chilling

Monitor carcasses are washed and chilled using potable water, meeting food safety, hygiene, and temperature requirements, and maintaining product quality.

Verification of potable water

Verification of potable water according to the testing schedule Annex 33 - Testing Schedule ICMS TSS

Monitor Screw Chiller water temperature and Carcass Temperature

According to Annex 34 – Work instruction ICMS-WI-PD-06 and record all data in the Annex 35 -QAD_010_CEL_2026_022 Check list. Annex 37 - Chilling Process

Measure temperature with a calibrated thermometer Annex 36 - Use of thermometer

Verify chlorination of water

Verify chlorination of water by measuring free chlorine using a test kit and ensure chlorine levels are within plant specified range 30ppm and record in QAD010/CEL/2026/019

Water Cleanliness Verification

Inspect water visually for debris, feathers, or dirt. record all data in the Annex 35 -QAD_010_CEL_2026_022 Check list.

  • Record measurements in Annex 38 - Operational Inspection Check list QAD_010_CEL_2026_013.

Annex 38 - Operational Inspection Check list QAD_010_CEL_2026_013.

SOP-QA-10.06 Management of FSSC Additional Requirements

  1. Purpose

To define the system for managing, implementing, and monitoring FSSC 22000 Additional Requirements through controlled procedures to ensure food safety, regulatory compliance, and continuous improvement.

  1. Scope

This SOP applies to all FSSC 22000 Additional Requirements implemented within the organization, including food safety culture, external services, food defense, food fraud, and environmental monitoring.

  1. Responsibility

Role

Responsibilities

Top Management

Ensure commitment to food safety and FSSC requirements

Provide necessary resources

Review system performance

Quality Assurance Department

Ensure implementation of all additional requirement procedures

Monitor compliance and effectiveness

Maintain documentation and records

Coordinate audits and reviews

Department Heads

Implement relevant procedures in their areas

Support QA in compliance activities

  1. Definitions

Team

Meaning

FSSC 22000

Food Safety System Certification standard for food safety management, based on ISO 22000, PRPs, and additional FSSC requirements.

Framework ensuring that all food safety hazards are identified, controlled, and monitored in the food production process.

Additional Requirements (FSSC 22000)

Mandatory requirements in FSSC 22000 beyond ISO 22000 and PRPs, such as food safety culture, food defense, and fraud prevention.

Specific extra controls that must be implemented to strengthen food safety management.

Food Safety Culture

Shared values, beliefs, and behaviors that prioritize food safety in the organization.

Ensures that all employees understand, respect, and follow food safety practices.

Escalation

The process of reporting critical or high-risk issues to higher management for immediate action.

Ensures timely decision-making for serious complaints, non-conformances, or food safety incidents.

Corrective Action

Actions taken to eliminate the cause of a detected non-conformity.

Prevent recurrence of a problem in production or quality.

Preventive Action

Actions taken to eliminate the cause of potential non-conformities.

Proactively improves systems to avoid future issues.

Non-Conformance

Failure to meet specified requirements (e.g., product, process, or system).

Triggers investigation, corrective action, and monitoring.

External Laboratory

A third-party accredited lab that performs sampling and testing.

Ensures objective, independent verification of product quality and food safety.

Customer Complaint

Any feedback from a customer indicating dissatisfaction with product quality, safety, or service.

Requires investigation, corrective/preventive actions, and resolution.

Food Defense

Measures to protect food from intentional contamination.

Protects products, consumers, and brand reputation.

Food Fraud

Intentional substitution, addition, tampering, or misrepresentation of food for economic gain.

Prevents adulteration, counterfeiting, and mislabeling of products.

Traceability

Ability to track a product through all stages of production, processing, and distribution.

Ensures affected batches can be identified quickly in case of non-conformance.
