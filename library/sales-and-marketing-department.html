SOP-SMD-011.01 : Route Planning

  1. Purpose

To design efficient, balanced, and profitable Sales Territories and Routes for each Sales Representative in order to provide timely service to customers, achieve sales targets, minimize operational costs, and maximize the utilization of available resources.

  1. Scope

This procedure applies to the Sales Manager, Field Sales Managers (FSM), Sales Representatives, and Sales Administration Department. It covers the creation of new Sales Territories, Territory Realignment, Route Planning, and updating of existing Routes.

  1. Responsibility

Role

Responsibility

Sales Admin Manager

Review and approve Route Plans and Territory Alignment and make final decisions.

Field Sales Manager (FSM)

Analyze Sales Territories, prepare Route Plans, and manage the field activities of Sales Representatives.

Sales Representative

Visit customers according to the Route Plan, provide territory information, and submit recommendations for necessary updates.

Sales Administration Officer

Maintain Route Plans, Territory Maps, and related documents.

  1. Procedure

Steps

Description

Route Planning

A specific Sales Territory shall be allocated to each Sales Representative.

  • Each Route Plan shall be designed to cover a minimum of five (05) working days per week.
  • The Route Plan shall be implemented only after obtaining approval from the Sales Manager.
  • Each Sales Representative shall follow the Route Plan allocated to them.
  • Prior approval shall be obtained before making any changes to an approved Route.

Factors to be Considered When Preparing Route Plans

  1. Distance
  • Routes shall be planned to minimize travel time.
  • Routes shall be selected to minimize fuel costs.
  • Customers located along the same route shall be included in the same Route wherever practical.
  1. Customer Base

The following factors shall be analysed:

  • Active Customers
  • Potential Customers
  • New Customer Opportunities
  • Customer Classification
  1. Competition

The following factors shall be evaluated:

  • Competing Brands
  • Market Share
  • Pricing Strategy
  • Promotional Activities
  1. Market Demand

The following factors shall be considered:

  • Product Demand
  • Sales Potential
  • Seasonal Demand
  • Customer Buying Pattern
  1. Location & Road Network
  • Road Conditions
  • Traffic Congestion
  • Accessibility
  • Geographical Location
  1. Demographics

The following factors shall be considered:

  • Population Density
  • Income Levels
  • Consumer Lifestyle
  • Buying Behaviour
  • Target Customer Groups

Route Plan Approval

After preparing the Route Plan:

  • The Field Sales Manager shall review the Route Plan.
  • The Sales Manager shall provide final approval.
  • Only approved Route Plans shall be implemented.

Route Review & Update

Route Plans shall be reviewed under the following circumstances:

  • Addition of new customers.
  • Removal of existing customers.
  • Changes in Sales Performance.
  • Market Changes.
  • Business Expansion.
  • Distribution Changes.
  • Route Review shall be conducted at least once every six (06) months or whenever required.

Territory Boundary Definition

When creating new Routes or reorganizing existing Routes, the following factors shall be considered.

  1. Customer Distribution
  • An excessive number of customers shall not be assigned to one Sales Representative.
  • Customer Distribution shall be balanced to ensure efficient service.
  1. Sales Potential
  • Sales Potential shall be distributed fairly among Routes.
  • The sales capacity of a Route shall not exceed the capability of the relevant Sales Representative.
  1. Competitive Environment

When planning Routes, the following shall be considered:

  • Competition Level
  • Market Coverage
  • Growth Opportunities
  1. Cost Optimization

Route Boundaries shall be designed to minimize:

  • Fuel Cost
  • Travel Cost
  • Vehicle Utilization Cost
  • Operational Cost
  1. Customer Service

Routes shall be designed to:

  • Increase Customer Visit Frequency.
  • Minimize Response Time.
  • Improve Customer Satisfaction.
  1. Distribution Efficiency

The following shall be considered when planning Routes:

  • Delivery Efficiency
  • Delivery Lead Time
  • Vehicle Capacity
  • Delivery Route

Market Study & Field Survey

Before carrying out Route Alignment or Realignment, the following activities are mandatory:

  • Conduct a Market Study.
  • Conduct a Field Survey.
  • Analyze Customer Distribution.
  • Analyze Sales Performance.
  • Conduct Competitor Analysis.
  • Analyze Route Efficiency.

Based on these analyses, a detailed report shall be prepared covering:

  • Current Situation.
  • Analysis Results.
  • Requirement for Route Changes.
  • Proposed Route Map.
  • Cost and Benefit Analysis.
  • Recommendations.

The report shall be submitted to the Sales Manager and Sales Admin Manager for approval. Route changes shall be implemented only after obtaining the required approval.

  1. Forms & Records

• Route Planning Form

• Sales Territory Allocation Form

• Territory Review Report

• Market Study Report

• Field Survey Report

• Territory Realignment Proposal

• Approved Route Plan

• Customer Master List

• Territory Map

• Route Visit Schedule

  1. Safety & Compliance Requirements
  • Sales Admin Manager approval shall be mandatory for all Route Plans and Route Changes.
  • A Market Study and Field Survey shall be conducted before carrying out any Route Realignment.
  • Route Plans and Route Maps shall be reviewed at least once every six months.
  • All Route Changes, Route Amendments, and Customer Allocation Changes shall be documented and an Audit Trail shall be maintained.
  • Consistency between GPS/ERP data and the Route Plan shall be reviewed regularly.
  • Route Performance Reports shall be reviewed monthly by Sales Management and necessary Corrective Actions shall be taken.

SOP-SMD-011.02 : Customer Registration and Customer Code Issuance

  1. Purpose

To establish a standard procedure for registering new customers in accordance with the organization's policies and control procedures, entering accurate and complete customer information into the ERP system, obtaining the required approvals, and issuing, modifying, and cancelling Customer Codes.

  1. Scope

This procedure applies to the registration of all new customers, Customer Code issuance, maintenance of the ERP Customer Master, Credit Customer approval, and Customer Code cancellation. It applies to Sales Representatives, Field Sales Managers (FSM), Sales Administration Department, Finance Department, and ERP Administration.

  1. Responsibility

Role

Responsibilities

Sales Representative

Complete the Customer Registration Form, obtain the required documents, and submit the registration for processing.

Field Sales Manager (FSM)

Review the Customer Registration, approve On-Date Cheque transactions, and recommend Customer Registration.

Sales Administration Manager

Approve Credit Customers, approve Customer Code issuance, and approve Customer Code cancellation.

Sales Administration Officer

Verify documents, create the ERP Customer Master, issue Customer Codes, and update customer information.

ERP Administration

Maintain the Customer Master accurately in the ERP system and ensure system controls are maintained.

  1. Procedure

Step

Description

New Customer Registration

To register a new customer, the approved Customer Registration Form shall be completed accurately and completely and submitted to the Sales Administration Department.

Mandatory Documents

The following documents shall be obtained together with the Customer Registration Form:

  • Copy of National Identity Card (NIC) of the relevant individual.
  • Business Registration Certificate (BR).
  • VAT Registration Certificate – for VAT-registered businesses.
  • Photographs of the front and inside of the business premises.
  • Location Map of the business premises.
  • If the customer is a company, details of a Director or Manager.
  • If the customer is a hotel/institution, details of the Head of the Purchasing Department.

The Sales Administration Officer shall verify that all required documents are complete.

Payment Method Approval

The required approvals shall be obtained according to the customer's payment method.

On-Date Cheque

  • Written approval from the Field Sales Manager (FSM) is mandatory.

Credit Invoice / Post-Dated Cheque (PDC)

  • Written approval from the Sales Administration Manager is mandatory.

The following shall also be approved together with the payment method:

  • Credit Period
  • Credit Limit
  • Price Category

A customer shall not be activated as a Credit Customer in the ERP system until the required approvals have been obtained.

Credit Facility Approval

Before granting a credit facility, the following factors shall be evaluated:

  • Stability of the customer's business.
  • Existing stock level at the business premises.
  • Previous transaction history.
  • Market Reputation.
  • Trade References.
  • Bank References.
  • Financial Capability.

A Field Verification may be conducted where necessary.

Customer Code Issuance

After confirming that all required information and documents for Customer Registration are complete:

  • The Field Sales Manager shall provide the recommendation.
  • The Sales Administration Manager shall provide final approval.

After approval, the Sales Administration Officer shall:

  • Issue the Customer Code.
  • Create the ERP Customer Master.
  • Accurately enter Customer Name, Address, Contact Details, Tax Information, Payment Terms, Credit Limit, Price Category, and other relevant information.
  • Activate the Customer Status as Active.

A Customer Code shall not be issued without the required approval.

Customer Information Update

If any of the following customer information changes, it shall be updated in the ERP system immediately:

  • Address
  • Telephone Numbers
  • Business Name
  • VAT Information
  • BR Information
  • Contact Person
  • Bank Details
  • Payment Terms

All amendments shall be supported by the required documents and approvals.

Customer Code Cancellation

A Customer Code may be cancelled due to the following reasons:

  • Shop Closed.
  • Long Outstanding Issues.
  • No purchases made during a period of 12 months.
  • Management Decision.
  • Other valid business reasons.

The following approvals are mandatory for Customer Code cancellation:

  • Written approval from the Field Sales Manager.
  • Written approval from the Sales Administration Manager.

Upon cancellation:

  • Customer Status shall be changed to Inactive in the ERP system.
  • The reason for cancellation shall be recorded in the ERP system and Customer Master File.
  • All relevant documents shall be filed for Audit purposes.
  1. Forms & Records
  • Customer Registration Form
  • Customer Information Update Form
  • Customer Credit Approval Form
  • Credit Limit Approval Form
  • Customer Code Request Form
  • Customer Code Cancellation Form
  • Customer Master File
  • ERP Customer Master Record
  • Field Verification Report
  • Customer Document Checklist
  1. Safety & Compliance Requirements
  • A Customer Code shall not be issued if all required registration documents are incomplete.
  • Written approval for Credit Limit, Credit Period, and Price Category shall be mandatory before activating a customer as a Credit Customer.
  • An Audit Trail shall be maintained in the ERP system for Customer Code issuance, amendments, and cancellation.
  • The ERP Customer Master shall be reviewed monthly to identify and correct Inactive or Duplicate Customer Codes.
  • The Customer Master File and relevant documents shall be securely stored in accordance with the organization's document retention policy.

SOP-SMD-011.03 : Sales Order Management

  1. Purpose

To ensure that customer orders are received accurately, completely, and within the required time, entered correctly into the ERP system, and processed efficiently to ensure accuracy and effectiveness throughout the Sales Order process.

  1. Scope

This procedure applies to all Sales Representatives, Field Sales Managers (FSM), Sales Administration Department, ERP Department, and Finished Goods Department.

  1. Responsibility

Role

Responsibilities

Sales Representative

Obtain customer orders and enter them accurately into the ERP system.

Field Sales Manager (FSM)

Approve special orders and Order Amendments/Cancellations.

Sales Admin Manager

Approve Orders requiring special approval and Order Amendments/Cancellations.

Sales Admin Officer

Review Orders and carry out the necessary coordination. Maintain accurate ERP data and carry out approved amendments.

Finished Goods Officer

Confirm stock availability and coordinate Order Fulfilment.

  1. Procedure

Step

Description

Obtaining Sales Orders

When obtaining an Order, the Sales Representative shall consider the following requirements.

  1. Customer Requirement Identification
  • Confirm the products and quantities required by the customer.
  • Confirm the required Delivery Date and Delivery Location.
  • Check whether there are any special requirements such as Packing, Labelling, or Cutting Requirements.
  1. Sales History Review

Before obtaining an Order, the following shall be considered:

  • Previous Sales History.
  • Normal Buying Pattern.
  • Seasonal Demand.
  • Customer Buying Pattern.
  1. Product Planning

The following shall be considered when obtaining Orders:

  • Products prioritized by the organization.
  • Slow Moving / Non-moving Items.
  • Excess Stock Items.
  • Promotional Products.
  • Available Inventory.
  1. Route & Sales Target Consideration

Orders shall be obtained:

  • In line with the planned Route Plan.
  • In a manner that supports achieving Sales Targets.
  • Considering Delivery Capacity.

ERP Order Entry

After receiving the Order, the Sales Representative shall accurately enter the following information into the ERP system:

  • Customer Code
  • Product Code
  • Product Description
  • Quantity
  • Selling Price
  • Discount
  • Delivery Date
  • Delivery Route
  • Special Instructions

Before saving the ERP Order, the following shall be rechecked:

  • Quantity
  • Price
  • Discount
  • Delivery Date
  • Customer Details

Special Approval Requirements

Prior to confirming an ERP Order, approval from the FSM and Sales Admin Manager shall be obtained for the following Orders:

  • Non-standard Products
  • Promotional Prices
  • Special Discounts
  • Large Quantity Orders
  • Urgent Deliveries
  • Special Packing Requirements
  • Customer Requested Price Deviations

Approval shall be obtained in writing or through the ERP Approval Workflow.

Order Cut-off Time

The following Cut-off Times shall be followed for entering Customer Orders into the ERP system:

Day

Cut-off Time

Monday – Saturday

Before 6.00 p.m.

Sunday

Before 4.00 p.m.

Orders received after the Cut-off Time shall be planned for the next Delivery Cycle. Sales Admin Manager approval shall be mandatory for special requirements.

Order Amendment / Cancellation

To amend or cancel an Order entered into the ERP system, approval shall be obtained from:

  • Field Sales Manager (FSM)
  • Sales Admin Manager

After approval, the following officers shall be informed:

  • Sales Admin Officer
  • Head of ERP
  • Finished Goods Officer

The reason for all Order Amendments and Cancellations shall be recorded in the ERP system or Order Amendment Log.

Order Fulfillment Communication

If a Customer Order cannot be fulfilled fully or partially due to stock shortages or other operational reasons:

  • The Sales Admin Department shall immediately inform the relevant Sales Representative.
  • The Sales Representative shall inform the customer and agree on an alternative solution such as Partial Delivery, Substitute Product, or Revised Delivery Date.
  • All changes shall be recorded in the ERP system.
  1. Forms & Records
  • Sales Order (ERP)
  • Customer Order Register
  • Special Approval Form
  • Order Amendment Form
  • Order Cancellation Form
  • Daily Order Report
  • Order Exception Report
  • Delivery Schedule
  1. Safety & Compliance Requirements
  • Order Entry and Order Amendment shall be carried out only according to ERP User Access Rights.
  • Mandatory approval shall be obtained for Price Override, Special Discounts, and Non-standard Orders.
  • An ERP Audit Trail shall be maintained for Order Amendments and Cancellations.
  • The Daily Order Exception Report shall be reviewed by the Sales Admin Manager.
  • Compliance with Order Cut-off Times shall be monitored daily.
  • Reconciliation between ERP Orders and Customer Orders shall be conducted daily.

SOP-SMD-011.04 : Sales Invoice Issuance & Control

  1. Purpose

To ensure that Sales Invoices are issued to the correct customers with accurate product details, quantities, prices, and tax information, while ensuring proper invoice control, payment documentation, and accuracy throughout the revenue process.

  1. Scope

This procedure applies to Sales Representatives, Sales Administration Department, Finance Department, ERP Department, and all Cash, Credit, and Cheque sales.

  1. Responsibility

Role

Responsibilities

Sales Representative

Verify all information before issuing the Invoice, sign the Invoice, and obtain the customer's signature.

Sales Administration Officer

Issue Invoices through the ERP system, control Invoices, and coordinate Credit Note issuance.

Finance Officer

Review and approve Credit Invoices, Cheques, and Credit Notes.

Field Sales Manager (FSM)

Approve Invoice Cancellations and special circumstances.

Sales Administration Manager

Approve Credit Notes and special price amendments.

  1. Procedure

Step

Description

Pre-Invoice Verification

Before printing an Invoice, the following information shall be verified in the ERP system:

  • Customer Code
  • Customer Name
  • Billing Address
  • Credit Status
  • Mode of Payment (Cash / Credit / Cheque)
  • VAT / Tax Status
  • Item Code and Item Description
  • Quantity
  • Batch Number, where applicable
  • Selling Price
  • Discount, where applicable

The Invoice shall be printed only after confirming that all the above information is correct.

Invoice Printing & Certification

  • The Invoice shall be printed from the ERP system.
  • Every Invoice shall be signed and certified by the Sales Representative.
  • The Invoice details shall be checked one final time before handing it over to the customer.

Cheque Payment Collection

When accepting a Cheque, the following requirements shall be fulfilled:

  • The Cheque shall be made payable to the correct Payee.
  • Cheque Date, Amount, and Signature shall be correct.
  • The customer's signature shall be obtained using the official rubber stamp as required by the organization.
  • Cheque details shall be recorded in the ERP system or relevant register.

Credit Invoice Control

When issuing a Credit Invoice:

  • The customer's signature shall be obtained on all three (03) Invoice Copies.
  • The customer's rubber stamp and the organization's official rubber stamp shall be affixed.
  • The Sales Representative shall retain one copy of the Credit Invoice.
  • The signed copy shall be submitted to the company within the required time period.

Invoice Cancellation

If an Invoice needs to be cancelled:

  • The word "CANCELLED" shall be clearly marked on the Original Invoice.
  • A photograph of the cancelled Invoice shall be sent to the relevant WhatsApp Group.
  • Written or digital approval shall be obtained from the Field Sales Manager (FSM).
  • The Sales Administration Department shall update the Invoice Status as Cancelled in the ERP system.
  • The reason for cancellation shall be recorded in the ERP Audit Trail.

Credit Note Issuance

Credit Notes may be issued for the following reasons:

  • Price Changes.
  • Billing Errors.
  • Invoice Corrections.
  • Approved Sales Returns.
  • Price Amendments agreed with special institutions or star-class hotels.

Credit Notes shall be issued only by:

  • Sales Administration Department; or
  • Finance Department

and the required approvals shall be obtained.

Invoice Record Management

  • All Invoice Copies and Credit Notes shall be properly documented and securely stored.
  • ERP Invoice Records shall be updated daily.
  • Invoice Number Sequences shall be maintained continuously without gaps, except for properly documented cases.
  1. Forms & Records
  • Sales Invoice
  • Credit Invoice
  • Cash Invoice
  • Cheque Collection Register
  • Invoice Cancellation Form
  • Credit Note
  • Debit Note, where applicable
  • Invoice Amendment Request Form
  • Daily Invoice Report
  • ERP Invoice Register
  1. Safety & Compliance Requirements
  • Customer Code, Payment Mode, VAT Status, Item Details, Quantity, and Batch Number shall be verified before printing an Invoice.
  • Sales Representative and Customer signatures, together with rubber stamps where applicable, shall be obtained for all relevant Invoices.
  • FSM approval and an ERP Audit Trail shall be mandatory for Invoice Cancellations.
  • Credit Notes shall be issued only by the Sales Administration Department or Finance Department.
  • All Invoice, Credit Note, and Cancellation Records shall be reviewed monthly by the Finance and Sales Administration Departments and maintained for Internal Audit purposes.

SOP-SMD-011.05 : Customer Collection & Receipt Issuance

  1. Purpose

To establish a standard procedure for collecting payments from customers, properly receiving Cash, Cheques, and Online Payments, accurately recording such payments in the ERP system, and issuing Receipts in a timely manner.

  1. Scope

This SOP applies to the collection of payments through Cash, Cheque, and Online Transfer, issuance and cancellation of Receipts, and management of Dishonored Cheques.

  1. Responsibility

Role

Responsibilities

Marketing Representative / Sales Representative

Collect Cash, Cheques, and Online Payments from customers.

Sales Admin Officer

Enter Payments into the ERP system, issue Receipts, and verify relevant documents.

Field Sales Manager (FSM)

Approve Receipt Cancellations and special circumstances.

Sales Admin Manager

Supervise the Collection Process, approve Receipt Cancellations, and control Dishonored Cheques.

Sales Admin Executive

Carry out Bank Deposits, Bank Reconciliation, and update Accounts Receivable.

  1. Procedure

Step

Description

Checks Before Payment Collection

Before collecting payments, the following information shall be checked through the ERP system or Customer Statement:

  • Customer Outstanding Balance
  • Overdue Invoice List
  • Credit Limit
  • Customer Credit Status

Note:

  • Priority shall be given to settling old Overdue Invoices.
  • Where necessary, Outstanding balances shall be confirmed with the Finance Department and FSM.

Cash Payment

  • Cash shall be counted and verified in the presence of the customer.
  • A Receipt shall be issued immediately after recording the Cash in the ERP system.
  • The collected Cash shall be securely handed over to the Finance Department.

Cheque Payment

Before accepting a Cheque, the following shall be checked:

  • Cheque Date
  • Amount in Figures
  • Amount in Words
  • Authorized Signature
  • Payee Name – official company name

The following details shall be written on the reverse side of the Cheque:

  • Customer Name
  • Customer Address
  • Invoice Number
  • Sales Representative Name

Note:

  • Post-dated Cheques shall be accepted only in accordance with company policy.
  • Cheque Details shall be accurately entered into the ERP system.

Online Payment

When payment is made through Online Transfer:

  • The customer shall make the payment at the relevant time.
  • A Bank Transfer Slip or Payment Confirmation Copy shall be obtained.
  • Payment shall be entered into the ERP system after confirming receipt of the funds with the Finance Department.
  • Thereafter, the Receipt shall be issued

Receipt Issuance

Immediately after receiving Cash, Cheque, or Online Payment, the following details shall be accurately entered into the ERP system:

  • Customer Code
  • Customer Name
  • Invoice Number(s)
  • Payment Date
  • Payment Method
  • Amount Received
  • Reference Number (Cheque / Transfer Reference)

A Receipt shall be issued immediately after the payment entry is completed.

The Original Receipt shall be provided to the customer and a copy shall be retained for company records.

Receipt Cancellation

If a Receipt needs to be cancelled:

  • The reason shall be recorded in writing.
  • Prior approval shall be obtained from the Field Sales Manager (FSM) and Sales Admin Manager.
  • Receipt Cancellation shall be carried out in the ERP system while maintaining the Audit Trail.
  • The cancelled Receipt copy shall be filed with the relevant documents.

Dishonored Cheques

When a Cheque is dishonored:

  • The customer shall be informed immediately.
  • The relevant amount shall be collected in Cash or through a replacement Cheque.
  • New Credit Sales shall be suspended until the outstanding amount is settled.
  • The Customer Account may be placed on Credit Hold where necessary.
  • The matter shall be reported to the Finance Department, FSM, and Sales Admin Manager.
  1. Forms / Records
  • Customer Outstanding Report
  • Accounts Receivable Aging Report
  • Customer Statement
  • Official Receipt
  • Cheque Register
  • Online Payment Slip / Bank Transfer Copy
  • Receipt Cancellation Form
  • Dishonored Cheque Register
  • ERP Payment Entry Report
  1. Safety & Compliance Requirements
  • Outstanding balances and Credit Limits shall be checked through the ERP system as mandatory controls.
  • A Receipt shall be issued on the same day that the payment is received.
  • FSM and Sales Admin Manager approval shall be mandatory for Receipt Cancellation.
  • The Finance Department shall conduct Daily Bank Reconciliation.
  • Dishonored Cheques shall be maintained in a separate Register and monitored regularly.
  • Internal Audit reviews relating to Collections and Receipts shall be conducted periodically.

SOP-SMD-011.06 : Daily, Weekly & Monthly Sales Reports Preparation & Handover

  1. Purpose

To establish a standard procedure for Sales Representatives to prepare Daily, Weekly, and Monthly Sales Reports accurately, completely, and within the specified time period, and to hand over the reports and relevant documents to the respective departments.

  1. Scope

This SOP applies to all Marketing Representatives, Sales Representatives, Sales Administration Department, Finance Department, and relevant administrative departments. It covers the Daily Tour, Long Tour, Weekly, and Monthly Sales Reporting processes.

  1. Responsibility

Role

Responsibilities

Marketing Representative / Sales Representative

Prepare Daily, Weekly, and Monthly Reports and collect all required supporting documents.

Sales Admin Officer

Check received reports, reconcile them with ERP data, and update relevant records.

Field Sales Manager (FSM)

Monitor the completeness of reports and ensure timely submission.

Sales Admin Manager

Supervise the Reporting Process, prepare Management Reports, and distribute reports to the relevant departments.

Security Department

Receive submitted documents, record them in the relevant Register, and securely forward them to the Sales Administration Department.

  1. Procedure

Step

Description

Time for Preparing Daily Sales Reports

Daily Sales Reports shall be prepared as follows:

  • Day Tour – At the end of the day.
  • Long Tour – Before commencing the new Tour.

The Sales Representative shall prepare the reports immediately after completing all transactions for the day.

Documents to be Included with the Daily Report

The following documents shall be included with the Daily Report:

  • Invoice Copies – arranged in sequence.
  • Receipt Copies – arranged according to Invoice sequence.
  • Shop Return Notes – where applicable.
  • Goods Received Note (GRN) Copy.
  • Stock Return Final Sheet.
  • Bank Deposit Slips.
  • ERP End of Day Report.
  • Other relevant Supporting Documents.

Points to be Considered When Preparing Documents

  • Arrange all Invoice Numbers in sequence.

  • Ensure that the relevant Shop Return Note is attached for Shop Returns.
  • Include the GRN Copy.
  • Complete the Stock Return Sheet.
  • Attach the Bank Deposit Slip.
  • Attach the ERP End of Day Report.
  • Recheck all documents to ensure that they are complete.

Document Handover

  • After checking all documents, they shall be properly filed and arranged.

  • The Security Department shall record receipt of the documents in the relevant Register.
  • Thereafter, the documents shall be forwarded to the Sales Administration Department.

Weekly Reports

On a weekly basis, the Sales Administration Department shall prepare the following reports and submit them to the Field Sales Manager and Management:

  • Weekly Sales Summary
  • Sales by Territory
  • Sales by Representative
  • Collection Summary
  • Outstanding Summary
  • Product-wise Sales Report
  • Customer-wise Sales Report
  • Shop Return Summary

Monthly Reports

At the end of each month, the Sales Administration Department shall prepare the following reports and submit them to Senior Management and the Finance Department within the specified deadline:

  • Monthly Sales Performance Report
  • Monthly Collection Report
  • Product Sales Analysis
  • Customer Sales Analysis
  • Sales Representative Performance Report
  • Outstanding & Aging Report
  • Shop Return Analysis
  • Bank Collection Summary
  • KPI Dashboard
  1. Forms / Records
  • Daily Sales Report
  • Weekly Sales Report
  • Monthly Sales Report
  • Invoice Copies
  • Receipt Copies
  • Shop Return Note
  • Goods Received Note (GRN)
  • Stock Return Final Sheet
  • Bank Deposit Slip
  • ERP End of Day Report
  • Sales Summary Report
  • Collection Summary Report
  • Security Document Handover Register
  1. Safety & Compliance Requirements
  • The Sales Admin Officer shall verify that all Reports reconcile with ERP data.
  • Invoice, Receipt, Bank Deposit Slip, and other Supporting Documents shall be completely included.
  • The Security Department shall maintain a Document Handover Register.
  • Weekly and Monthly Reports shall be submitted to Management only after approval by the Sales Admin Manager.
  • All Reports and Supporting Documents shall be filed and retained in accordance with the company's Document Retention Policy.

SOP-SMD-011.07 : Shop Return (Return of Products from Customers / Shops)

  1. Purpose

To establish a standard procedure for products returned by customers, ensure that Shop Returns are accepted only for valid business reasons, maintain accurate revenue and stock control, and ensure accurate recording in the ERP system.

  1. Scope

This procedure applies to Sales Representatives, Field Sales Managers (FSM), Sales Administration Department, Finished Goods Department, Quality Assurance (QA) Department, Finance Department, and all Shop Return transactions.

  1. Responsibility

Role

Responsibilities

Sales Representative

Confirm the reason for the Shop Return, inspect the products, and complete the required documentation.

Field Sales Manager (FSM)

SOP-SMD-011.20 : Product Quality Maintenance & Market Selling Procedure

  1. Purpose

To establish a standardized procedure for selling products to the market while maintaining product quality and food safety standards, enhancing customer satisfaction, minimizing product complaints, and protecting and strengthening the company’s brand reputation.

  1. Scope

This SOP applies to Sales Representatives, Field Sales Managers, Sales Administration Department, Transport Department, and all employees involved in the transportation, distribution, and selling of products through Freezer Trucks.

  1. Responsibility

Role

Responsibilities

Sales Representative

Maintaining product quality, food safety, and FIFO practices.

Field Sales Manager (FSM)

Supervising field operations, monitoring product quality, and implementing corrective actions.

Sales Administration Department

Transport Department

Maintaining Freezer Trucks in proper condition and ensuring the cold chain is maintained.

Monitoring product quality standards and food safety requirements.

  1. Procedure

Step

Description

Product Quality Objectives

The following objectives shall be achieved when selling products:

  • Maintain Product Quality.
  • Maintain Food Safety Standards.
  • Improve Customer Satisfaction.
  • Minimize Product Complaints.
  • Protect and strengthen Brand Reputation.

Vehicle & Stock Inspection Before Departure

Before accepting the stock and dispatching the Freezer Truck, the following shall be verified:

  • Ensure that products are received in the correct quantity and in good condition.
  • Ensure that the Freezer Truck is operating at the required temperature.
  • Maintain the Temperature Monitoring Book properly.
  • Check that Vehicle Door Seals and the Refrigeration System are functioning properly.
  • Ensure that Stock Loading Documents and Delivery Documents are properly matched.

Product Handling During Transportation

The following procedures shall be strictly followed during transportation:

  • Employees shall not enter the Freezer Truck with muddy, dusty, or contaminated footwear.
  • Products shall be properly and safely arranged.
  • The FIFO (First In – First Out) method shall be followed when handling and unloading products.
  • Throwing, dragging, or dropping products shall be avoided.
  • Products shall be handled carefully to prevent damage to packets and packaging.
  • Walking on or placing heavy objects on top of products is prohibited.
  • Products shall not be placed on dirty or contaminated surfaces.
  • Freezer Truck doors shall not be opened unnecessarily in order to maintain the cold chain.
  • The required temperature range for products shall be maintained throughout transportation.

Product Handling at Customer Location

The following procedures shall be followed at the customer’s outlet:

  • Products shall be displayed according to the FIFO method.
  • Company products shall be displayed neatly and separately from competing products.

  • A sample shall be submitted to the QA Department when required.
  • Corrective and Preventive Actions (CAPA) shall be implemented.

  • The outstanding amount must be settled or an alternative payment arrangement must be obtained within three (03) days from the date of dishonor.
  • Once payment is confirmed, the Sales Administration Department must be informed.

Requesting the Original Cheque

  • If the Original Dishonored Cheque is required, a request must be submitted to the Sales Administration Department via email.
  • Once the request is approved, the original cheque must be handed over to the relevant officer as soon as possible.

Handover and Documentation of Original Cheque

  • A Document Handover Form must be used when handing over the Original Cheque.
  • The signature and date of the receiving officer must be recorded.

Implementation of Credit Control Policy

  • If the Dishonored Cheque has not been settled within seven (07) days, action must be taken according to the Credit Control Policy.
  • Where necessary, the following actions may be taken:
  • Suspension of Credit Facility.
  • Temporary suspension of new orders.
  • Issuing an official written notification.
  • Referral for legal action.
