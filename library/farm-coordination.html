FCO-SOP-01 Farm Supplies, Employee Dry-Ration Coordination

1. Purpose

To ensure that dry food items required for farm operations and employee meals are identified, procured, delivered, documented, and accounted for accurately and on time.

2. Scope

Applies to Werallugama, Grower, Hatchery, Layer units and all commercial farms, including Farm Coordination, farm managers, Purchasing, Stores, Transport and Accounts.

3. Responsibility

Role

Responsibility

Farm Manager/Supervisor

Monitor requirements and submit complete, authorized requests by email.

Farm Coordination Department

Review requests, coordinate purchasing and delivery, collect supporting documents, and submit to the Finance Department.

Purchasing Department

Source and purchase approved goods in accordance with procurement controls.

Stores/Logistics

Dispatch goods to the correct farm and retain proof of delivery.

Accounts Department

Verify invoices and supporting records and process accounting/payment entries.

4. Definitions

Term

Meaning

Farm Request

Authorized list of operational goods and employee dry-ration items required by a farm.

Dry Rations

Non-perishable food ingredients used to prepare employee meals.

GRN

Goods Received Note confirming the quantity and condition received.

5. Required Records & References

  • Authorized farm request/email
  • Purchase requisition
  • Supplier invoice

6. Procedure

Steps

Description

Requirement Monitoring

Farm Manager/Supervisor checks operational stock and employee meal requirements and identifies replenishment needs.

Request Submission

Submit an itemized email request showing item, specification, quantity, required date, farm, stock on hand, and authorization.

Review and Clarification

Farm Coordination checks completeness, urgency, duplication, and budget/approval status; clarify exceptions with the requesting farm.

Procurement Coordination

Forward the approved requirement to Purchasing and follow up on sourcing, purchase approval, and availability.

Delivery Arrangement

Coordinate Stores/Logistics to deliver the correct goods to the named farm by the required date, giving priority to meal supplies and critical operational items.

Receipt Verification

Farm staff count and inspect goods, record shortages/damage on the delivery document, and issue a GRN or signed receipt.

Invoice Collection

Obtain the supplier invoice and farm receiving documents and reconcile item, quantity, price, and destination.

Accounts Submission and Filing

Submit the complete document pack to Accounts Department.

7.Safety and Compliance Requirements

  • Do not purchase or dispatch unapproved, incorrectly specified, or expired items.
  • Maintain food hygiene and separate employee food supplies from chemicals, medicines, and contaminated farm materials.
  • Use approved suppliers and comply with procurement, authorization, and segregation-of-duty requirements.

8.Non-Conformance Handling

  • Incomplete/unauthorized request: return for correction before procurement.
  • Late delivery of a critical item: escalate to the Coordinating Manager and arrange an approved alternative.
  • Missing invoice/GRN: hold payment processing and recover the required evidence.

FCO-SOP-02 Parent Stock Day-Old Chick Purchase from Pussalla (Local Supplier)

1. Purpose

To establish a controlled process for planning, approving, purchasing, paying for, collecting, and receiving parent stock day-old chicks from Pussalla while protecting biosecurity and chick welfare.

2. Scope

Applies to breeder farm management, Farm Coordination, the Managing Director, Purchasing, Accounts, Logistics, and staff involved in the annual parent stock input programme.

3. Responsibility

Role

Responsibility

Breeder Farm Manager

Prepare the annual input schedule and confirm farm readiness.

Managing Director

Review and approve the annual purchase schedule and material changes.

Farm Coordination Department

Communicate with the supplier, verify confirmations and invoices, coordinate payments and delivery.

Accounts Department

Arrange advance and balance payments against authorized documents.

Farm Manager/Supervisor

Prepare facilities and receive, count, and inspect chicks.

Logistics Team

Provide a cleaned, disinfected/fumigated vehicle and transport chicks safely.

4. Definitions

Term

Meaning

Parent Stock/DO Chicks

Day-old breeder chicks purchased for parent-bird production

Input Schedule

Annual plan stating batch quantity and intended placement date.

Pro-forma Invoice

Supplier invoice used to confirm quantity, price, payment terms, and delivery date

Biosecurity Clearance

Evidence that the transport vehicle and receiving farm satisfy required sanitation controls

5. Required Records & References

  • MD-approved annual input schedule
  • Supplier confirmation
  • Pro-forma and tax invoices
  • Purchase order and payment records
  • Vehicle cleaning/disinfection or fumigation record
  • Delivery note, chick count, and farm receiving/GRN record.

6. Procedure

Steps

Description

Annual Schedule Preparation

Breeder Farm Manager prepares the annual input schedule, normally covering the five planned parent-stock batches, with quantity and placement dates.

Approval

Submit the schedule to the Managing Director and retain the approved version.

Supplier Confirmation

Send the approved schedule to Pussalla and obtain written confirmation of batch quantities and dates.

Invoice Verification

Obtain the pro-forma invoice and verify breed/strain, sex, quantity, unit price, taxes, delivery point, date, and payment terms against the approved schedule.

Farm Notification

Provide confirmed delivery details to the Farm Manager/Supervisor so brooding facilities, staff, utilities, feed, and vaccines are ready.

Payment

Arrange the agreed advance and balance payments through Accounts; where the agreed structure is 10% advance and 90% balance, ensure completion by the supplier due date, normally at least 20 days before collection.

Transport Biosecurity

Schedule vehicle cleaning, disinfection/fumigation and verification before collection; do not use a vehicle without clearance.

Collection and Transport

Collect chicks from the supplier hatchery, verify dispatch documents and chick count, and transport under suitable ventilation, temperature, and journey-time controls.

Farm Receipt and Closure

Count and assess chicks on arrival, record mortality/shortage/condition, issue the receiving record/GRN, notify exceptions, and file the complete purchase pack.

7.Safety and Compliance Requirements

  • Apply farm and supplier biosecurity rules throughout collection and delivery.
  • Handle chick boxes carefully and maintain suitable ventilation, temperature, loading density, and minimum transit delay.
  • Release payments only against approved schedules and verified invoices

8.Non-Conformance Handling

  • Schedule or invoice mismatch: stop processing and obtain written correction and approval.
  • Supplier delay: escalate and revise farm readiness and placement plans.
  • Payment delay: notify the Finance Manager and Coordinating Manager immediately.
  • Biosecurity failure: halt collection until corrective sanitation is completed and verified.
  • Mortality, shortage, wrong strain/sex, or poor chick condition: document at receipt, notify the supplier, and pursue replacement/credit under agreed terms.

9. Annexures

  • Spent Parent Bird Sale Reconciliation Form
  • Monthly Spent Bird Sales Report

FCO-SOP-03 Trade and Environmental Licence Renewal and File Maintenance

1. Purpose

To ensure each farm maintains valid trade and environmental licences by completing applications, inspections, payments, renewals, and record retention before expiry.

2. Scope

Applies to all farms requiring licences or permits from local authorities, the Central Environmental Authority or other responsible government bodies.

3. Responsibility

Role

Responsibility

Farm Coordination Department

Maintain the licence register, prepare submissions, coordinate inspections and payments, and file renewed licences.

Farm Manager

Provide site information, correct compliance gaps, and support inspections.

Managing Director

Approve applications, declarations, and required payments.

Accounts Department

Process authorized licence fees and retain payment records.

Government/Regulatory Officer

Inspect the premises and communicate statutory requirements or licence decisions.

4. Definitions

Term

Meaning

Trade Licence

Local-authority authorization to conduct the farm business.

Environmental Licence

Permit/approval applicable to activities with environmental impacts

Licence Register

Controlled list of licence number, authority, issue date, expiry date, status, and responsible person.

5. Required Records & References

  • Licence register and reminder log
  • Previous licence and conditions
  • Application and supporting documents
  • MD approval
  • Inspection notice/report
  • Fee voucher and receipt
  • Renewed licence and correspondence

6. Procedure

Steps

Description

Register Review

Review the licence register anually and begin renewal early enough to meet the authority lead time.

Requirement Confirmation

Confirm the current form, fee, supporting documents, inspection requirements, and any changed legal conditions with the relevant authority.

Application Preparation

Complete the application accurately and obtain farm information and attachments.

Internal Approval

Obtain the Managing Director’s approval/signature and payment authorization.

Submission

Submit the application to the responsible government authority before expiry and retain acknowledgement.

Inspection Coordination

Coordinate the official visit with the Farm Manager and make records, facilities, and responsible staff available.

Corrective Action

Record inspection observations, assign actions, collect evidence, and close requirements within the stated deadline.

Fee Payment and Licence Collection

Pay the approved statutory fee, collect the renewed licence, and verify licence details and conditions.

Display and Filing

Provide a copy for display/use at the farm, update the register, and file the full renewal pack.

7.Safety and Compliance Requirements

  • Never operate knowingly under an expired licence without top level management.
  • Make no false declaration or unauthorized payment to any official.
  • Track and close environmental conditions, waste controls, and inspection findings.

8.Non-Conformance Handling

  • Expiry risk: escalate at once and submit an urgent renewal plan.
  • Incomplete/rejected application: correct and resubmit with evidence.
  • Adverse inspection finding assign corrective action and prevent recurrence before reinspection.
  • Incorrect licence details: request formal correction from the issuing authority.

9. Annexures

  • Trade and Environmental Licence Register

FCO-SOP-04 DAPH Farm Licence Renewal and File Maintenance

1. Purpose

To maintain valid Department of Animal Production and Health (DAPH) licences/registrations for applicable farm operations through timely applications, inspections, corrective actions, and controlled filing.

2. Scope

Applies to breeder, grower, hatchery, layer, and other poultry farm units subject to DAPH licensing or registration.

3. Responsibility

Role

Responsibility

Farm Coordination Department

Monitor expiry dates, prepare and submit applications, coordinate DAPH inspections, and maintain files.

Farm Manager/Veterinary Personnel

Maintain required farm, flock, health, vaccination, biosecurity, and facility records and support inspection.

Managing Director

Approve/sign applications and related payments.

Accounts Department

Process authorized fees.

DAPH Officer

Inspect and decide on licence/registration renewal.

4. Definitions

Term

Meaning

DAPH

Department of Animal Production and Health.

DAPH Licence/Registration

Statutory authorization applicable to the poultry establishment

Corrective Action Evidence

Documents or photographs demonstrating closure of an inspection requirement.

5. Required Records & References

  • DAPH licence register
  • Current/previous licence
  • Application and attachments
  • Farm health, production, vaccination and biosecurity records
  • Inspection report
  • Corrective action evidence
  • Payment receipt and renewed licence

6. Procedure

Steps

Description

Expiry Monitoring

Review the DAPH licence register anually and start renewal before the applicable lead time.

Document Collection

Obtain the current application requirements and compile farm, flock, veterinary, biosecurity, facility, and ownership information.

Application and Approval

Complete the forms and obtain Managing Director approval/signature.

Submission

Submit the application and supporting records to the relevant DAPH office before expiry; retain acknowledgement.

Inspection Preparation

Notify the Farm Manager/Veterinary personnel and verify sanitation, flock records, medicine/vaccine records, mortality disposal, and biosecurity controls.

Inspection Coordination

Facilitate the DAPH inspection and formally record all observations and required actions.

Corrective Action Closure

Coordinate corrective actions and submit evidence or arrange reinspection as required.

Fee and Licence

Arrange authorized payment, obtain the renewed licence/registration, and check all details and conditions.

Update and file

Update the register, provide the farm copy, and retain the complete renewal file.

7.Safety and Compliance Requirements

  • Comply with DAPH animal-health, welfare, disease-reporting, medicine, vaccination, and biosecurity requirements.
  • Do not conceal disease events, mortality, or inspection findings.
  • Restrict visitors and inspection movement according to farm biosecurity protocols.

8.Non-Conformance Handling

  • Late application/expiry: escalate and obtain formal guidance from DAPH.
  • Failed inspection: prepare a dated corrective-action plan and verify closure.
  • Missing farm or veterinary records: reconstruct from primary evidence and strengthen record controls.
  • Licence conditions not implemented: notify management and suspend the affected activity where legally or operationally required.

9. Annexures

  • DAPH Licence Register

FCO-SOP-05 Submission of Monthly Poultry Evaluation Reports to DAPH

1. Purpose

To compile, validate, authorize, and submit accurate monthly poultry production/evaluation data to DAPH within the required deadline.

2. Scope

Applies to monthly reports obtained from Grower, Hatchery, Layer and other breeding-farm units, including chick production and other data required by DAPH.

3. Responsibility

Role

Responsibility

Farm Managers/Unit Heads

Prepare complete monthly source reports and certify their accuracy.

Farm Coordination Department

Collect, validate, consolidate, submit, and archive the report.

Farm Operations Manager

Review exceptions and approve/certify the consolidated report where required.

DAPH

Receive the report and request clarifications or corrections.

4. Definitions

Term

Meaning

Monthly Evaluation Report

DAPH-required return covering poultry population, production, chick output, mortality, health or other prescribed information.

Reporting Cut-off

Last date/time by which farm data must reach Farm Coordination.

5. Required Records & References

  • Farm monthly production/evaluation reports
  • Hatchery chick production records
  • Mortality and flock movement records
  • Consolidated DAPH return
  • Review/approval evidence
  • Submission acknowledgement and correction correspondence

6. Procedure

Steps

Description

Reporting Calendar

Maintain the monthly cut-off and statutory due date and remind each reporting unit before month-end.

Farm Report Collection

Collect signed/authorized monthly reports from Grower, Hatchery, Layer and other applicable units.

Completeness Check

Check reporting period, flock/batch identification, opening and closing balances, production, chick output, mortality, movements, and required signatures.

Reconciliation

Reconcile significant figures with hatchery schedules, farm registers, sales/distribution records, and the previous month; query unexplained variances.

Consolidation

Prepare the prescribed DAPH form or consolidated return without altering approved source data.

Review and Authorization

Obtain Farm Operations Manager or authorized signatory review before submission.

Submission

Submit through the prescribed email, portal, or DAPH office within the deadline and retain acknowledgement.

Correction and Filing

Respond to DAPH queries, clearly mark revised returns, and file the final report with source reports and evidence of submission.

7.Safety and Compliance Requirements

  • Protect confidential farm and production data and use authorized submission channels.
  • Do not estimate or alter statutory figures without documented source and approval.
  • Report notifiable disease information immediately under veterinary/legal protocols rather than waiting for the monthly return.

8.Non-Conformance Handling

  • Late application/expiry: escalate and obtain formal guidance from DAPH.
  • Failed inspection: prepare a dated corrective-action plan and verify closure.
  • Missing farm or veterinary records: reconstruct from primary evidence and strengthen record controls.
  • Licence conditions not implemented: notify management and suspend the affected activity where legally or operationally required.

  • Monthly Report Submission Register

FCO-SOP-06 - CIC Feed Ordering and Distribution - Breeder Farms

1. Purpose

To establish a standardized process for forecasting, ordering, paying for, collecting, distributing, and reconciling CIC feed for breeder farms.

2. Scope

Applies to Grower and Layer breeder farms and Farm Coordination, Purchasing, Accounts, Logistics, and CIC personnel involved in feed supply.

3. Responsibility

Role

Responsibility

Farm Manager/Staff

Submit an authorized weekly feed requirement and verify receipt.

Farm Coordination Department

Validate requirements, submit the CIC order, prepare procurement documents, coordinate collection/delivery, and report monthly.

Purchasing Department

Create/approve the purchase order in accordance with procurement controls.

Accounts Department

Arrange authorized voucher/cheque/payment and process invoice set-off.

Logistics Team

Collect feed from CIC and deliver it to the correct farm under suitable conditions.

4. Definitions:

Term

Meaning

CIC

Feed supplier – Chemical Industrial Ceylon

PO

Purchase Order authorizing procurement

GRN

Goods Received Note confirming receipt.

5. Required Records & References

  • Weekly farm feed schedule/request
  • CIC email order and confirmation3
  • Approved PO
  • Payment voucher/cheque record
  • CIC feed order form
  • Tax invoice and delivery note
  • Farm GRN
  • Monthly feed procurement report

6. Procedure

Steps

Description

Weekly Requirement

Obtain the weekly Grower/Layer feed schedule showing feed type, quantity, farm, stock on hand, consumption basis, and required date.

Requirement Review

Check quantities against flock age, bird numbers, expected consumption, stock balance, and outstanding orders; resolve abnormal variances.

Supplier Order

Email the confirmed order to CIC and obtain written acceptance, availability, collection date, and value.

PO Preparation

Prepare and obtain approval of the PO against the confirmed order.

Payment Arrangement

Submit the PO and supplier documents to Accounts for the approved voucher, cheque, or other payment method.

Collection Documents

Prepare the CIC feed order form and securely hand over the payment/authorization documents to the approved collector.

Feed Collection

At CIC, verify feed type, batch, manufacture/expiry date, bag count, weight, packaging condition, invoice, and delivery note before departure.

Farm Delivery and GRN

Deliver to the named farm; farm staff inspect and count the feed, record exceptions, and issue the GRN.

Invoice Set-Off and Monthly Report

Reconcile PO, invoice, delivery note, GRN, and payment; submit the document pack and monthly feed report to Accounts.

7.Safety and Compliance Requirements

  • Protect feed from rain, contamination, pests, chemicals, and damaged packaging during transport.
  • Apply FIFO/FEFO and reject expired, wet, contaminated, or incorrectly labelled feed.
  • Secure cheques/payment instruments and maintain authorized custody

8.Non-Conformance Handling

  • Order mismatch/unavailable feed: obtain corrected confirmation and revise the distribution plan.
  • Delayed payment or collection: escalate before farm stock reaches the minimum level.
  • Shortage, damaged bags, or quality concern: segregate affected feed, note it on receiving records, notify CIC, and seek replacement/credit.
  • Missing PO/invoice/GRN: do not complete set-off until the document pack is complete.

9. Annexures

  • Weekly Breeder Feed Requirement Schedule
  • Monthly CIC Feed Reconciliation Report

FCO-SOP-07 Broiler Day-Old Chick Allocation, Distribution and Excess-Chick Sales

1. Purpose

To ensure systematic, timely, biosecure, and accurately documented allocation of broiler day-old chicks to company commercial farms and buy-back farms, including controlled sale and accounting of excess chicks.

2. Scope

Applies to Hatchery, Commercial Farm Operations, Buy-Back/Extension personnel, Farm Coordination, Logistics, Sales, and Accounts.

3. Responsibility

Role

Responsibility

Hatchery Unit

Issue monthly and weekly production forecasts and final hatch quantities.

Farm Coordination Department

Prepare schedules, coordinate distribution and excess sales, and reconcile records.

Commercial Farm Operations Manager

Review and approve allocation schedules.

Buy-Back/Extension Manager

Confirm eligible farmers, placement capacity, and receipt.

Logistics Team

Provide biosecure transport and complete route/delivery documents.

Sales and Accounts

Authorize excess-chick sales, issue sales/receipt records, and account for revenue.

4. Definitions:

Term

Meaning

DO Chicks

Day-old broiler chicks.

Buy-Back Farm

Contract/partner farm receiving chicks under the approved programme.

Allocation Schedule

Approved farm-wise quantity and delivery plan.

Allocation Schedule

Saleable chicks remaining after approved company and buy-back allocations

5. Required Records & References

  • Monthly and weekly hatchery production schedules
  • Approved farm/buy-back placement plan
  • Dispatch and route sheet
  • Delivery/receiving record
  • Excess-chick sales invoice and receipt
  • Monthly chick distribution and sales report

6. Procedure

Steps

Description

Monthly Forecast

Receive the Hatchery monthly chick-production schedule and current placement requirements from Commercial Farm Operations and Buy-Back/Extension.

Monthly Allocation Plan

Prepare the farm-wise allocation considering house capacity, downtime, farmer eligibility, placement date, distance, and expected hatch quantity.

Approval

Obtain Commercial Farm Operations Manager approval and communicate the controlled schedule.

Weekly Confirmation

Receive the weekly/final hatch forecast and update the delivery plan, normally for Monday and Thursday distributions.

Readiness Check

Confirm each receiving farm’s house preparation, brooding, feed, water, utilities, biosecurity, labour, and delivery contact.

Dispatch Plan

Prepare route, vehicle, driver, chick-box quantities, biosecurity clearance, dispatch documents, and emergency contacts.

Distribution

Count and dispatch chicks to company and approved buy-back farms; obtain signed farm receiving records with count, time, and arrival condition.

Variance Reconciliation

Reconcile hatch quantity, dispatched quantity, farm receipts, mortality/shortage, and balance immediately.

Excess-Chick Sale

With authorization, coordinate Sales to sell genuine excess chicks and issue invoice/delivery documents and official receipt.

Accounts and Monthly Reporting

Submit receipts/deposit evidence and the monthly chick distribution and sales reconciliation to Accounts.

7.Safety and Compliance Requirements

  • Use cleaned/disinfected chick vehicles and crates/boxes and apply hatchery/farm biosecurity.
  • Maintain ventilation, suitable temperature, careful handling, and minimum transport time.
  • Allocate only to approved farms with verified readiness and capacity.

8.Non-Conformance Handling

  • Forecast/allocation mismatch: revise and reapprove the schedule before dispatch.
  • Farm not ready: suspend/reallocate chicks with Operations approval.
  • Delivery delay, mortality, shortage, or poor condition: record immediately, notify responsible managers, and investigate.
  • Unsold excess: escalate promptly for an approved welfare-safe disposition.
  • Missing receipt/sales record: hold reconciliation and recover evidence.

9. Annexures

  • Weekly Chick Allocation and Route Plan
  • Monthly Chick Distribution and Sales Reconciliation

FCO-SOP-08 Procurement and Payment Coordination for New Farm Construction

1. Purpose

To ensure materials, equipment, services, contractor payments, site support, GRNs, and finance documents for new farm construction are authorized, supplied, verified, and recorded accurately.

2. Scope

Applies to new farm construction sites and Farm Coordination, Site Control/Project personnel, Construction Management, Purchasing, Stores/Logistics, Accounts, contractors, and suppliers

3. Responsibility

Role

Responsibility

Site Control Officer/Project Manager

Plan requirements, verify work and deliveries, certify progress, and maintain site records.

Farm Coordination Department

Coordinate purchasing, deliveries, facilities, document completion, payment routing, and filing.

Purchasing Department

Source and order approved materials/services.

Contractor/Supplier

Deliver compliant work/goods and valid invoices.

Accounts Department

Pay only against authorized and complete supporting documents.

Managing Director/Authorized Approver

Approve budgets, orders, variations, and payments within authority limits.

4. Definitions:

Term

Meaning

Site Request

Authorized requirement for construction goods, equipment, services, or site facilities.

GRN

Evidence that specified goods were received in acceptable quantity/condition

Progress Certificate

Authorized confirmation of completed work supporting contractor payment

Variation

Approved change to scope, quantity, specification, price, or time

5. Required Records & References

  • Approved budget/BOQ and programme
  • Site requisition and purchase documents
  • Quotations/PO/contracts
  • Delivery notes and inspection/GRN
  • Invoice, payment voucher and approval
  • Variation approval
  • Payment and document register

6. Procedure

Steps

Description

Requirement Planning

Site Control Officer identifies materials, services, facilities, required date, specification, BOQ/budget reference, and delivery location.

Request and Approval

Submit an authorized site request; obtain approval for budget availability, procurement route, and any variation.

Procurement Coordination

Forward approved requirements to Purchasing and monitor supplier/contractor confirmation.

Delivery and Site Verification

Coordinate access and unloading; site staff verify quantity, specification, condition, and supporting delivery documents.

GRN and Record Update

Issue GRN only for accepted goods and update the site material/asset register; quarantine and document rejected items.

Service/Work Certification

For contractor work, obtain measured and authorized progress/service certification before invoice processing.

Payment Pack

Match contract/PO, approved variation, invoice, GRN or progress certificate, statutory deductions, prior payments, and approval.

Accounts Submission

Send the complete payment pack to Accounts and track payment without bypassing authorization controls.

Site Support Coordination

Coordinate utilities, transport, accommodation, security, temporary facilities, and other approved support with relevant departments.

File and reconcile

Maintain supplier/contractor payment files and reconcile commitments, receipts, work certified, and payments periodically.

7.Safety and Compliance Requirements

  • Materials and work must meet approved drawings, specifications, HSE requirements, and inspection controls.
  • Do not certify goods not received or work not measured/completed.
  • Control site access, lifting, unloading, electrical work, work at height, and contractor permits through the responsible site/HSE procedures.

8.Non-Conformance Handling

  • Unauthorized request/variation: stop procurement or work and obtain approval.
  • Non-compliant material/work: reject or quarantine, issue a non-conformance, and obtain correction/replacement.
  • Invoice/GRN/progress mismatch: hold payment and reconcile.
  • Urgent site delay: escalate with documented impact and approved mitigation.
  • Duplicate/overpayment risk: block payment and investigate against the payment register.

9. Annexures

  • Construction Site Request
  • Contractor Payment Document File
