SOP–GS–27.01 Material Master & Item Classification

Scope

    • Electrical and Electronic Items

SOP–GS–27-08-Stock Adjustment & Disposal

Purpose

To ensure that all inventory discrepancies are formally adjusted with authorization, and that obsolete, damaged, expired, or unusable materials are disposed of in a controlled, safe, and compliant manner, preventing misuse, financial loss, and environmental risk.

Scope

This SOP applies to:

  • All inventory stock adjustments arising from:
    • Physical verification differences
    • Damage, spillage, or deterioration
    • System posting errors
  • Disposal of:
    • Obsolete materials
    • Expired materials
    • Damaged or contaminated materials
    • Scrap and waste materials

Responsibility

Role

Responsibility

Store Officer

Identify discrepancies and damaged stock

Store Manager

Review and recommend adjustment/disposal

QA

Approve disposal of food-contact items

Environmental and safety compliance

Finance / Accounts

Financial approval and write-off

ERP / Inventory Officer

System posting

Internal Auditor

Verification, compliance review and write-off

Definitions

Term

Meaning

Stock Adjustment

Authorized correction of inventory quantity

Disposal

Controlled removal of unusable stock

Scrap

Material with recoverable value

NCR

Non-Conformance Report

ERP

Enterprise Resource Planning System

Procedure

A. Identification of Adjustment or Disposal Requirement

Stock adjustment or disposal may arise due to:

  • Physical count variance
  • Expiry or shelf-life breach
  • Damage or contamination
  • Obsolescence
  • ERP posting errors

B. Stock Adjustment Process

  • Storekeeper prepares Stock Adjustment Request detailing:
    • Item code & description
    • System quantity vs physical quantity
    • Reason for variance
  • Store Manager reviews and forwards for approval.
  • Finance approves value impact.
  • ERP adjustment posted with audit trail.

C. Identification of Disposal Items

  • Items identified for disposal are:
    • Labelled “For Disposal”
    • Segregated from usable stock
  • Disposal type identified:
    • Return to supplier
    • Scrap sale
    • Destruction
    • Authorized waste disposal

D. Disposal Approval

  • Disposal proposal prepared with:
    • Item details
    • Quantity
    • Reason
    • Disposal method
  • Approval obtained from:
    • Store Manager
    • QA (if food-contact)
    • Safety / EHS
    • Finance

E. Disposal Execution

  • Disposal carried out as per approved method.
  • For destruction:
    • Witnessed by Store & QA representatives
  • For scrap sale:
    • Conducted as per procurement policy
  • Disposal records completed.

F. ERP & Financial Posting

  • Stock removed from ERP.
  • Financial write-off or scrap income recorded.
  • Disposal transaction linked to approval documents

Required Records / References

  • Stock Adjustment Request Form
  • Disposal Authorization Form
  • Scrap Register
  • ERP Adjustment Logs
  • NCR Records
  • Environmental Regulations

Safety & Compliance Requirements

  • PPE mandatory during disposal handling
  • Chemical disposal as per MSDS
  • No disposal without approval
  • Environmental and waste management regulations complied
  • Food safety compliance strictly followed.

Non-Conformance Handling
