01.Financial Reporting

A. Monthly Closing Procedure (Month-End Closing)

01. Purpose

To ensure all financial transactions are completely recorded, verified, adjusted, and finalized within the approved reporting timeline so that accurate financial statements can be prepared.

02. Scope

This procedure applies to all financial activities of Imo Chicken and Agro (Pvt) Ltd including:

• Sales and Revenue
• Purchases and Expenses
• Cash and Bank Transactions
• Payroll
• Inventory
• Fixed Assets
• Taxation
• General Ledger adjustments

03. Responsibility

Role

Responsibility

Accounts Assistant

Record transactions and update ERP system

Accounts Executive

Perform reconciliations and verify schedules

Assistant Finance Manager

Review postings, adjustments, and financial accuracy

Finance Manager

Final review and approval of month-end closing

Department Heads

Confirm operational data (sales, stock, etc.)

Internal Auditor

Ensure compliance and internal control review

04. Definitions

Term

Meaning

Month-End Closing

Process of finalizing accounting records for a financial month

Cut-Off

Ensuring transactions are recorded in the correct accounting period

Accruals

Expenses incurred but not yet recorded

Reconciliation

Matching ledger balances with supporting records

Trial Balance

Summary of all ledger accounts before financial statements

05. Required Records / References

a. Sales Invoices and Credit Notes
b. Purchase Invoices and GRNs
c. Cash Book and Bank Statements
d. Payroll Reports
e. Inventory Reports
f. Fixed Asset Register (FAR)
g. Journal Vouchers
h. Tax Schedules (VAT/SSCL/APIT)
i. ERP Reports and Trial Balance
j. Reconciliation Statements

06. Procedures

A. Transaction Cut-Off

  1. Ensure all transactions for the month are recorded in the ERP system.
  2. Confirm no pending invoices, receipts, or payments remain unrecorded.
  3. Verify correct posting period for all transactions.

B. Revenue & Sales Review

  1. Ensure all sales invoices are recorded.
  2. Verify delivery notes and invoice completeness.
  3. Review discounts, returns, and credit notes.
  4. Reconcile sales ledger with customer balances.

C. Purchase & Expense Review

  1. Ensure all supplier invoices are recorded.
  2. Match invoices with PO and GRN.
  3. Verify expense completeness and accuracy.
  4. Reconcile supplier balances.

D. Cash & Bank Reconciliation

  1. Reconcile cash book with physical cash.
  2. Complete bank reconciliations.
  3. Investigate outstanding deposits and payments.
  4. Record bank charges and interest.

E. Payroll Posting

  1. Verify payroll computation.
  2. Ensure EPF, ETF, PAYE/APIT are correctly recorded.
  3. Post payroll journal entries into ERP.
  4. Reconcile payroll control accounts.

F. Inventory Accounting

  1. Perform physical stock reconciliation.
  2. Compare ERP stock with physical stock.
  3. Record stock adjustments and variances.
  4. Update inventory valuation.

G. Fixed Asset Accounting

  1. Verify additions, disposals, and transfers.
  2. Calculate monthly depreciation.
  3. Post depreciation entries.
  4. Update Fixed Asset Register.

H. Journal Entries & Adjustments

  1. Record accruals and prepayments.
  2. Record provisions (audit, bad debts, bonuses, etc.).
  3. Post correcting and reclassification entries.
  4. Ensure proper approvals for all journal entries.

I. Tax Reconciliation

  1. Reconcile VAT, SSCL, APIT accounts.
  2. Verify tax schedules with ERP balances.
  3. Ensure statutory compliance and accuracy.

J. Financial Review

  1. Review Trial Balance for unusual balances.
  2. Compare current month with:
    • Previous month
    • Budget
    • Prior year
  3. Investigate major variances.

K. Final Closing Approval

  1. Ensure all reconciliations are completed and reviewed.
  2. Obtain Assistant Finance Manager and Finance Manager approval.
  3. Lock accounting period in ERP system.
  4. No further entries allowed after closure without authorization.

07. Safety & Compliance Requirements

a. All transactions must be fully recorded before closing.
b. No unauthorized post-closing entries are allowed.
c. Segregation of duties must be maintained.
d. All reconciliations must be reviewed and approved.
e. ERP system must be locked after month-end closure.
f. All supporting documents must be properly filed.

08. Non-Conformance Handling

a. Missing transactions must be identified before closing.
b. Unreconciled balances must be escalated to management.
c. Unauthorized adjustments shall be strictly prohibited.
d. Repeated delays in closing shall be reported to management.
e. Any suspicious entries shall be investigated immediately.

04.Definitions

Term

Meaning

Final Accounts

The complete set of annual financial statements prepared at the end of the financial year, including all required statements and disclosures in accordance with SLFRS/LKAS/IFRS.

Financial Statements

A complete set of financial reports comprising the Statement of Financial Position, Statement of Profit or Loss and Other Comprehensive Income, Statement of Changes in Equity, Statement of Cash Flows, and Notes to the Financial Statements.

Trial Balance

A listing of all general ledger account balances used as the basis for preparing the final accounts and financial statements.

Adjusting Journal Entry

A journal entry recorded at the end of the reporting period to recognize accrued, deferred, estimated, or corrected transactions before preparing the financial statements.

The primary accounting record containing all financial transactions classified by account and used for preparing the trial balance and financial statements.

Reconciliation

The process of comparing accounting records with supporting documents or external records to ensure completeness, accuracy, and consistency.

An omission, error, or incorrect disclosure that could reasonably influence the decisions of users of the financial statements.

  9. Incorporate audit adjustments (if applicable).

D. Bank Reconciliation Procedure

01.Purpose

To ensure that all bank transactions recorded in the company’s accounting records are accurately matched with the bank statement, and to identify, investigate, and correct any differences in a timely manner.

02.Scope

This procedure applies to all bank accounts maintained by Imo Chicken and Agro (Pvt) Ltd, including current accounts, savings accounts, loan accounts, and any other banking facilities used for business transactions.

03.Responsibility

Role

Responsibility

Prepare bank reconciliation statements and update ERP

Accounts Executive

Review reconciliation and verify supporting documents

Assistant Finance Manager

Approve reconciliation and post adjustment entries

Finance Manager

Monitor bank control accounts and approve exceptions

Internal Auditor

Conduct periodic independent verification

Cashier (if applicable)

Provide cash deposit and payment supporting documents

04.Definitions

Term

Meaning

Bank Reconciliation

Process of matching bank statement with company cash book

Cash Book

Internal record of all bank receipts and payments

Bank Statement

Official record provided by the bank

Outstanding Cheques

Issued cheques not yet cleared by bank

Deposits in Transit

Deposits recorded but not yet reflected in bank statement

Reconciliation Item

Difference between bank and book balance

05.Required Records / References

a. Bank Statements
b. Cash Book / ERP Bank Ledger
c. Payment Vouchers
d. Receipt Records
e. Bank Deposit Slips
f. Cheque Registers
g. Journal Vouchers
h. Loan Statements
i. Previous Month Bank Reconciliation Statement

06.Procedure

Step 1 – Obtain Bank Statements

  1. Collect monthly bank statement from all company bank accounts.
  2. Ensure statement completeness for the full month period.

Step 2 – Update Cash Book

  1. Ensure all bank transactions are recorded in ERP/Cash Book.
  2. Include:
    1. Deposits
    2. Withdrawals
    3. Bank charges
    4. Interest income
    5. Loan repayments

Step 3 – Match Transactions

  1. Match each bank statement entry with cash book records.
  2. Identify:
    1. Outstanding cheques
    2. Deposits in transit
    3. Missing entries
    4. Duplicate postings

Step 4 – Identify Differences

  1. List all reconciliation items such as:
    1. Bank charges not recorded
    2. Interest not recorded
    3. Direct debits/credits
    4. Timing differences

Step 5 – Prepare Reconciliation Statement

  1. Prepare Bank Reconciliation Statement showing:
    1. Bank balance as per statement
    2. Adjustments
    3. Adjusted bank balance
    4. Cash book balance
    5. Reconciliation differences

Step 6 – Review and Verification

  1. Accounts Executive reviews reconciliation.
  2. Accountant verifies supporting documents.
  3. Ensure all reconciling items are valid and explained.

Step 7 – Adjustments Posting

  1. Pass necessary journal entries for:
    • Bank charges
    • Interest income
    • Corrections
  2. Ensure proper approval before posting.

Step 8 – Approval

  1. Submit reconciliation for Accountant approval.
  2. Finance Manager reviews significant variances.
  3. Final approved reconciliation is filed.

07.Safety & Compliance Requirements

a. Bank reconciliation must be prepared monthly without delay.
b. All reconciling items must be supported with valid documents.
c. Unauthorized adjustments are strictly prohibited.
d. Segregation of duties must be maintained.
e. ERP postings must be accurately updated and approved.
f. All reconciliation reports must be retained for audit purposes.
g. Differences must be investigated promptly and resolved.

08.Non-Conformance Handling

a. Unreconciled differences must be reported to the Accountant immediately.
b. Repeated unreconciled items shall be escalated to Finance Manager.
c. Missing or unexplained bank entries must be investigated.
d. Unauthorized journal entries must be corrected and reviewed.
e. Delays in reconciliation completion may result in corrective action.
f. Suspected fraud or manipulation must be escalated for audit review.

  6. Any material misstatement identified after reporting shall be investigated and corrected promptly.

  2. Material discrepancies shall be reported immediately to the Finance Manager.
  3. Incorrect journal entries shall be corrected through approved adjustment journals.
  4. Delays in report preparation shall be documented, and corrective actions implemented.
  5. Repeated reporting deficiencies shall be reviewed during Finance Department performance meetings.

  • Errors identified in prior period balances shall be corrected through approved accounting adjustments in accordance with applicable accounting standards.

  • Material discrepancies identified during internal or external audits shall be investigated, documented, and corrected promptly.
