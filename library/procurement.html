PRC028 - 01 Procurement Planning & Requirement Identification

1. Purpose

To establish a structured and systematic process for identifying, planning, and approving procurement requirements to ensure uninterrupted operations, cost efficiency, regulatory compliance, and timely availability of materials, services, and resources for the poultry farm & Processing Plant.

2. Scope

This SOP applies to all departments involved in planning, requesting, approving, and coordinating procurement of:

  • Feed and feed ingredients
  • Fuel & Rubriquant
  • Day-old chicks (DOCs)
  • Veterinary medicines, chemicals and vaccines
  • Packaging materials
  • Spare parts, consumables, and services
  • Capital items and infrastructure-related purchases.

Prioritization & Scheduling

(Purchasing Executive)

An organization cannot buy everything at the same time. Therefore, it uses 4 key factors to decide which purchases must happen first:

  • Impact on Biosecurity: Highest priority is given to items that prevent diseases or protect the safety of animals and products (e.g., disinfectants, vaccines, bio-cleaners).
  • Continuity of Production: Top priority is given to essential materials needed to keep daily operations running smoothly without stopping (e.g., animal feed, raw manufacturing ingredients).
  • Statutory Deadlines: Government regulations, taxes, license renewals, or legally mandated payments have strict due dates that cannot be missed, so they are prioritized.
  • Lead Time and Supplier Capacity: "Lead time" is the time it takes from placing an order to receiving the goods. If an item takes a long time to arrive (e.g., imported goods) or if the supplier has limited production capacity, these orders must be scheduled well in advance.

  • Logistics & Transport Department: To arrange trucks, shipping, and labor required to move and unload the goods.

To establish a standardized, controlled process for the procurement, handling, and management of veterinary drugs, chemicals, disinfectants, and sanitizing agents in poultry operations; ensuring precise disease control, optimum production standards, regulatory compliance, and environmental protection, while guaranteeing the delivery of safe, drug-residue-free meat to the consumer market.

2. Scope

This SOP applies to

The entire procurement process—from the initial email requisition to final supplier payment—for all routine and emergency drugs and chemical agents used in

  • Poultry Health and Disease Management.

  • Water, waste, and pest control.

8.Safety & Compliance Requirements

  • Purchase drugs and vitamins ONLY from DAPH-approved or GMP- certified suppliers

9. Non-Conformance Handling

  • Damaged/expired materials: Reject immediately and issue NCR.
  • Incorrect batch or quantity: Hold stock; inform supplier and procurement.
  • Invoice discrepancies: Resolve before sending to finance.
  • Quality failure:
  • Quarantine material
  • Run QA retesting
  • Inform supplier and initiate claims if necessary
  • All non-conformances must be recorded in the Non-Conformance Register and followed with CAPA actions.

10. Safety & Non-Conformance Handling

  • Quality Inspection: feed should be strictly inspected before acceptance to ensure that it is not damp or expired
  • Rejecting Order: If the order is short delivered, contaminated, damaged, or fails quality tests, the location must halt unloading and immediately draft a Non-Conformance Report (NCR
  • Supplier Notification: Report any defect or shortage to the supplier within 24 hours with photographic/video evidence to arrange an immediate replacement or a Credit Note.

  • Submit the physical Documents (PO, GRN, and Invoices) to the Purchasing Manager for final review and validation.

Account Settlement & Filing

(Purchasing Executive)

  • Forward the validated Invoices, GRN, and PO (along with any balance cash from the IOU method) to the Accounts Department to close the advance ledger.
  • Log the transaction and obtain the necessary signatures in the 'Document Handover Record Book' to confirm receipt by Accounts. (PRC028/REG/2026/001)
  • Organize and file copies of all completed transaction documents securely in the department archives.

8. Safety & Non-Conformance Handling

  • Financial Safety: Hand-carried IOU cash and supplier banking credentials must be managed with extreme caution. The Purchasing Assistant is personally accountable for cash safety until it is converted to goods or returned.
  • Mismatches Handling: If any discrepancy in quantity, price, or specification is discovered between the PO, GRN, and Invoice, the process must be halted immediately. Escalate the issue to the Purchasing Manager and contact the supplier or requesting department for rectification.
  • Unauthorized Purchases: No purchase or supplier bank transfer shall be executed without explicit MD approval. Unapproved invoices will be rejected by the Accounts Department.
  • Incomplete Documentation: Invoices or GRNs lacking official stamps, security verification, or authorized signatures must not be sent to the Accounts Department or filed. They must be returned to the source for proper completion

PRC 028 - 10 Vehicle Insurance Management and Insurance Claim Settlement

1. Purpose

To establish a systematic process for obtaining and managing vehicle insurance, ensuring legal compliance and maximizing financial protection by securing appropriate compensation in the event of accidents, damage, theft, or other insured incidents.

2. Scope

This SOP applies to:

  • All vehicles owned by the Company.
  • All organizational vehicles operated from regional offices, departments, or external locations.
  • Leased or rented vehicles used for organizational purposes where insurance responsibility rests with the organization according to contractual agreements.

3. Definitions

Term

Definition

Vehicle Insurance

Insurance coverage obtained to provide legal and financial protection for a vehicle.

Comprehensive Insurance

Insurance coverage that includes third-party liabilities, vehicle damage, theft, natural disasters, and other covered risks.

Third-Party Insurance

Insurance coverage providing compensation only for damages caused to third parties.

Claim

A request made to an insurance company for compensation following an insured event.

NCB

No Claim Bonus

Vehicle Custodian

The officer responsible for the custody and management of a vehicle.

Cover Note

A temporary insurance certificate issued until the original insurance policy is received.

CR Book

Certificate of Registration of the vehicle.

IOU (I Owe You)

An internal document used to obtain temporary funds from the Company.

4. Responsibility

Role

Responsibility

Insurance Officer

  • Assist the Licensing Administration Officer and drivers in ensuring vehicle insurance is renewed before the expiry date.
  • Monitor insurance compliance and coordinate insurance-related activities.

Insurance Officer

  • Manage the annual insurance procurement process.
  • Maintain insurance records and documentation.
  • Coordinate with insurance companies.
  • Maintain a comprehensive vehicle insurance register.
  • Monitor insurance expiry dates.
  • Initiate insurance renewal actions in a timely manner.
  • Obtain quotations from insurance providers.
  • Secure management approvals for insurance procurement

Branch / Department Heads

  • Monitor the operational condition of vehicles under their responsibility.
  • Verify the validity of insurance policies.
  • Ensure vehicle information is regularly updated.
  • Immediately report accidents, damages, or thefts to Insurance Officer.
  • Assist with insurance inspections and claim investigations.

Finance Department

  • Process and settle insurance-related payments.

Drivers

  • Safeguard insurance certificates and related documents.
  • Immediately report accidents to the insurance company and Insurance Division.
  • Maintain vehicle registration certificates and insurance certificates securely.
  • Cooperate with insurance investigations and claim procedures
  • Notify relevant officials at least 7 days before the expiration of all licenses.

5. Required Records

  • Vehicle Details & Excel Sheet
  • Insurance Policy Copy
  • Premium Payment Records
  • Accidents Reports
  • Insurance Claim Files
  • Handing Over Taking Records Book
  • Cash Vouchers and IOU Records

Steps

Description

  • Policy Number

  • Third-Party Vehicle Insurance

Newly acquired vehicles shall be included in the vehicle Details Excel sheet.

Verification of Renewal Lists

  • The prepared renewal lists shall be verified with vehicle drivers and responsible officers to ensure accuracy before processing.

Vehicle Valuation and Loss History Review

  • Review current vehicle market values with senior management.
  • Verify No Claim Bonus (NCB) eligibility.
  • Review leasing company requirements where applicable.
  • Prepare and obtain approval for the final insurance schedule

Obtain quotations for comprehensive insurance coverage.
