Safety & Compliance Requirements

  • Proper storage to prevent moisture damage in dry goods.
  • Daily freshness checks to avoid foodborne illness.
  • Hygienic handling and cold chain maintenance for fish/meat.
  • Compliance with food safety standards
  • Immediate removal of spoiled or contaminated items.
  • Vehicles must meet hygiene standards before unloading.
  • Proper equipment must be used for heavy loads to prevent injury/damage.
  • Expired, spoiled, or unhygienic items must not be accepted.
  • Staff must wear appropriate PPE (gloves, masks, uniforms) during handling.
  • Meals must meet nutritional standards and dietary requirements.
  • Accurate records must be maintained to ensure accountability.
  • Waste disposal must follow environmental guidelines.
  • Use only approved disinfectants and cleaning materials.
  • Maintain safe food temperatures during service.
  • Conduct medical examinations for kitchen employees periodically.

8. Non-Conformance Handling

Non-Conformance

Action

Unloading Issues

Damaged goods documented and reported; corrective action taken.

Quality Failures

Expired/spoiled items immediately rejected; supplier notified.

Menu Issues

If meals are not balanced or cost-effective, review and adjust menu plans.

Strength Count Errors

Incorrect counts leading to over/under rationing must be corrected immediately

Ration Distribution Problems

Misallocation or wastage must be documented and investigated.

Waste Disposal Failures

Improper disposal must be reported; corrective measures enforced.

Cleaning Failures
