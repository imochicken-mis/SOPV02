To establish a standardized, controlled, and auditable procedure for employee entry and exit.

Scope

This SOP applies to all:

  • permanent employees
  • security personnel stationed at entry and exit points
  • shift times, including nights, weekends, and holidays
  • Employees Personal Vehicles

Carry ID, comply with security instructions and get permission for personal vehicles from host department

SEC-SOP-04-Freezer truck entry and exit Control

Purpose

To establish a standardized procedure for the entry, unloading, loading, weighing, parking, documentation, and exit of freezer trucks arriving at and departing from the poultry processing plant.

Scope

This procedure applies to:

  • All freezer trucks returning from sales routes.
  • Trucks carrying sales returns or shop returns.
  • Trucks being loaded with finished goods for dispatch.
  • All distributors
  • All security personnel, weighbridge operators, dispatch supervisors, and freezer drivers.

Responsibility

Role

Responsibility

Security Officer

Enty & exit freezer checks and record in freezer truck entry & exit log book, seal inspection, vehicle inspection at the gate and record all weights in loading sheet at the loading bay

OIC

Documentation, access control, key control of freezers and give the parking instructions.

CSO

Scalation special cases, compliance monitoring and record verification.

Weighbridge Operator

Record first and second weights, verify the documentation, print weighbridge tickets

FG Dispatch Supervisor

Verify return items, Scale weight against the loading sheet and oversee the finished goods handling at loading and unloading.

Driver / Helper

Provide documents, cooperate with checks, follow instructions

Annex- sales dep driver helper working instruction

QA Supervisor

Freezer truck and product Temperature checks, product quality verification

Finished Goods Manager

Handling weight differences.

Definitions

Term

Meaning

Return Items

Products coming back from sales routes (shop return, sales return.)

First Weight

Weight recorded upon entry or before loading/unloading

Second Weight

Weight recorded after completion of unloading/loading

Seal Tag

Security seal placed on truck back / side doors for food defence and prevent food fraud.

Loading Sheet

Official document assigned for record product name and the weight of products at dispatch.

Required Records

  • Freezer truck Seal Verification and entry / exit logbook
  • Weighbridge Tickets (1st and 2nd weight)
  • Approved Security Loading Sheets
  • Good Return Note
  • Freezer truck Key Control Register
  • Approved Good Issued Note
  • vehicle in-out book

Procedure

A. ENTRY PROCESS (RETURNING TRUCK FROM SALES)

Steps

Description

1 Gate Arrival

  1. Truck reaches the main gate.
  2. Security officer stops the vehicle and conducts initial checks.

2 Seal Verification

  1. Inspect rear/ side door seal tag.
  2. Record seal number in the Freezer truck Seal Verification and entry / exit logbook
  3. Conduct full inspection If seal is broken/tampered inform QA/dispatch supervisor immediately for further investigation.

3 Vehicle Inspection

  1. Security checks and records in the Freezer truck Seal Verification and entry / exit logbook for Presence of sales return/shop return items and any unauthorized items including,
  • Out Date and time
  • Vehicle No
  • Driver Name
  • Helper Name
  • Route
  • Sales Representative
  • Temperature reading
  • Odometer reading
  • Seal Number
  • In time and date

4 Refuelling

  1. Security directs truck to fuel station.
  2. Fill fuel full tank before the first weight at loading and unloading.
  3. Security officer record the details in the Fuel Issued Register

5 Initial Weighing (First Weight)

  1. Driver brings vehicle to weighbridge.
  2. Operator print the first weight in the ticket and send the ticket to finished goods supervisor.

6 Check for Return Items

  1. If NO, returns → go to parking.
  2. If YES, returns → proceed to unloading bay.

7 Unloading Returns

  1. Open seal under supervision of finished goods supervisor
  2. Unload return items.
  3. Verify the Quantity
  4. Record in the Return Sheet.

8 Post-Unloading Weighing (Second Weight)

  1. Truck returns to the weighbridge.
  2. Operator prints the second weight in the ticket and get the signatures of driver and the security officer.
  3. Send the ticket to finished goods supervisor.

9 Parking

  1. Security directs truck to parking.
  2. Driver hands over the key to the OIC.
  3. OIC record the details in the Freezer truck Key Control Register

B. LOADING FINISHED GOODS

1.Night Driver Arrival at Security

  1. Night Driver arrives at gate.
  2. Requests vehicle key and OIC handover key to the driver

2 Initial Weighing (First Weight)

  1. Driver brings vehicle to weighbridge.
  2. Operator saves the first weight in the system.
  3. Applicable for dealers

3 Loading

  1. Truck drives to loading bay.
  2. Loading team loads finished goods as per the loading list under supervision of security officer and FG loading supervisor.
  3. Verify the loading items and record the weight in the loading sheet.
  4. No unauthorized persons allowed in loading/unloading areas.
  5. Applicable for dealers

4 Post-loading Weighing (Second Weight)

1. Truck returns to the weighbridge.

2. Operator prints the second weight in the relevant freezer truck first weight saved ticket and get the signatures of driver and the security officer.

3. Security officer record weight bridge ticket number, first weight, second weight, net weight, GIN number, number of bags, weight difference of finished good scale and the weight bridge in the Finished Goods dispatch book.

4.Applicable for dealers and record details in the dealer dispatch record book.

5 Weight difference handling

      1. Inform to the finished goods manager and get the corrections according to the

SOP 4.8 Finished Goods Weighing Process

2. Applicable for dealers.

6 Parking

1.Security directs truck to parking.

2.Driver hands over the key to the OIC

3. OIC hang the keys in the key holder.

4.Applicable for dealers

B. EXIT PROCESS (AFTER LOADING FINISHED GOODS)

1 Route Driver Arrival at Security

1.Driver arrives at gate.

2.Requests vehicle key.

3.OIC handover key to the driver after record in the Freezer truck Key Control Register

  1. Documentation
  1. OIC record the information of the Freezer truck Seal Verification and entry / exit logbook.
  2. OIC issue the first copy of GIN to the driver after putting the signature of the driver.
  3. OIC send the duplicate to the finished good supervisor.

6 Final Security Clearance

  1. OIC record all the information of the Freezer truck Seal Verification and entry / exit logbook including,
  • Seal tag number,
  • Vehicle No,
  • driver details,
  • Helper information,
  • Route details,
  • Temperature reading,
  • Meter reading,
  • Loading list validation
  1. Applicable for dealers and record details in the dealer dispatch record book.
  1. Exit
  1. Security officer Confirms all documents are complete, record in the vehicle in-out book, opens gate, allows vehicle to exit.
  2. Applicable for dealers and record details in vehicle in-out book.

SEC-SOP-06- Receive and issue fuel at the fuel station.

1.Purpose

To establish a standardized procedure for receiving and issuing fuel stocks and documentation, which is essential for the smooth operation of all vehicles, machineries and generators

2.Scope

This SOP applies to receiving fuel, loading the storage tank and issuing vehicles, machinery and generators.

3.Roles & Responsibilities

Role

Responsibility

Always take steps to order and receive fuel at the right time and without shortage.

Check whether the ordered stocks have been received

Security Officer

Properly receive, store and issue fuel and record the details.

Driver

Give vehicle details and verify the amount taken.

Weighbridge Operator

Verify the bulk

4. Definitions

Meaning

First Weight

Weight before the unloading of fuel.

Seal Tag

Security seal affixed to vehicle to prevent tampering

Second Weight

Weight after unloading of fuel

5.Required reports

  • Fuel receiving and issuing log
  • Weighbridge weight record sheet (first and second weight)
  • fuel stock purchasing bill.
  • Fuel request note

6. Procedure

Fuel Stocks Receiving

Step

Description

Receiving fuel stocks and first weighing

      1. The vehicle carrying the stock arrives at the main

security room on the day the fuel stock is ordered.

      1. Check the vehicle carrying any unauthorized materials.
      2. Record details in the Fuel receiving and issuing log.
      3. Direct the vehicle carrying the fuel stock to the weighbridge for the first weighing and record and save in the system.
      4. Direct the vehicle into the shed.

Unloading the fuel stock

  1. Before unloading the fuel stock, the OIC checks the seal tag of the fuel tank and records in the Fuel receiving and issuing log.
  2. Remove the seal and give permission to unload.
  3. Direct the vehicle into the weighbridge.

Second Weighing

  1. Take the print of the second weight.
  2. Sign on the ticket by driver and security officer.
  3. Verify the quantity of the bill with the ticket.
  4. Send it to the purchasing department.

Issuance of fuel

Verification of fuel Stock Balance

  1. Monitor the stock balances in every morning before issuing fuel to the vehicles.
  2. Record in the Fuel receiving and issuing log

Fuel Release

  1. Check the approval of the fuel request note of the plant or any other location.
  2. Check that the meter is 0, before dispensing fuel to the vehicle.
  3. Dispense according to the fuel request note, verify requested amount and subtract it from the fuel stock.
  4. Record in the Fuel receiving and issuing log

Inform the reorder level

1. Whenever the fuel stock decreases by 50% according to the fuel stock book, the fuel stock order informs the Fuel in-charge officer.

Non-compliant handling

        • Broken seals on the fuel tank
        • mismatch weight of the Fuel Stock
        • Use of flammable items near the fuel station
        • Smoking
  • Use mobile phones near the fuel station

Stop operation and report immediately to CSO, Fuel in-charge officer and QAD

Purpose

Identifying and directing the contractors to the designated area of the relevant departments and facilitate conducting the transparent contract operations in processing plant

Scope

This SOP applies to,

  • All contractors and their workers,
  • Materials, machineries, equipment and tools
  • Vehicles

Role

Ensuring individuals do not enter unauthorized zones and comply with company rules.

Host Department

Checking for service issues.
