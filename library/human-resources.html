13.01 – Recruitment Selection & Employee Hiring

1. Purpose

To establish a structured, fair, transparent, and compliant recruitment and selection process to ensure the right employees are selected to support poultry, agricultural, and operational excellence.

2. Scope

All permanent, contract, temporary, and casual employees.

3. Responsibility

Role

Responsibility

Department Head

Raising Manpower request

HR Manager

Recruitment governance & compliance

HR Assistant

Recruitment governance & documentation

Managing Director

Approval of key recruitments

4. Definitions

Term

Meaning

Manpower Requisition

Formal request to recruit a new employee

Manpower Budget

A plan that shows the number of employees required and their salary costs for a department or company.

CV

A document that shows a person’s education, work experience, skills, and achievements

Job Description (JD)

Document defining duties and responsibilities

Shortlisting

Selecting suitable candidates for an interview

Interview Panel

Group conducting interviews for candidates

Offer Letter

Provisional employment offer

Appointment Letter

Final employment confirmation

5. Required Records & References

  • Manpower Requisition Forms
  • Approved JDs
  • Vacancy advertisement
  • CV database
  • Interview evaluation
  • Offer & appointment letters
  • Employee personal file
  • Job Agencies & Institutions

6. Procedure

Steps

Description

Manpower Planning & Request

1. Identify manpower needs based on operational demand and manpower budget.

2. The Department Head completes the Manpower Requisition Form.

HRD013/FRM/2026/003-Manpower Requisition Form

3. The Department Head attaches the approved Job Description to the requisition.

HRD013/FMT/2026/001-Job Description Template

4. Submit the requisition form for management approval.

Advertisement & Sourcing

1. Select recruitment channels based on job level and urgency.

2. Draft the vacancy advertisement, including:

  • Job Title
  • Company Name and brief introduction
  • Job Location
  • Key Responsibilities / Job Duties
  • Required Qualifications (education, experience, skills)
  • Preferred Skills
  • Employment Type
  • Salary / Benefits
  • Application Process
  • Closing Date for Applications
  • Contact Details

3. Release advertisements on job portals, social media, the company website, internal notices, and agencies.

4. Track advertisement dates and response status.

CV Collection, Screening & Shortlisting

1. Collect CVs and store them in the CV database.

2. Screen each CV against vacancy requirements.

3. Reject ineligible candidates and record reasons.

4. Shortlist suitable candidates for interviews.

HRD013/FRM/2026/004-CV Shortlisting Form

5. Obtain approval of shortlisted CVs from the Department Head or HR Manager.

6. Confirm final shortlisted candidates for interviews.

Interview Scheduling

1. Fix interview dates with HR, Department Head, or interview panel.

2. Communicate interview schedules to candidates and the security department.

3. Confirm candidate attendance and prepare interview files.

Interview Process

1. Conduct structured interviews by HR, Department Head, or interview panel.

2. Assess candidates’ technical, behavioral, and cultural fit.

3. Complete Interview Evaluation Forms.

HRD013/FRM/2026/005-Staff Interview Evaluation Form

4. Conduct labour interviews based on the relevant job role and the suitability of each candidate, and select accordingly.

5. Recommend suitable candidates for selection.

Final Selection

1. Consolidate panel recommendations.

2. Confirm final candidate selection by Department Head / HR Manager / Interview Panel.

3. Obtain management approval where required.

Reference & Background Check

1. Contact previous employers and referees.

2. Verify employment history and conduct.

3. Document background check results.

Offer Letters Issuance

1. Prepare an offer letter including salary, benefits, probation, and joining date.

HRD013/FMT/2026/002-Offer Letter Template

2. Issue an offer letter to selected candidates.

3. Obtain written acceptance from the candidate.

Medical & Document Verification

1. Collect the medical fitness report from the candidate.

HRD013/FRM/2026/006-Pre-Employment Health Questionnaire for Food Handlers

2. Verify NIC, certificates, and employment documents.

3. Issue clearance confirmation.

Employee Onboarding

Refer to the SOP – Employee Onboarding & Orientation

13.02 – Employee Onboarding & Orientation

1. Purpose

Onboarding & orientation ensure that a new employee feels welcomed, informed, supported, and ready to perform their role confidently within the organization.

2. Scope

This procedure applies to all newly recruited and transferred employees and covers HR, administrative, organizational, safety, and job-related orientation activities from appointment through completion of the initial onboarding period.

3. Responsibility

Role

Responsibility

HR Manager

Overall owner of the onboarding process & Ensure job induction and supervision

HR Executive

Coordinate onboarding activities

HR Assistant

Day-to-day guidance and monitoring

Safety, GMP, and compliance induction & ID cards, other tools

Provide a computer/laptop and accessories

ERP Manager

Provide the System/software logins

New Employee

Follow the onboarding requirements

4. Definitions

Term

Meaning

Onboarding

The complete process of integrating a new employee into the organization.

Orientation

The initial introduction to the organization, policies, and work environment.

OHS (Occupational Health and Safety)

Focuses on protecting the health, safety, and well-being of people at work.

HRIS

A software system used by companies to manage employee-related information and HR processes in one place. (Human Resource Information System.)

Probation Period

Initial employment period used to evaluate employee performance before confirmation.

GMP

Good Manufacturing Practices – quality and hygiene standards followed in production areas.

HSE

Health, Safety & Environment procedures ensuring workplace safety compliance.

Steps

Description

1. HR prepares the Onboarding Checklist prior to the joining date.

HRD013/CEL/2026/001-Employee Onboarding Checklist

  • Creation of Employee ID / Number
  • Payroll and statutory registrations (EPF/ETF, taxes)
  • Attendance and biometric system setup
  • Provision of PPE, uniforms, or other role-specific requirements

3. Inform the respective department of the new employee’s joining date, role, and reporting structure.

1. Employee reports to HR on the joining date.

2. HR completes joining formalities:

  • Verify employee identity and submit documents (ID copy, GS Certificate, Educational Certificate, Police Report, Driving License, Service letters, and Bank Details)

3. Issue and sign the Appointment Letter / Employment Contract.

4. Create the Employee personnel file (physical and/or digital HRIS record).

5. HR explains:

  • Employment terms and conditions
  • Working hours, shifts, and attendance requirements
  • Leave policy and attendance rules

6. Complete and sign the service bonds and control policies for the sales representatives/Field sales managers & supervisors.

Employee Policy Acknowledgment & Record Filing

1. Employee signs acknowledgment forms:

  • Ethics/Conduct declaration

HRD013/POL/2026/001-Code of Ethics

2. HR files all signed documents in the employee record.

  • HR policies and procedures

  • Disciplinary rules

  • Grievance mechanism

4. Company policies

  • Working hours, leave, code of conduct
  • Payroll and benefits

5. Job-related information

  • Role responsibilities
  • Department overview

  • Emergency exits, fire drills
  • Safety procedures and reporting accidents

7. Workplace tour

  • Show departments, restrooms, exits, and first aid points

8. Q&A documentation

  • Let employees ask questions
  • Collect signed forms (policies, safety acknowledgment)

1. HSE / QA conducts mandatory induction covering:

a. Workplace safety rules

b. Emergency procedures

c. PPE usage

d. GMP / hygiene requirements

2. Provide safety PPE for entering the production area.

1. Department Head / Supervisor:

a. Introduces team and reporting structure

b. Explains job responsibilities

  • Check if duties match actual work
  • Verify job title, responsibilities, and reporting line
  • Consult the Department Head / Supervisor
  • Update missing or outdated information
  • Ensure compliance with HR policies and Occupational Health and Safety (if applicable)
  • Get HR/Management approval
  • Save updated version in HR records / HRIS
  • Inform the relevant employee and department

3. Explain workstations, tools, and processes.

System & Access Enablement

1. IT / HR Admin provides:

a. ERP / HRIS access (role-based)

b. Email/system credentials

c. Access cards/gate passes

2. Access rights are logged and approved.

3. Unauthorized access is strictly prohibited.

Initial Training Coordination

1. HR links the employee to:

  • Mandatory training (HSE, GMP)
  • Role-specific training plan
  • Training records updated.
  • Supervisor monitors initial performance.

Onboarding Completion & Handover

1. HR verifies onboarding checklist completion.

HRD013/CEL/2026/001-Employee Onboarding Checklist

2. Onboarding status updated in HRIS or the Onboarding Checklist Excel.

3. Employee formally handed over to the department.

4. Probation tracking initiated.

HRD013/REG/2026/006-Probation Tracking Register

13.04 – Company Item Handover

1. Purpose

To establish a standardized procedure for the issuance, handover, tracking, and return of company property/items to employees in order to ensure accountability, proper record maintenance, and asset control.

2. Scope

All employees in the organization

3. Responsibility

Role

Responsibility

HR Manager

  • Overall owner of the Company Item Handover process.

HR Assistant

  • Coordinate employee onboarding and offboarding item handovers
  • Maintain employee acknowledgment and handover records.

Payroll Executive

  • Deduct the cost of lost, damaged, or unreturned company items from the employee's salary, subject to management approval and company policy.

Department Heads

  • Request required company items for employees based on operational needs
  • Ensure employees properly use company-issued items.

Stores

  • Issue and receive company items.
  • Maintain inventory records and item handover documentation.
  • Verify returned items are in acceptable condition.

Employee

  • Ensure proper use and safekeeping of company property.
  • Return all issued items upon transfer, resignation, termination, or upon company request.

4. Definitions

Term

Meaning

Company Item

Any company-owned item given to an employee for work purposes.

Handover Form

A document used to record the issue or return of company items.

Item Handover Register

A company record containing details of items issued to employees.

Employee Acknowledgment

A signed confirmation by the employee accepting responsibility for the issued items.

5. Required Records & References

  • Company Item Handover Form
  • Asset Register
  • Employee Personal File
  • Final Settlement & Clearance
  • Company Asset Policy

6. Procedure

Steps

Description

Item Issuance

1. Obtain approval from the Managing Director for the items requested by the relevant department.

2. Submit the approved request to the Procurement Department and inform the Human Resources Department.

3. Notify the Human Resources Department once the items are delivered to the Stores Department.

4. Collect the items from the Stores Department through the HR Assistant.

5. Record the following item details:

  • Item description
  • Serial number (if applicable)
  • Condition of the item

HRD013/FRM/2026/010 – Item Handover Sheet

HRD013/POL/2026/003 – Item Handover Policy

6. Complete the Company Item Handover Sheet.

7. Obtain signatures from:

  • Issuing Officer
  • Employee
  • Department Head or Management Representative (if required)

8. Hand over the item to the employee.

9. Update the Asset Register with the issued item details.

Item Return

1. Return all company-issued items upon resignation, transfer, designation change, or termination of employment.

2. Inspect the returned items for damage, defects, or missing parts.

3. Recover the cost of damaged or missing items from the employee's final payment, subject to company policy and management approval.

4. Withhold the employee's final salary payment if payroll for the relevant month has already been processed and record all applicable deductions in the Employee Clearance Form.

HRD013/FRM/2026/011-Final Clearance Form

5. Record the condition of all returned items in the Item Return Handover Form.

6. Obtain acknowledgment signatures from both the employee and the receiving officer.

7. Update the Asset Register to reflect the return of the items.

8. Inform the Payroll Department upon completion of the asset return process to facilitate employee clearance.

Damaged or Lost Items

1. Report any damaged or lost company item immediately to the Department Head and HR Manager.

2. Conduct an investigation where necessary.

3. Recover or charge the replacement cost according to management approval and company policy.

4. Follow the Item Issuance procedure if a replacement item is required.

5. If the item or any other items are required again, follow the item handover procedure described above.

Non-Conformance

Corrective Action

Incorrect or incomplete documentation

Correct records and resubmit proper documents.

Misuse or unauthorized use of items

Disciplinary action as per company policy.

Steps

Description

Leave Recording & Balance Update

2. Update leave balances automatically.

3. Align the attendance system with approved leave records.

4. Flag unauthorized absences for further action.

1. Apply carry-forward limits according to company policy.

2. Allow leave encashment only for employees covered under the Wages Boards.

3. Obtain management approval for leave encashment.

4. Process approved encashment through payroll.

Leave Audit & Monitoring

1. Review leave reports monthly by HR.

2. Analyse high absenteeism trends.

3. Escalate leave misuse or abuse for appropriate action.
