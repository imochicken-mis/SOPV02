SOP-SMD-012.01 : Pricing & Discount Management

  1. Purpose

To establish a controlled and transparent system for setting, approving, maintaining, and monitoring product prices and discounts to ensure profitability, fairness, regulatory compliance, and prevention of revenue leakage.

  1. Scope

This SOP applies to:

  • All Frozen chicken products
  • All customer categories (Standards, Bulk buyers, SMMT, Sub distributor, Dealer)
  • All pricing mechanisms including base price, promotional pricing, special discounts, and credit-related discounts.
  • ERP-based pricing and billing activities
  1. Responsibility

Role

Responsibility

Managing Director

Approve propose price list & Discount structure

Sales Admin Manager

Overall control of pricing & discount process

Field Sales Manager

Propose pricing & discount structures

Sales Admin Executive

Send Approved price list via Mail

ERP / IT Officer

ERP price master setup & access control

Sales Admin Officer

Daily execution & monitoring

Internal Audit

Periodic compliance verification

  1. Definitions

Term

Meaning

Standard Price

Standard selling price before discounts

Discount

Approved reduction from Standard price

Promotional Price

Temporary price for campaigns

Price List

Approved list of item-wise selling prices

Price Override

Manual change during invoicing (restricted)

Margin

Difference between selling price and product cost

SMMT

Small medium modern trade

  1. Required Records & References
  • Approved Price Lists
  1. Procedure

Steps

Description

Price Structure Development

  1. Prepare proposed price structure by Field Sales Manager based on:
  • Market conditions
  • Competitor pricing
  • Target margins
  1. Price structure must include:
  • Item code & description
  • Unit of measure (kg / pack / unit)
  • Standard price
  • Minimum allowable price
  1. Submit proposal price structure to sales admin manager

Price Approval Process

  1. Review proposal price structure by Sales Admin Manager
  • Accuracy
  • Transparency
  1. Submit Reviewed price list to Managing Director
  2. Final approval documented using Price Approval Form. (Annexture 01)
  3. Send approved price list to ERP Dep. Via mail.
  4. No price may be activated without written approval

Discount Approval Procedure

  1. Request Discount by FSM to Sales Admin Manager via mail
  2. Must include:
  • Customer name
  • Item & quantity
  • Discount % / value
  1. Get Approval from Managing Director
  2. Approved discount uploaded to ERP before invoicing.

Price Override Control

  1. Manual price override at invoicing is strictly restricted.
  2. Weekly override report reviewed by Sales Admin Manager.
  3. Repeated overrides trigger investigation.

  • Compliance with company financial policies
  • Compliance with tax and invoicing regulations
  • ERP access control & segregation of duties

  1. Purpose

To establish a structured process for recovering overdue receivables, managing delinquent customers, and initiating legal follow-up where required, while minimizing financial loss and ensuring legal and ethical compliance.

  1. Scope

This SOP applies to:

  • All recovery stages: internal follow-up to legal escalation
  • Sales, Finance, Legal, Management, and ERP teams
  • Cash, cheque, and credit customers
  1. Responsibility

Role

Responsibilities

Team

  1. Procedure

Recovery Process – Stage 1 (Internal Follow -up)

  1. Credit Control Officer review AR aging report Daily
  2. Credit Control Officer Share overdue list to Sales Reps via WhatsApp
  3. Sales Rep contacts customer through phone call or Visit customer
  4. Credit Control officer Update the Commitment date in Excel Report

Annexure 17 – AR Excel Report

Recovery Process – Stage 2 (Escalation)

If payment not received within agreed time:

  1. Credit control officer informs to Field sales manager
  2. Send Sales admin manager Approved reminder letter to customer by credit control officer
  3. Sales admin officer update status of customer credit block in ERP.
  4. Further sales strictly prohibited
  1. Recovery Process – Stage 3 (Final Demand)
  1. Sales admin officer prepares the case file.

Annexture 18 - Case file preparation procedure

  1. If the customer not respond to the remind letter sales admin manager send letter of demand to customer through lawyer.
  2. Settlement plan may be negotiated
  3. Written agreement required
  4. Request to settle his overdue balance, sign written agreement in the present of lawyer.
  1. Recover Process – Stage 4 (Final Step)
  • If the customer breaks the final agreement, under the top management instruction & forward into the court.
  1. Cheque Return & Fraud-Related Recovery
  1. Receive the cheque return note from accounts officer to sales admin officer. Inform the top management & Field sales manager through WhatsApp group by sales admin executive.
  2. Sales admin officer Returned cheque cases flagged immediately.
  3. Sales admin officer Inform to responsible sales rep.
  4. Inform to customer by Sales rep or Field sales manager
  5. Sales rep collect the return cheque amount from the customer.
  6. If not paid the return cheque amount follow the recover process of stage 1,2,3,4.
  7. Sales admin officer blocks the customer if 3 consecutive cheques return under the approval of sales admin manager.
  1. Legal Follow-up Initiation

Legal action initiated when:

  • Overdue >90 days
  • Customer non-responsive
  • Fraud / repeated default identified

Procedure:

  1. Case file prepared with:
  • Invoices
  • PODs
  • Receipts
  • Communication records
  1. Management approval obtained
  2. Legal notice issued through legal advisor
  1. Agree Payment Monitoring
  1. Credit control officer review monthly in ERP
  2. No informal settlements without approval
  3. ERP customer status remains Blocked
  1. Settlement & Closure

On settlement:

  1. Payment verified by sales admin officer through bank statement
  2. Sales rep issue the receipt to the responsible customer.
  3. ERP AR update automatically
  4. Customer unblocked only with approval of sales admin manager.

  • The candidate is being referred to the Marketing Consultant for the second interview.

  • The candidate will be introduced to a Field Sales Manager and assigned to a route.

  • The Sales Admin Manager will also conduct an interview, and if the candidates are found suitable, they will be recruited accordingly.
