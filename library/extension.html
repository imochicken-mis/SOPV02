BBF006/SOP/2026/003: Feed Management

1.Purpose

To establish a controlled and transparent system for the storage, issuance, transportation, and monitoring of inputs—including feed, day-old chicks (DOC), medicines, and supplements—supplied to buy-back farmers, ensuring quality, traceability, and optimal production performance.

2.Scope

This SOP applies to all farm operation under the company’s buy-back arrangement.

  • Feed supply and distribution
  • DOC allocation
  • Veterinary inputs and supplements
  • Inventory control
  • Input usage monitoring

3.Responsibility

Role

Responsibility

Purchasing Manager

Overall coordination of inputs

Feed Mill / Supplier

Quality production & dispatch

Veterinary Officer

Health, medicine & vaccine approval

Storekeeper

Inventory management

Field Officer

Delivery verification & farm coordination

Farmer

Proper usage & reporting

QA / QA Manager

Quality & compliance verification

4.Definitions

Term

Definitions

Input

Feed, DOC, vaccines, medicines, and supplements

DOC

Day-Old Chicks supplied for rearing

FIFO

First-In-First-Out inventory principle

Batch / Lot

Identifiable production batch of feed or DOC

Feed Conversion Ratio (FCR)

Ratio of feed consumed to weight gained

COA

5.Required Records & References

Company Quality Standards

  1. Feed Issue Notes
  2. Delivery Challans
  3. Stock Registers
  4. Feed Quality Reports
  5. Usage & Consumption Reports

6.Procedure

1 Input Planning & Forecasting

  • Extension manager Estimate input requirements based on:
    • Hatchery output
    • Production and live sale requirements
    • Cage capacity
  • Extension manager Prepare weekly/monthly/annual input plans with advice of top management.

Annex - input plan format

2 Feed Procurement & Quality Assurance

  • Procure feed only from approved suppliers / in-house feed mills.
  • If required Maintain the analysis test repots for all the type of process feed
    • Fat
    • Protein
    • Toxin
  • Each lot must include:
    • Batch number
    • Manufacturing & expiry date
    • Nutritional specification
  • Conduct:
    • Filed officer Visual inspection
    • Random lab testing (if required)

3 Storage & Inventory Management

  • Feed mill manager Store feed under dry, ventilated conditions.
  • Follow FIFO principles.
  • Maintain pest control and hygiene.
  • Record stock movement in inventory system.

4 Input Dispatch & Transportation

  • Extension manager Prepare feed delivery Plan daily/ weekly/monthly basis
  • Load feed and inputs as per approved quantities.
  • Generate issue order to farmer

5 Receipt & Verification at Farm

  • Farmer / feed delivery driver and helper verifies:
    • Quantity received
    • Batch number
    • Package integrity
  • Acknowledgement signed and returned to Extension Manager

6 Usage Monitoring & Control

  • Field officer Monitor daily feed consumption vs. standard norms.
  • Track FCR, mortality, and growth trends.
  • If any deviation field officer informs to the extension manager.
  • Managers investigate the deviation and correct

7 Feed Quality Complaints & Corrective Action

  • Farmer reports quality issues immediately to extension manager.
  • Field officer Sample collection for analysis.
  • Inform to feed mill / supplier.

8 Reporting

  • Admin Assistant prepare Batch performance report
  • Report abnormal losses to management.

BBF006/SOP/2026/005: Farm Monitoring & Biosecurity Management

1.Purpose

To establish a systematic approach for continuous farm monitoring and strict biosecurity management to prevent disease introduction, ensure bird health, optimize production performance, and safeguard company assets under the buy-back farming model

2.Scope

This SOP applies to:

  • All contract farms under the buy-back program
  • Company field officers, veterinarians, QA teams, and biosecurity staff
  • All production stages from chick placement to catching

3.Responsibility

Role

Responsibility

Farmer

Daily farm operations & biosecurity enforcement

Field Officer

Monitoring, reporting, farmer guidance

Veterinary Officer

Disease diagnosis, treatment, biosecurity control

QA / Compliance Officer

Audit and verification

Extension manager

continuous farm monitoring and strict biosecurity management

4.Definitions

Term

Definitions

Biosecurity

Measures designed to prevent disease introduction and spread

Flock

Group of birds placed at the same time in one house

Farm Visit

Scheduled or unscheduled technical inspection

High-Risk Zone

Area with elevated disease transmission risk

Quarantine

Isolation of birds suspected of disease

5.Required Records & References

  • Company Quality Standards
  1. Farm Monitoring Log
  2. Biosecurity Checklist
  3. Mortality Records
  4. Vaccination Records
  5. Incident Reports

6.Procedure

Field officers inspect weekly farms for following criteria are followed by farmers and record in Farm inspection Record.

Any non conformities report to the Extension manager.

Any out break occur take action according to the government veterinary guide lines.

1 Farm Biosecurity Zoning

  • Define clean, buffer, and dirty zones.
  • Install footbaths, hand-wash stations, and access control.
  • Display biosecurity signage.

2 Entry & Exit Control

  • Restrict unauthorized access.
  • Maintain visitor & vehicle logbooks.
  • Mandatory PPE for all entrants.
  • Disinfect vehicles and equipment before entry.

3 Routine Farm Monitoring

Field officers conduct schedule field visits and record:

  • health observation
  • Feed and water intake monitoring
  • Mortality tracking
  • Litter condition checks
  • Environmental monitoring

Annex: Field visit record

4 Disease Surveillance & Reporting

  • Observe clinical signs: lethargy, reduced intake, abnormal mortality.
  • Immediate reporting of abnormal trends.
  • Sample collection and lab testing when required.
  • Isolation of affected birds or sheds.

5 Vaccination & Medication Control

  • Administer vaccines as per approved schedule.
  • Record batch numbers, dates, dosage.
  • Ensure cold chain integrity.
  • Monitor withdrawal periods for medications.

6 Biosecurity Audits

  • Conduct periodic audits using standard checklist.
  • Score compliance and issue corrective actions.
  • Re-audit non-compliant farms.

7 Emergency Disease Response

  • Immediate farm lockdown.
  • Notification to veterinary authorities.
  • Controlled culling if required.
  • Safe disposal of carcasses.
  • Enhanced disinfection protocols.

5.8 Training & Awareness

  • Extension Manager conduct Regular training sessions for farmers and workers.
  • Awareness on disease symptoms and reporting.

SOP–BB–12: Farmer Exit, Suspension & Re-Registration

1.Purpose

To define a clear, fair, and controlled process for temporary suspension, permanent exit, or re-registration of buy-back farmers, ensuring business continuity, risk mitigation, legal compliance, and protection of company interests.

2.Scope

This SOP applies to:

  • All buy-back farmers under contract
  • Temporary suspension cases
  • Permanent termination cases
  • Re-onboarding of previously suspended or exited farmers

It covers voluntary exits, performance-based exits, compliance-related suspensions, and reinstatement procedures.

Suspension Procedure

  • Root cause analysis.

  • Farmer acknowledgment required.

  • Review CAPA implementation.
  • Decide: Reinstate / Extend suspension / Terminate.

8.Annex

9.Triggers for Suspension or Exit

1 Grounds for Suspension

  • Repeated biosecurity violations
  • Non-compliance with SOPs
  • Repeated poor performance
  • Temporary inability to operate (disease outbreak, infrastructure failure)

2 Grounds for Exit

  • Repeated critical non-compliance
  • Fraud or data manipulation
  • Contract Violation
  • Failure to meet corrective action deadlines

10.Exit Procedure

1 Voluntary Exit

  • Written notice from farmer.
  • Clearance of outstanding obligations.
  • Final settlement of accounts.

2 Forced Exit

  • Issued due to non-compliance or Violations
  • Formal termination letter.
  • Settlement of dues and recovery.

11.Re-Onboarding Process

1 Eligibility Criteria

  • Completion of cooling-off period.
  • No outstanding dues.
  • Corrective actions fully implemented.
  • Satisfactory inspection results.

2 Re-Assessment Process

  • Full farm audit (same as new onboarding). Re evaluation
  • Approval by management committee.
  • Signing of new agreement.
  • Refer SOP–BB–01 Farmer Registration
