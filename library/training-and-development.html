Standard Operating Procedure

T & D- SOP-01 Training & Development Procedure

  1. Purpose

To establish a systematic process for identifying employee training needs, planning, implementing, evaluating, and maintaining training records. This procedure aims to develop employees' competencies, knowledge, and skills, thereby improving organizational performance and supporting the achievement of business objectives.

  1. Scope

• All permanent, contract, temporary, and casual employees

  1. Responsibility

Role

Responsibility

Department Head

Identify training needs, nominate employees.

HR Manager & Training Development Manager

Training governance & budget approval

HR Executive / HR Assistant

Training coordination, documentation & monitoring

Managing Director

Final approval for training programs

Resource Person

Deliver training programs

  1. Procedure

Steps

Description

Training Need Identification

  1. Monitor employee performance, safety compliance, productivity, and quality standards continuously.
  2. Review customer complaints, audit findings, accidents, near misses, and machinery upgrades.
  3. Identify competency gaps, knowledge deficiencies, behavioral concerns, and unsafe work practices.
  4. Obtain training needs from each departmental heads quarterly or whenever training need arises due to company requirements.
  5. Use Skill Matrix as a tool to analyze training gaps and then to identify training needs.
  6. When a fresher joins as a new hire, a training need identification to be carried out in liaise with particular departmental head.
  7. Record training requirements in the Training Need Identification Form.

HRD013/FRM/2026/016 – Training Need Analysis Form

  1. Document training objectives, affected employees, expected outcomes, and urgency in the Training Need Identification Form.

Submission of Training Requests

  1. Submit completed Training Need Identification Forms quarterly or immediately for urgent requirements based on the company requirements.

HRD013/FRM/2026/016 – Training Need Analysis Form

  1. Review and acknowledge receipt of training requests.
  2. Record each training request in the Training Register and assign a reference number.
    HRD013/REG/2026/007 – Training Register
  3. Maintain training records and supporting documents for audit purposes.

Training Need Analysis (TNA)

  1. Review all submitted training requests.
  2. Categorize training requirements by type (technical, safety, compliance, behavioral, or leadership).
  3. Assign priority levels based on operational risk and urgency.
  4. Identify target employee groups.
  5. Evaluate estimated training costs and duration.
  6. Prepare and submit a consolidated Training Need Analysis Report for management review.

HRD013/FRM/2026/016 – Training Need Analysis Form

Preparation of Annual Training Plan

  1. Prepare the Annual Training Plan based on approved training needs.

HRD013/FMT/2026/017 – Annual Training Plan Template

  1. Record training schedules, timelines, training methods, and proposed trainers.
  2. Define expected performance outcomes.
  3. Document the completed plan for management review.

Approval of Training Plan

  1. Submit the Annual Training Plan for management approval.
  2. Review feasibility, budget requirements, and operational impact.
  3. Grant formal approval in writing.
  4. Conduct training activities only after obtaining documented approval.

Training Budget Allocation

  1. Forward approved training plans for budget allocation.
  2. Allocate department-wise training budgets.
  3. Control costs effectively to ensure that approved expenditure limits are not exceeded.

Training Scheduling & Communication

  1. Implement training activities according to the Annual Training Plan.

HRD013/FMT/2026/017 – Annual Training Plan Template

  1. Communicate training schedules to participants and department heads at least seven (7) days in advance.
  2. Confirm employee participation and availability.
  3. Prepare final attendance lists.

Trainer Logistics Arrangement

  1. Arrange training venues, equipment, and training materials.
  2. Provide safety equipment and PPE where required.
  3. Prepare refreshments and training kits where applicable.
  4. Complete final readiness checks before training commencement.

Training Execution

  1. Conduct training programs according to the approved plan.
  2. Record attendance using approved attendance sheets.

HRD013/FRM/2026/017 – Training Attendance Sheet

  1. Conduct safety briefings before practical sessions.
  2. Encourage active participant involvement during training.

Training Evaluation

  1. A pre-training evaluation to be carried out prior to commence training session.
  2. Complete Training Evaluation Forms after each training session.

HRD013/FRM/2026/018 – Training Evaluation Form

  1. Evaluate participant feedback, training effectiveness, and trainer performance.
  2. Compare evaluation results against expected performance outcomes.
  3. Document evaluation results and recommendations.

Certification & Documentation

  1. Issue participation or competency certificates where applicable.
  2. Update training records in the Training Register or Training Database.

HRD013/REG/2026/007 – Training Register

  1. File training records in employee personnel files.
  2. Retain records in accordance with the document retention procedure.

Post-Training Performance Monitoring

  1. Monitor employee performance for three (3) months following training.
  2. Evaluate:
  • Improvement in job performance.
  • Improvement in work quality.
  • Compliance with procedures and safety requirements.
  • Application of knowledge and skills gained.
  1. Report evaluation results.
  2. Recommend additional training, coaching, or corrective actions where required.

Continuous Improvement Review

  1. Review training effectiveness on a quarterly basis.
  2. Analyze performance gaps and opportunities for improvement.
  3. Submit recommendations for management review.
  4. Revise training plans and programs accordingly.

Development

  1. Identify potential employees during training sessions in order to make them ready for next level assignments focusing on managerial development.
  2. Identify suitable training requirements to make employee improve in order to take up next level assignment.
  3. Analyze such information and assist top management in selecting new hires or internal replacements.
  4. Use above steps to develop competencies of future work force.
  5. Continuously monitor whether employees apply what they learnt from training through monitoring KPI’s and performance appraisals by receiving feedback.
  6. Prevent employees from reverting previous work habits by liaising with all departments.

Training of an intern

  1. A request letter may receive from the particular higher education/training institution whether governmental or private owned.
  2. Request letter is subjected for the top management approval.
  3. Once, top management approves the request letter liaise with particular institution for further administrative actions.
  4. Initiate necessary business communication with the guest organization.
  5. Liaise with HR department for preparation of agreements in relation to intern training.
  6. Intern student would join to the host organization.
  7. A suitable training program is served to the intern student.
  8. Conduct training according to the structured training program in liaise with host department.
  9. On completion of the stipulated training period trainee/s has/have to forward a comprehensive training report to the T and D Manager prior departure from the host organization.
  10. Trainee/s is/are released from the host organization after duly completed handing over duties if only applicable.
  11. Host organization may initiate actions to obtain a Training Feedback Report from the particular higher education organization.

  1. Non- Conformance Handling

Non-Conformance

Corrective Action

Training needs not identified.

Conduct proper Training Need Analysis (TNA)

Training plan not approved.

Obtain management approval before training.

Employees are not informed about the training

Communicate the training schedule in advance

Attendance records missing

Maintain and file attendance sheets properly.

Training evaluation not conducted.

Complete evaluation forms after training

Training records not updated.

Update training database and employee files.

Safety briefing not conducted.

Conduct a mandatory safety briefing before training.

Budget exceeded without approval.

Monitor training budget and obtain approval for changes.

Trainer materials or logistics are unavailable

Arrange all training resources before the program starts.

Post-training performance not monitored.

Monitor employee improvement and report feedback.
