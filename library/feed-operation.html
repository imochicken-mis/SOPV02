SOP 15.1: Drugs, Vitamins and feed formulation related material Purchasing Process – Feed Mill

15.1 Purpose

To define a standardized, transparent, and compliant procedure for the procurement of drugs, vitamins, premixes, and feed-grade additives used in feed formulation.

This ensures quality, traceability, cost control, and regulatory compliance.

Scope

This procedure applies to the procurement of all feed-grade drugs, vitamins, premixes, additives, and feed formulation related materials required for feed production.

It includes planning, supplier selection, ordering, receiving, invoice verification, and payment processing.

Responsibility

Role

Responsibility

Production Manager / Nutritionist

Identify material requirement and initiate purchase plan

Coordinating Manager

Coordinate quotation calling, supplier communication, prepare POs

Managing Director (MD)

Approve procurement decisions and grant special permission for price increases.

Warehouse Supervisor

Receive goods and verify quantities and help to check quality requirements.

QC Officer / Nutritionist

Inspect materials and check Certificates of Analysis

Accounts / Finance Department

Verify invoices and process supplier payments

Definitions

  • PO (Purchase Order): Formal document authorizing the purchase.
  • GRN (Goods Received Note): Document confirming physical receipt of goods.
  • COA (Certificate of Analysis): Quality verification document issued by supplier.
  • Approved Supplier List: List of suppliers authorized based on quality and compliance.
  • Feed-grade Drugs/Vitamins: Additives used in feed formulation.
  • MSDS
  • (RFQ)
  • Comparative statement
  • Electronic approval

Required Records & References

  • Drugs Purchase Plan
  • Supplier Quotations
  • Comparative Statement
  • MD Approval
  • Purchase Orders
  • Delivery Notes
  • COA / MSDS
  • GRN
  • Stock records
  • Invoice copies
  • Payment confirmations
  • DAPH
  • Quality Certificates

Procedure

Steps

Description

Drugs, Vitamins and feed formulation related material Purchase Plan
  • Review production schedules and formulation requirements.
  • Identify required drugs, vitamins, premixes, and additives.
  • Check current warehouse stock levels and expiry dates.
  • Determine quantities needed based on consumption rates and safety stock levels.
  • Prepare the Drugs Purchase Plan and obtain initial approval from the Production Manager or Nutritionist. (Annex ‘G’)
Quotation Call from the Supplier
  • Send Request for Quotation (RFQ) to suppliers from the Approved Supplier List. (Annex ‘E’)
  • RFQ should include:
    • Material name & specification
    • Required quantity
    • Delivery timeline
    • COA requirement
    • Payment terms
  • Collect quotations from approved suppliers.
  • Verify the quotations with:
    • Batch quality
    • Price
    • Expiry date range
    • Packing standards
    • Delivery lead time
  • Prepare a comparative statement. (Annex ‘F’)
MD Approval
  • Submit the comparative quotation summary and purchase justification to the Managing Director.
  • MD reviews comparative statement
    • Cost competitiveness
    • Supplier performance history
    • Budget availability
    • Quality and safety compliance
  • Obtain written or electronic approval before proceeding.
Select the Supplier
  • Based on MD approval, finalize the supplier.
  • Communicate confirmation to the selected supplier.
  • Request the following documents from the supplier,
    • Proforma Invoice
    • COA
    • Delivery confirmation
  • Update in the procurement records with selected supplier details. (Annex ‘E’)
Make the Purchase Order (PO)
  • Feed operation Coordinating manager Create a PO in the ERP or manual system including:
    • Item name & specifications
    • Quantity
    • Unit price
    • Delivery date
    • Payment terms
    • Special instructions
  • Cross-check PO details against the approved quotation.
  • Send PO to the supplier and request acknowledgment.
Goods Received & Invoice Collection
  • Supplier delivers goods to the feed mill warehouse.
  • Warehouse Supervisor checks:
    • Quantity
    • Batch number
    • Expiry date
    • Integrity of supply
  • QC/ Nutritionist verifies material against COA and conducts sampling.
  • Receive commercial invoice from supplier at the time of delivery.
  • Record Drugs & Vitamins stock in register. (Annex – ‘J’
Prepare the GRN (Goods Received Note)
  • Feed operation Coordinating manager Create a GRN in ERP immediately after physical receiving.
  • Ensure GRN captures:
  • PO Number
  • Received quantity
  • Batch/lot number
  • Expiry date
  • Condition of goods
  • GRN must be verified by:
  • Warehouse Supervisor
  • QC Officer / Nutritionist
  • Update stock in ERP or record in the stock update after QA clearance. (Annex ‘J’)
Invoice Verification
  • Coordinating Manager checks the invoice against:
    • PO
    • GRN
    • Approved quotation
  • Verify:
    • Unit price accuracy
    • Quantity
    • Taxes
    • Payment terms
  • Any discrepancies must be communicated to supplier for correction.
Invoice Handover to Finance Department
  • Submit GRN with the invoice to Finance dep.
Payment Details Sent to the Supplier
  • Finance processes payment according to the invoice terms
  • Finance department Prepare the bank slip or transfer confirmation payment proof.
  • Coordinating Manager Send payment confirmation to the supplier.
  • Update procurement records with payment completion status. (Annex ‘H’)

Safety & Compliance Requirements

Purchase Drugs, Vitamins and feed formulation related material only from DAPH-approved or GMP-certified suppliers.

Verify expiry dates and ensure minimum 12 months remaining shelf life

Ensure proper storage conditions: cool, dry, and pest-free.

Follow MSDS guidelines for hazardous additives.

Maintain batch traceability for all additives entering feed production.

Non- Conformance Handling

Damaged/expired materials: Reject immediately and issue NCR.

Incorrect batch or quantity: Hold stock; inform supplier and procurement.

Invoice discrepancies: Resolve before sending to finance.

Quality failure:

  • Quarantine material
  • Run QA retesting
  • Inform supplier and initiate claims

All non-conformances must be recorded in the NC Register. (Annex ‘K’)

SOP 15.2: Import of Raw Materials for Feed Production – Feed Mill

Purpose

To establish a standardized procedure for planning, purchasing, importing, clearing, and receiving raw materials used in feed production.

This SOP ensures accuracy, compliance with import regulations, cost-effectiveness, and traceability throughout the import process.

Scope

This procedure applies to all raw materials imported by the Feed Mill, including maize, soybean meal, MBM, Dry Bakery Meal, Oil and any other feed ingredients.

Responsibility

Role

Responsibility

Coordinating Manager
  • Import planning, supplier selection, approval of purchase terms.
  • Communication with suppliers, arranging quotations, coordinating documentation.
  • Shipment coordination, loading instructions, monitoring vessel schedules.
  • Handling licenses, regulatory approvals, and customs documents.
Chairman / Director

Approving the sales contracts.

Finance Department

Processing advance and final payments.

Warehouse Supervisor

Receiving goods, checking quantities, updating stock.

QC Officer/ Nutrition / Feed Mill Operation Manager

Checking COA, sampling, verifying quality upon receipt.

Coordinating assistant

Coordinate with the finance department and arrange the payments.

Definitions

Term

Meaning

COA (Certificate of Analysis)

Document showing laboratory results of raw materials.

/Proforma Invoice (PI)

Preliminary invoicing used for import approval and payment processing.

Consignee

The party receiving the goods.

Import Licenses

Regulatory approvals required DAPH, Import Control, NPQS.

Import Costing

Calculation of total landed cost including CIF value, bank charges, freight, duty, transport, and overheads.

RFQ

Request for Quotation

FOB/CIF/CFR

FOB – Free on Board

CIF – Cost, Insurance and Freight

CFR – Cost and Freight

SC

Sales Contract

FCL, BL/ AWB / TT

FCL – Full Container Load

BL – Bill of Lading

AWB – Air Way Bill

TT – Tele Graphic Transfer

Required Records & References

  • Import Plan
  • Quotations
  • PI and Sales Contract
  • Payment records (TT slips)
  • Insurance documents
  • Import licenses
  • Shipping and customs documentation
  • Receiving reports
  • Stock book entries
  • QA laboratory reports
  • Import costing sheet
  • Sri Lanka Customs, Import & Export Control, and DAPH regulations feed safety standards

Procedure

Steps

Description

Import Plan
  1. Coordination manager Review production forecasts and stock levels.
  2. Determine required raw materials, quantities, and timelines.
  3. Prepare an Import Plan and obtain approval. (Annex ‘G’)
Quotation Call and Select the Supplier
  1. Coordinating manager Send RFQ (Request for Quotation) to approved suppliers through the E-mail.
  2. Collect quotations including price, quality specs, packing details, delivery terms (FOB/CIF/CFR), and shipment schedule.
  3. Coordinating manager Compare received quotations against value and terms and select the supplier.
  4. Confirm selected supplier formally via email.
Collect Sales Contract & Proforma Invoice
  1. Coordinating manager Request PI and Sales Contract from selected supplier via email
  2. Verify terms: price, quantity, shipment period, payment terms, packing, quality specs against the PI and sales contract.
Obtain Approval
  1. Submit Sales Contract and PI to MD for approval.
  2. Send approved Sales Contract and PI to selected supplier.
Obtain Insurance
  1. Suppliers need to insure shipment.
  2. If contract or Incoterms require local insurance, arrange insurance with approved insurer.
  3. File insurance documents in import file.
Complete Relevant Import Licenses
  1. Request, coordinate and complete the Import Control License, DAPH approval, and NPQS certificate before the loading of vessel. Import licenses request and renewal procedure. (Annex ‘L’)
  2. Submit Import Control License, DAPH approval, and NPQS certificate to the selected supplier.
  3. Maintain copies of approved licenses in the Import File.
Advance Payment Processing
  1. Coordinating assistant Submit PI, contract, and bank request form to Finance.
  2. Finance processes the advance payment according to the supplier request.
  3. Coordinating manager Forward payment confirmation/slip to supplier.
  4. Update in payment detail register with payment details.
Provide Loading Instructions
  1. Issue written loading instructions to supplier including:
  • Packing details
  • FCL’s
  • Destination port
  • Documentation requirements
  1. Confirm supplier acknowledges instructions.
Renewal of Expired Import Licenses

Annex ‘L’ – Import licenses request and renewal procedure.

Obtain & Check Copy Documents from Supplier

Suppliers provide the following copy of documents through email before original documents are couriered:

  • Commercial Invoice
  • Packing List
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Bill
  • COA
  • Origin Certificate
  • Weight Certificate
  • Testing Reported.
  1. Review documents for accuracy and compliance.
  2. Check shipment details (HS code, weight, quantity) or any other corrections.
  3. Flag discrepancies to supplier for correction.
Goods Arrive at Consignee Port
  1. Monitor vessel arrival and port updates.
  2. Notify clearing agent and provide shipment clearance documents.
Final Payment Processing
  1. For contracts with split payments (E.g., 30% advance, 70% on arrival):
  • Submit final invoice to Finance
  • Finance processes final TT
  1. Send payment confirmation to supplier.
Collect Original Documents
  1. Receive original documents via courier from supplier:
  • B/L
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Packing List
  • COA
  • Origin Certificate
  • Other certificates
  1. Hand over documents to clearing agent.
Port Clearance & Transportation by Clearing agent
  1. Clearing agent processes customs clearance.
  2. Arrange transport from port to warehouse.
  3. Ensure goods are insured during transit.
  4. Warehouse Supervisor receives goods and matches with documents.
Add Goods to Stock Book
  1. Feed Mill Operation Manager verify weight-, quantity- CI, and condition with COA.
  2. Coordinating Manager Update stock book and ERP system after QA approval.
Import Costing
  1. Coordinating manager Collect all cost components:
  • CIF price
  • Bank charges
  • Freight and port charges
  • Duty and levies
  • Transport
  • Clearing costs
  • Overheads
  1. Prepare Import Costing Sheet. (Annex ‘M’)
  2. Submit costing to management for review and record-keeping.

Safety & Compliance Requirements

  • Follow Sri Lanka Customs, Import & Export Control, and DAPH regulations.
  • Ensure storage and handling comply with safety standards.
  • Use PPE when handling raw materials at receiving.
  • Maintain food/feed safety standards (GMP/HACCP).

Non-Conformance Handling

Incorrect documentation: Notify supplier immediately; hold clearance until corrected.

Damaged or contaminated cargo:

  • Mark as “HOLD”
  • QA investigation
  • Supplier/insurance claim according to terms and conditions.

Delayed clearance due to missing documents:

  • Inform management
  • Implement corrective actions

Payment discrepancies:

  • Finance to notify procurement
  • Stop further payments until resolved

Annex ‘L’ – Import licenses request and renewal procedure

SOP 15.3: Renewal of Acreage Tax, Business Tax Certificate, DAPH License & Environment Protection License (EPL) – Feed Mill

Purpose

Renewal of Acreage Tax

To maintain legal ownership and usage rights of the land on which the feed mill operates and ensures the facility avoids penalties or disputes over land taxation.

Business Tax Certificate

To ensure timely renewal of the Business Tax Certificate for the poultry feed mill and maintain legal authorization for business operations and avoid penalties or disruptions.

DAPH License

To guarantee that poultry feed production meets national standards for quality, safety, and animal health and provides official authorization to manufacture and distribute feed.

Environment Protection License (EPL)

To ensure the feed mill operates in an environmentally responsible manner and confirms compliance with waste management, emissions control, and pollution prevention standards.

Scope

This scope ensures the feed mill remains legally compliant, avoids penalties, and sustains uninterrupted operations.

Responsibility

Role

Responsibility

Production Manager

Ensure to renew before expires.

Feed Mill Admin Officer

To inform the relevant parties before the expiration of the license validity period

Coordinating Manager – Feed Operations

Lead the renewal process, documentation, and timely submission to respective authority and follow-up the process until the renewal license is obtained.

Managing Director (MD)

Provide approvals, resources, and sign‑offs

Accounts / Finance Department

Verify invoices and process payments

Definitions

Renewal of Acreage Tax

  • Local government Acreage Tax regulations
  • Annual renewal before license expiry
  • Internal feed mill procurement SOPs

Renewal of Business Tax Certificate

  • Official authorization issued by the local authority permitting business operations upon payment of applicable taxes.
  • Urban council responsible for issuing business tax certificates.
  • Annual renewal before license expiry.

Renewal of DAPH License

  • Official authorization issued by the Department of Animal Production & Health for operating a feed mill.
  • Good Manufacturing Practices required for feed safety (GMP)
  • Certificate of Analysis for raw materials and finished feed (COA)
  • Annual renewal before license expiry

Renewal of Environment Protection License (EPL)

  • EPL (Environment Protection License): A license issued by the Central Environmental Authority permitting operations with specified environmental safeguards.
  • CEA: Central Environmental Authority, the regulatory body overseeing environmental compliance in Sri Lanka.
  • Renewal Application: Official submission to extend the validity of the EPL before expiry.

Required Records & References

Renewal of Acreage Tax

  • Previous acreage tax receipts
  • Land deeds or survey reports
  • Assessment notices
  • Tax levied based on land area
  • Land valuation or measurement used for taxing

Renewal of Business Tax Certificate

  • Previous acreage tax receipts
  • Land deeds or survey reports
  • Assessment notices
  • Tax levied based on land area
  • Land valuation or measurement used for taxing

Renewal of DAPH License

  • DAPH Licence (current and previous)
  • Renewal application and correspondence
  • Corrective action reports
  • Complete Annex I – Application for Renewal of Licence
  • Prepare Annex II – Turnover Return (sales/production volumes)
  • Compile Annex III – Raw Material Usage (local and imported inputs)
  • Submit Annex IV – Animal Feed Production Data (types, quantities)

Renewal of Environment Protection License (EPL)

  • Copy of current EPL.
  • Environmental monitoring reports (air, water, noise, waste management).
  • Production records (monthly/annual).
  • Waste disposal records and agreements with authorized service providers.
  • Payment receipts for EPL fees.
  • Relevant environmental regulations and guidelines issued by CEA.

Safety & Compliance Requirements

Renewal of Acreage Tax

  • Timely submission to respective authority

Renewal of Business Tax Certificate

  • Ensure accuracy of financial records to avoid penalties
  • Maintain transparency in tax payments.
  • Train staff on compliance requirements.
  • Avoid delays to prevent fines or suspension of business operations.

Renewal of DAPH License

  • Purchase drugs and vitamins and row materials ONLY from DAPH-approved or GMP-certified suppliers.
  • To apply following steps for safety:
  • Fire extinguishers serviced and accessible
  • Emergency exits clear and marked
  • First aid kits stocked
  • PPE available and used by staff
  • Ensure proper storage conditions: cool, dry, and pest-free.
  • Follow MSDS guidelines for hazardous additives.
  • Maintain batch traceability for all additives entering feed production.

Renewal of Environment Protection License (EPL)

  • Ensure all environmental monitoring is conducted by accredited laboratories.
  • Maintain compliance with emission standards, waste disposal regulations, and noise limits.
  • Adhere to occupational health and safety requirements during inspections.
  • Immediate reporting of any environmental incidents to CEA.

Non-Conformance Handling

Renewal of Acreage Tax

  • Conduct internal audits before submission.
  • Keep backup copies of all submitted documents.
  • If renewal is delayed, document reasons, notify MD, and apply for late renewal with penalty fees.
  • Incorrect acreage reported → Re-assessment and correction.

Renewal of Business Tax Certificate

  • Identify missed deadlines or incorrect submissions.
  • If renewal is delayed, document reasons, notify MD, and apply for late renewal with penalty fees.

Renewal of DAPH License

  • Monitor for counterfeit or substandard raw materials.
  • Conduct internal audits before submission.
  • Keep backup copies of all submitted documents.
  • If renewal is delayed, document reasons, notify MD, and apply for late renewal with penalty fees
  • Renewal of Environment Protection License (EPL)

Renewal of Environment Protection License (EPL)

If renewal is delayed or rejected:

  • Notify Plant Manager and Environmental Officer immediately.
  • Implement corrective actions as per CEA recommendations.
  • Record non-conformance in Environmental Management System (EMS).
  • Conduct root cause analysis and preventive measures to avoid recurrence.
