7.1 Preparing a Monthly Live Bird Catching Forcast

1. Purpose

The purpose of this SOP is to establish a systematic procedure for preparing the Monthly Birds Catching Forecast to ensure proper coordination among the BuyBack Farms, Commercial Farms, Production Planning, Processing Plant, and Sales Operations. This process aims to ensure the availability of required live birds for processing and market demand while minimizing shortages, excess birds, operational disruptions, and financial losses.

2. Scope

This applies to:

  • BuyBack farms
  • Company-owned/commercial farms
  • Birds allocation for processing plant
  • Live bird sales planning
  • Catching schedules and transportation planning

3. Responsibility

Role

Responsibility

Sales & Operations Manager

Review and approve monthly forecast

Live Bird Sales Admin Assistant (File maintenance and paperwork)

Collect Data And Prepare forecast

BuyBack Extension Manager

Provide placement and expected harvest information

Farm Manager

Provide company farm bird availability details

Production Manager

Provide production Requirement

Live Transport Officer

Arrange vehicle Requirement based on Live bird catching forecast

4. Definitions

Term

Definitions

Bird Catching Forecast

A monthly projection indicating expected bird harvesting quantities, dates, farm allocations, and destination Requirement.

BuyBack Farms

Contract farms operated externally under company supervision

Commercial Farms

Company-owned poultry farms

Harvest Age

Standard age at which birds are suitable for processing or sale.

5. Required Records & References

  • Chick Placement Report (Buyback Farm)
  • Daily Performance Sheet
  • Processing Requirements Plan
  • Monthly Catching Forecast Schedule

6. Procedure

1.1 Obtain BuyBack Farm Placement Information

The Live Sales Admin Assistant (File maintenance and paperwork) obtain monthly chick placement reports from the BuyBack/Extension Department, including : Annex LSD007/FMT/2026/041 Monthly Chicks Input & Withdrawal Plan (BuyBack)

1.1.2 Obtain Company Farm Placement Information

The Live Sales Admin Assistant (File maintenance and paperwork) collect bird placement and expected harvesting information from commercial farms, including: Annex LSD007/FMT/2026/002 Daily Performance Sheet (Own Farm)

1.2 Analyze Harvesting Availability

The Live Sales Admin Assistant (File maintenance and paperwork) analyze: LSD007/FMT/2026/006 Monthly Chicks Input & Withdrawal Plan Sheet (BuyBack), LSD007/FMT/2026/007 Monthly Chicks Input & Withdrawal Forecast (Own Farm) And expected bird availability estimated for each week of the month.

1.3 Coordinate with the Production Department

The Sales & Operations Manager coordinate with the Production Department to determine:

  • Daily processing capacity
  • Production Requirement
  • Market demand expectations
  • Special seasonal Requirement
  • Product mix demand

Adjustments be made to align bird availability with processing Requirement.

1.4 Prepare Monthly Catching Schedule

  • Based on available data, a Monthly Bird Catching Forecast Schedule prepared By Live Sales Admin Assistant (File maintenance and paperwork) including:- Annex LSD007/FMT/2026/008 Monthly Chicks Input & Withdrawal Forecast Schedule

The schedule prepared considering:

  • Farm location
  • Vehicle optimization
  • Labor availability
  • Plant intake capacity
  • Weather conditions
  • Biosecurity concerns

1.5 Internal Review and Verification

The draft forecast reviewed By Live Sales admin Assistant (Operations)

Any discrepancies or operational constraints be corrected.

1.6 Approval

Prepared By - Live Sales Admin Assistant (File maintenance and paperwork)

Reviewed By - Live Sales admin Assistant (Operations)

Approved By - Sales & Operations Manager

7. Safety & Compliance Requirement

The following safety and compliance Requirement strictly followed during the preparation and implementation of the Monthly Bird Catching Forecast:

1 Biosecurity Compliance

  • Farm biosecurity status be considered before including farms in the forecast.
  • Farms under disease surveillance, quarantine, or veterinary restrictions not be scheduled without veterinary clearance.
  • Movement planning minimize cross-contamination risks between farms.

2 Animal Welfare Compliance

  • Forecasted catching schedules avoid excessive bird holding periods.
  • Bird harvesting age and weight comply with approved welfare and operational standards.
  • Overcrowding and delayed catching that may create bird stress be avoided.

3 Transportation Compliance

  • Vehicle availability and transport capacity be verified before finalizing the forecast.
  • Vehicle planning ensure adequate ventilation and safe transport conditions.

4 Operational Safety

  • Catching schedules consider labor safety, weather conditions, and operational risks.
  • Night catching schedules include proper lighting and supervision Requirement.

5 Regulatory & Documentation Compliance

The following documents be maintained and made available for audit purposes:

  • LSD007/FMT/2026/006 Monthly Chicks Input & Withdrawal Plan Sheet (BuyBack)
  • LSD007/FMT/2026/002 Daily Performance Sheet (Own Farm)
  • LSD007/FMT/2026/008 Monthly Chicks Input & Withdrawal Forecast Schedule
  • LSD007/FMT/2026/005 Processing Plant Weekly Requierment request Sheet
  • Veterinary notifications (if applicable)

7. Non-Conformance Handling

Incorrect bird forecast - Review placement data and Deploying Corrective actions assigned - Live Sales Admin Assistant (File maintenance and paperwork)

Farm unavailable for planned catching - Reallocate birds from alternative farms - Sales & Operations Manager / Live Sales admin Assistant (Operations)

Major quantity shortage - Major quantity shortage - Sales & Operations Manager / Live Sales admin Assistant (Operations) / Live Sales Admin Assistant (File maintenance and paperwork)

Bird weight deviation from target - Revise harvesting schedule - Farm Management Team

Disease outbreak affecting planned farms - Suspend farm allocation and obtain veterinary clearance - Veterinary Officer

Labour / Vehicle Shortage - Arrange backup transport and labours - Live Sales admin Assistant (Operations) / Live Transport Officer

7.2 Coordination with BuyBack Department & Company Farms for Live Bird Supply

  1. Purpose

To establish a structured process for coordinating with BuyBack Farms and Company-Owned Farms to ensure the timely availability of live birds according to production Requirement, market demand, and monthly bird catching forecasts.

  1. Scope

This Applies the period from monthly forecast finalization to actual catching confirmation.

  • BuyBack farms
  • Company-owned/commercial farms
  • Farm bird availability planning
  • Harvest readiness monitoring
  • Farm confirmation for bird collection
  • Supply adjustments due to operational changes
  1. Responsibility

Role

Responsibility

Sales & Operations Manager

Review supply readiness and resolve shortages

Live Sales admin Assistant (Operations)

Coordinate farm-wise bird supply.

BuyBack Extension Manager

Communicate BuyBack farm availability

Farm Managers

Confirm company farm bird readiness

Veterinary / Technical Team

Confirm bird health and harvest suitability

Transport Coordinator

Arrange collection logistics

  1. Definitions

Term

Definitions

Bird Supply Coordination

The process of confirming bird readiness and availability from farms according to operational demand

Harvest Readiness

The condition where birds have achieved the required body weight, health condition, and suitable age for catching.

Farm Confirmation

Official communication confirming the availability of birds for harvesting

  1. Required Records & References
  • Monthly Catching Forecast
  • BuyBack Farm Availability Report
  • Weekly Bird Supply Schedule
  1. Procedure

2.1 Prepare the Approved Monthly Catching Forecast

The Sales & Operations Manager / Live Sales admin Assistant (Operations) obtain the approved:Annex LSD007/FMT/2026/008 Monthly Bird Catching Forecast Schedule and identify:

  • Weekly harvesting Requirement
  • Farm allocations
  • Expected bird quantities
  • Required harvesting dates

The forecast used as the primary planning document

2.2 Coordinate with the BuyBack Department

The Sales & Operations Manager / Live Sales admin Assistant (Operations) coordinate with the BuyBack Extension Manager to obtain:

  • Farm-wise bird status
  • Mortality information
  • Growth performance
  • Expected body weight
  • Farm readiness for harvesting

The BuyBack Department confirm whether birds are available according to planned dates.

Any expected shortages or delays immediately reported.

2.3 Coordinate with Company-Owned Farms

The Sales & Operations Manager / Live Sales admin Assistant (Operations) communicate with Commercial Farm Managers to obtain:

  • Available bird counts
  • Batch maturity status
  • Mortality adjustments
  • Average bird weight
  • Farm operational readiness
  • Farm managers confirm whether birds can be released according to the: LSD007/FMT/2026/002 Daily Performance Sheet (Own Farm)

2.4 Confirm Bird Health & processing Suitability

The Sales & Operations Manager / Live Sales admin Assistant (Operations) coordinate with:

  • Technical farm supervisors
  • Farm quality teams

To confirm:

  • Disease-free status
  • Suitable body condition
  • Biosecurity compliance
  • Harvest suitability

Birds under disease surveillance or quarantine not be approved for catching without veterinary clearance.

2.5 Identify Supply Gaps

The Live Sales admin Assistant (Operations) compare:

Forecast Requirements vs Actual Farm Availability

If shortages are identified:

  • Reallocation between farms be evaluated.
  • Additional Buyback / Commercial farms may be arranged.
  • Harvest dates may be adjusted.
  • Processing plan adjustments may be recommended.

Escalation made to the Sales & Operations Manager where required.

2.6 Daily Review and Adjustments

The Sales & Operations Manager / Live Sales admin Assistant (Operations) Daily operational reviews conducted to identify:

  • Farm readiness changes
  • Weight deviations
  • Mortality changes
  • Weather-related issues
  • Vehicle availability constraints

Necessary adjustments communicated immediately.

  1. Safety & Compliance Requirement

1 Biosecurity Compliance

  • Farm biosecurity status verified before confirming harvesting schedules.
  • Farms under disease surveillance, quarantine, or veterinary restrictions not be approved for harvesting without veterinary clearance.
  • Cross-farm contamination risks be minimized during planning and movement coordination.

2 Animal Welfare Compliance

  • Birds only be scheduled for harvesting at approved maturity levels and acceptable health conditions.
  • Harvest schedules minimize bird stress caused by delayed collection or excessive holding periods.
  • Farm overcrowding due to delayed harvesting avoided.

3 Farm Readiness Compliance

Before confirming supply, farms verified for:

  • Bird readiness for harvest
  • Required average body weight achievement
  • Acceptable mortality level
  • Adequate catching access
  • Biosecurity compliance

Non-ready farms not be confirmed for catching.

  1. Non-Conformance Handling

Farm not ready for harvesting - Reallocate birds from alternative farms - Sales & Operations Manager / Live Sales admin Assistant (Operations)

Bird quantity shortage - Revise weekly supply plan - Sales & Operations Manager

Weight below target - Delay harvest or reassign allocation - Farm Manager

Disease outbreak at farm - Suspend harvesting and obtain veterinary clearance - Veterinary Officer

Incorrect farm confirmation - Revalidate supply information - Live Sales admin Assistant (Operations)

Last-minute farm cancellation - Activate contingency farm allocation - Sales & Operations Manager / Live Sales admin Assistant (Operations)

Escalation Procedure

Where significant supply disruptions occur:

  1. The non-conformance be documented immediately.
  2. Affected stakeholders be informed.
  3. Alternative supply arrangements be evaluated.
  4. Daily allocation plans be revised if required.
  5. Corrective action be recorded.

Repeated supply coordination failures be escalated to senior management for operational review.

7.3 Receiving Customer Orders for Live Birds & Order Booking Process

01. Purpose

The purpose of this SOP is to establish a standardized process for receiving, validating, recording, confirming, and coordinating customer orders for live birds to ensure timely fulfillment, accurate order processing, efficient bird allocation, and customer satisfaction.

This SOP ensures transparency and control over order booking while minimizing operational errors, supply mismatches, and customer disputes.

02. Scope

This applies to

  • Receiving customer live bird orders
  • Booking orders in the order system/register
  • Customer Requirements verification
  • Order confirmation
  • Quantity allocation
  • Coordination with farm and transport teams
  • Order amendments and cancellations

3. Responsibility

Role

Responsibility

Sales & Operations Manager

Approve major allocation decisions and disputes

Live Sales admin Assistant (Operations)

Receive and process customer orders

Confirm supply availability

Customer / Sales Agent

Submit bird order Requirement

Live Sales Admin Officer

Monitoring customer credit activity

Update data to the ERP System

Accounts Department

Verify customer credit status

Catching Officers

Weighing live birds that are sold at the farm and releasing lorries loaded with live birds if payment has been completed.

4. Definitions

Term

Definitions

Order Booking

The formal process of receiving and registering customer demand for live birds.

Confirmed Order

An approved customer request that has been accepted for supply.

Standing Customer

A customer with regular purchasing history and approved business relationship

Allocation

Assigning bird quantities from available supply to customer orders.

Payment terms

Whether a customer is making a purchase on a cash or Credit

5. Required Records & References

  • Order Book
  • ERP Order Entry System
  • Credit Status Report
  • Monthly Chicks Input & Withdrawal Forecast Schedule

6. Procedure

3.1 Receive Customer Order Request

The Live Sales admin Assistant (Operations) Customer orders received through:

  • Telephone calls
  • Messaging platforms

The Live Sales admin Assistant (Operations) collect the following mandatory details:

  • Customer name
  • Delivery/Pickup location
  • Required quantity

Record in: Annex LSD007/REG/2026/003 Oder Book

3.2 Verify Customer Eligibility

Before confirming the order, the Live Bird Admin Officer verify:

A. Customer Credit Status

For credit customers:

  • Outstanding balances
  • Credit limit
  • Overdue invoices
  • Previous payment behavior

The Finance Department may be consulted where required.

Customers exceeding approved limits require management approval before confirmation.

B. Customer Purchase History

The Live Sales admin Assistant (Operations) review:

  • Historical buying trends
  • Supply commitments
  • Customer reliability
  • Priority allocation category

3.3 Check Bird Availability

The Live Sales admin Assistant (Operations) compare the customer request with:

  • LSD007/FMT/2026/008 Monthly Chicks Input & Withdrawal Forecast Schedule
  • LSD007/FMT/2026/005 Processing Plant Weekly Requierment request Sheet
  • Existing committed orders

Factors to consider:

  • Bird availability
  • Average body weight
  • Harvesting schedule
  • Distance and logistics feasibility

3.4 Allocate Bird Quantity

Bird allocation based on:

  1. Customer priority
  2. Available supply
  3. Commercial profitability
  4. Delivery schedule feasibility
  5. Historical customer relationship
  6. Market demand

If requested quantity exceeds availability:

  • Partial allocation may be approved.
  • Alternative delivery dates may be offered.
  • Substitute weight categories may be proposed.

3.5 Confirm Customer Order and Record Order in Order Book / System

The Live Sales admin Assistant (Operations) informed the customer regarding:

  • Approved quantity
  • Delivery/collection date
  • Estimated bird weight
  • Pickup arrangement
  • Pricing
  • Payment terms

Customer confirmation details Record in: Annex LSD007/REG/2026/003 Oder Book and then prepare a: Annex LSD007/FMT/2026/013 Daily Catching Oder Sheet

Sales & Operation Manager Approve the Oder that receive

3.7 Communicate Order to Relevant Departments

The Live Sales admin Assistant (Operations) Confirmed orders Recorded in: Annex LSD007/FMT/2026/013 Daily Catching Oder Sheet communicate Via Telephone Calls and Whatsapp Messaging platform to:

  • Sales & Operations Manager
  • Finance Manager
  • Extension Manager (Buyback)
  • Live Sales Admin Officer
  • Catching Officers
  • Own Farm Managers
  • Buyback Growers

This communication occur before operational execution.

7. Safety & Compliance Requirement

The following safety and compliance Requirements be maintained during the customer order receiving and order booking process to ensure operational accuracy, financial control, customer satisfaction, and compliance with company policies.

1 Customer Verification Compliance

Before confirming orders, the following be verified:

  • Customer identity
  • Customer registration status
  • Credit approval status
  • Outstanding balances
  • Delivery location details

Unverified customers not be processed without management approval.

2 Financial Compliance

  • Credit customers be verified through the Accounts Department before order confirmation.
  • Orders exceeding approved credit limits require management authorization.
  • Cash customers comply with advance payment or agreed payment terms.
  • Unauthorized discounts or pricing arrangements are prohibited.

3 Operational Compliance

  • Orders only be accepted based on available bird supply and approved forecasts.
  • Overbooking beyond operational capacity is prohibited.
  • Weight Requirements comply with operational feasibility and market standards.
  • Order modifications be properly documented.

4 Documentation Compliance

The following records be maintained:
LSD007/REG/2026/003 Oder Book
Credit approval records

All order-related communication affecting supply be documented.

5 Customer Service Compliance

  • Customer commitments be realistic and achievable.
  • Misleading delivery promises are prohibited.
  • Customer complaints be handled professionally and documented.

6 Data Confidentiality & Authorization

  • Customer information remain confidential.
  • Unauthorized order entry, modification, or cancellation is prohibited.
  • ERP/manual register access be restricted to authorized personnel only.

8. Non-Conformance Handling

Incorrect order entry - Correct order and notify affected departments - Live Sales admin Assistant (Operations)

Customer credit limit exceeded - Obtain approval or suspend order - Accounts Department

Overbooking beyond availability - Revise allocation and inform customer - Live Sales admin Assistant (Operations)

Duplicate order entry - Validate and remove duplication - Live Sales admin Assistant (Operations)

Unauthorized order cancellation - Escalate to management and investigate - Sales & Operations Manager

Customer complaint on wrong order - Immediate corrective action and investigation - Live Sales admin Assistant (Operations)

Escalation Procedure

Where significant order discrepancies occur:

  • The issue documented in: Annex LSD007/REG/2026/003 Oder Book
  • Relevant stakeholders be informed.
  • Customer communication be initiated promptly.
  • Corrective action be implemented.
  • Root cause analysis be conducted for repeated issues.

Repeated order management failures escalated to senior management.

7.4 Live Birds Catching and Operations

01.Purpose

To establish a standardized procedure for planning, coordinating, catching, loading, transporting, and delivering live birds from company-owned farms and contract farms to processing plants or customers while ensuring bird welfare, product quality, employee safety, and regulatory compliance.

2. Scope

This SOP applies to all personnel involved in:

  • Live Bird Sales Department
  • Farm Management
  • Catching Crews
  • Transport Teams
  • Quality Assurance Personnel
  • Processing Plant Receiving Personnel
  • The procedure covers activities from receiving bird requirements to delivery and documentation completion.

3. Responsibility

Role

Responsibility

Sales & Operations Manager

  • Approve daily and weekly bird dispatch plans.
  • Coordinate with farm Managers and Production Manager
  • Monitor achievement of sales and delivery targets.

Live Sales admin Assistant (Operations)

  • Prepare catching schedules.
  • Communicate requirements to farm Managers and Live Transport Officer.
  • Verify dispatch records.

Farm Manager

  • Ensure birds are available as scheduled.
  • Confirm flock health and readiness.
  • Facilitate catching operations.

Live Transport Officer

  • Organize manpower and equipment
  • Arrange suitable vehicles.
  • Verify vehicle cleanliness and biosecurity.
  • Monitor transportation schedules.

Night Supervisor

  • Record mortality and non-conformances
  • Confirming and recording the number of birds loaded from farms and the number of birds brought to the production facility
  • Supervise the unloading and placement of bird crates in accordance with established procedures

Unloading Team

  • Unloading and positioning bird crates in accordance with established procedures
  • Keeping birds at the landing terminal under approved quality control procedures until the production process begins.

Drivers

  • Transport birds safely.
  • Maintain vehicle hygiene.

Report delays or incidents.

Quality Assurance Officer

  • Verify compliance with welfare and quality standards.
  • Record mortality and non-conformances.

Loading Officer

  • Ensure feed withdrawal is conducted according to company requirements.
  • Load birds according to approved crate density and Record Loading start and finish times/ Number of birds loaded/Number of crates

4. Definitions

Term

Definitions

Live Bird Catching

Process of collecting birds from poultry houses for transport

Flock

A group of birds reared together.

Loading Density

Number of birds loaded per crate/module according to weight and welfare requirements.

DOA

Dead on Arrival birds at the processing plant.

Biosecurity

Measures preventing disease transmission between farms and facilities.

Catching Crew

Personnel responsible for catching and loading birds.

5. Required Records & References

Records

  • Daily Catching Sheet
  • Live Bird Unloading Record Book
  • Weigh Bridge Bills
  • BuyBack sales Bills

References

  • QA Work Instruction
  • Occupational Health & Safety Guidelines
  • National Livestock Regulations

6. Procedure

4.1 Receive Bird Requirement

The Sales & Operations Manager/Live Sales admin Assistant (Operations) Obtain daily bird requirement from the processing plant through mail and customer through massaging platforms: Annex LSD007/FMT/2026/013 Daily Catching Oder Sheet

Verify:

  • Quantity required
  • Average live weight
  • Collection date and time
  • Farm source

4.2 Farm Confirmation

1. The Sales & Operations Manager/Live Sales admin Assistant (Operations) Contact the Farm Manager and Confirm:

  • Bird availability
  • Expected average weight
  • Health status
  • Feed withdrawal time

4.3 Preparation for Catching

1 Equipment Required

  • Bird crates
  • Catching curtains
  • Torches or low-intensity lighting
  • PPE
  • Vehicle loading equipment

2 Pre-Catching Activities

  • Farmer / Farm Manager Ensure feed withdrawal is conducted according to company requirements.
  • Check water availability until catching begins.
  • Clean and disinfect crates and vehicles.
  • Brief catching crew on welfare and safety requirements: Annex ICMS/ WI/ ED/ 01

4.4 Live Bird Catching

Catching Crew

1. Reduce light intensity inside poultry houses.

2. Move birds calmly toward catching area.

3. Catch birds carefully to prevent injuries.

4. Hold birds according to approved welfare practices: Annex ICMS/ WI/ ED/ 01

5. Avoid:

  • Throwing birds
  • Rough handling
  • Overcrowding

Welfare Requirements

  • Minimize stress.
  • Prevent wing and leg injuries.
  • Avoid excessive noise.
  • Ensure adequate ventilation.

6. The Sales & Operations Manager/Live Sales Admin Assistant (Operations) should be informed of any problems that arise during operations.

4.5 Loading Operations

1. Catching Officers / Loading Officer Count birds accurately.

2. Load birds according to approved crate density.

3. Ensure:

  • Proper ventilation
  • Stable crate stacking
  • Secure vehicle loading

4. Catching Officers / Loading Officer Record:

  • Number of birds loaded
  • Number of crates
  • Loading start and finish times

4.6 Customer Delivery & Handover

Upon arrival, the Customer Representative and Catching Officer jointly verify:

Bird quantity

Bird condition

Weight compliance

Mortality count

Delivery timing

The customer acknowledge delivery Including: Annex LSD007/REG/2026/043 Weigh Bridge Bills

4.7 Security Gate Clearance

Before dispatch, Security verify:

  • Vehicle authorization
  • Dispatch documents
  • Driver identification
  • Quantity verification
  • Destination confirmation

The Security Officer record:

  • Vehicle number
  • Departure time
  • Driver details
  • Quantity dispatched

Unauthorized dispatches are prohibited.

4.7 Transportation

1. Dispatch vehicle according to schedule: Annex LSD007/REG/2026/004 Plant Oder Book

2. Driver:

  • Follow designated route.
  • Avoid unnecessary stops.
  • Maintain suitable driving speed.
  • Monitor weather conditions.

3. Inform Night Supervisor of expected arrival time.

4.8 Delivery at Processing Plant

.1 Present dispatch documentation : Annex LSD007/REG/2026/043 Weigh Bridge Bills, LSD007/REG/2026/044 Buyback Bills

.2 Processing plant personnel verify:

  • Bird count
  • Average weight
  • Mortality
  • Welfare condition

3. Obtain signed receiving confirmation from night supervisor Annex LSD007/REG/2026/001 Live Bird Unloading Register Book

4.9 Post-Operation Review

1. Compare:

  • Planned birds: Annex LSD007/FMT/2026/013 Daily Catching Oder Sheet
  • Actual birds delivered: Annex LSD007/FMT/2026/011 Daily Live Bird Catching Summery, LSD007/FMT/2026/009 Live Bird Unloading Report,
  • Mortality rate: LSD007/FMT/2026/009 Live Bird Unloading Report, LSD007/FMT/2026/002 Daily Performance Sheet (Own Farm)

2. Analyze deviations.

3. Report issues to management.

4. File all operational records.

7. Safety & Compliance Requirement

1 Personnel Safety

  • Wear required PPE.
  • Use proper lifting techniques.
  • Follow vehicle safety procedures.
  • Report injuries immediately.

2 Animal Welfare

  • Handle birds humanely.
  • Minimize stress and suffering.
  • Follow approved loading densities.
  • Reduce transportation time where possible.

3 Biosecurity

  • Disinfect vehicles before and after use.
  • Restrict unauthorized access.
  • Use farm-specific protective clothing.
  • Follow disease prevention protocols.

4 Environmental Compliance

  • Dispose of waste properly.
  • Prevent contamination of surrounding areas.
  • Manage mortalities according to company policy.

8. Non-Conformance Handling

Farm unavailable for planned allocation - Reallocate birds from alternative farm - Sales and Operation Manager / Live Sales admin Assistant (Operations)

Bird quantity shortage - Reallocate birds from alternative farm - Sales and Operation Manager / Live Sales admin Assistant (Operations)

Weight mismatch from planned target - Reassign farm allocation - Farm Manager

Catching crew unavailable - Arrange replacement team - Live Sales admin Assistant (Operations) / Live Bird Transport Officer

Vehicle shortage or breakdown - Deploy backup vehicle - Live Sales admin Assistant (Operations) / Live Bird Transport Officer

Customer order exceeding available supply - Partial allocation or rescheduling - Sales and Operation Manager / Live Sales admin Assistant (Operations)

Incorrect dispatch schedule - Immediate schedule correction - Sales and Operation Manager / Live Sales admin Assistant (Operations)

Excessive mortality - Inform Sales & Operations Manager immediately – Night Supervisor

Weather-related operational disruption - Revise dispatch timing - Sales and Operation Manager / Live Sales admin Assistant (Operations)

2. Scope

This applies to all live bird sales transactions, bird dispatches, and live weight records maintained by the Live Bird Sales & Operations Department, including birds sourced from:

  • Company-owned farms
  • Buyback/contract grower farms
  • External suppliers (where applicable)

3. Responsibility

Role

Responsibility

Sales & Operations Manager

  • Overall supervision and approval of recordsEnsure
  • compliance with company procedures.

Live Sales admin Assistant (Operations)

Live Sales admin Officer

Farm Manager / Farm Supervisor

Finance Manager
